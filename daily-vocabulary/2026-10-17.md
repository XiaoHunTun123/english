# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-17

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accessible** /əkˈsesəbəl/  
   **English definition:** easy to reach, use, or understand; available to people with different needs  
   **中文释义：** 易于到达或使用的；易懂的；无障碍的  
   **Example:** The revised portal is accessible on mobile devices and screen readers.  
   **中文：** 修改后的门户网站可在移动设备和屏幕阅读器上使用。

2. **backlog** /ˈbækˌlɔːɡ/  
   **English definition:** a quantity of work or tasks that has accumulated and has not yet been completed  
   **中文释义：** 积压的工作；待办事项  
   **Example:** The support team cleared its backlog by adding a weekend shift.  
   **中文：** 支持团队增加了周末班次，清理了积压的工作。

3. **breach** /briːtʃ/  
   **English definition:** an act of breaking a law, agreement, or security measure; to break one  
   **中文释义：** 违反；违约；漏洞；突破  
   **Example:** The company reported a data breach to affected customers.  
   **中文：** 公司向受影响的客户通报了一起数据泄露事件。

4. **calibrate** /ˈkælɪbreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to adjust an instrument or process so that it measures or operates accurately  
   **中文释义：** 校准；调整（仪器或流程）  
   **Example:** Technicians calibrate the sensors before each field test.  
   **中文：** 技术人员在每次现场测试前都会校准传感器。

5. **contingency** /kənˈtɪndʒənsi/  
   **English definition:** a possible future event or problem that requires preparation  
   **中文释义：** 可能发生的情况；意外事件；应急准备  
   **Example:** The budget includes a contingency for unexpected shipping costs.  
   **中文：** 预算中预留了一笔资金，以应对意外的运输费用。

6. **correlate** /ˈkɔːrəleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to have a connection or relationship with something else  
   **中文释义：** 相互关联；有相关性  
   **Example:** The study found that sleep duration correlates with test performance.  
   **中文：** 研究发现，睡眠时长与考试表现存在相关性。

7. **delegate** /ˈdeləɡeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to give a task or responsibility to another person  
   **中文释义：** 委派；授权；把任务交给他人  
   **Example:** A good manager delegates routine tasks so the team can focus on complex work.  
   **中文：** 好的管理者会委派日常任务，让团队专注于复杂工作。

8. **deploy** /dɪˈplɔɪ/  
   **English definition:** to put people, equipment, or resources into position or use  
   **中文释义：** 部署；调动；投入使用  
   **Example:** The agency deployed additional staff to process applications.  
   **中文：** 该机构调派了更多员工来处理申请。

9. **dissent** /dɪˈsent/  
   **English definition:** to hold or express an opinion that differs from an official or majority view  
   **中文释义：** 持异议；表示反对；异议  
   **Example:** One member dissented from the committee’s recommendation.  
   **中文：** 一名成员对委员会的建议持不同意见。

10. **distinguish** /dɪˈstɪŋɡwɪʃ/  
   **English definition:** to recognize or show how someone or something differs from others  
   **中文释义：** 区分；辨别；使有别于  
   **Example:** The new label helps shoppers distinguish decaf from regular coffee.  
   **中文：** 新标签有助于顾客区分无咖啡因咖啡和普通咖啡。

11. **divert** /daɪˈvɜːrt/  
   **English definition:** to change the direction of something or use it for a different purpose  
   **中文释义：** 转向；改道；转移（用途或注意力）  
   **Example:** Road crews diverted traffic while they repaired the bridge.  
   **中文：** 道路施工人员维修桥梁期间对交通进行了改道。

12. **embody** /ɪmˈbɑːdi/  
   **English definition:** to represent or express an idea or quality in a clear, concrete way  
   **中文释义：** 体现；具体表现；包含（某种特质）  
   **Example:** The design embodies the company’s commitment to repairability.  
   **中文：** 这一设计体现了公司对可维修性的重视。

13. **exert** /ɪɡˈzɜːrt/  
   **English definition:** to use or apply effort, influence, or pressure  
   **中文释义：** 运用；施加；尽力  
   **Example:** The small team exerted considerable effort to meet the deadline.  
   **中文：** 这个小团队付出了相当大的努力来赶上截止日期。

14. **forecast** /ˈfɔːrkæst/  
   **English definition:** to predict a future event or condition based on available information  
   **中文释义：** 预测；预报  
   **Example:** Analysts forecast slower growth in the next quarter.  
   **中文：** 分析师预测下个季度的增长速度会放缓。

15. **fulfill** /fʊlˈfɪl/  
   **English definition:** to do what was promised or required, or to satisfy a condition or need  
   **中文释义：** 履行；完成；满足（要求或条件）  
   **Example:** The supplier fulfilled the order two days ahead of schedule.  
   **中文：** 供应商提前两天完成了订单。

16. **impair** /ɪmˈper/  
   **English definition:** to weaken or damage someone’s ability or something’s function  
   **中文释义：** 损害；削弱；妨碍正常功能  
   **Example:** Poor lighting can impair a driver’s ability to see road signs.  
   **中文：** 光线不足会影响驾驶员看清道路标志的能力。

17. **mobilize** /ˈmoʊbəlaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to organize or prepare people and resources for action  
   **中文释义：** 动员；调集；组织起来采取行动  
   **Example:** The clinic mobilized extra staff during the heat wave.  
   **中文：** 热浪期间，诊所调集了额外人员。

18. **optimize** /ˈɑːptəmaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to make something as effective or useful as possible  
   **中文释义：** 优化；使达到最佳状态  
   **Example:** We adjusted the route to optimize fuel use.  
   **中文：** 我们调整了路线，以优化燃油使用。

19. **overhaul** /ˌoʊvərˈhɔːl/  
   **English definition:** to examine and repair something thoroughly, or make major improvements to it  
   **中文释义：** 全面检修；彻底改革；大幅改进  
   **Example:** The city plans to overhaul its aging water network.  
   **中文：** 市政府计划全面改造老旧的供水网络。

20. **overstate** /ˌoʊvərˈsteɪt/  
   **English definition:** to describe something as greater, more serious, or more important than it really is  
   **中文释义：** 夸大；言过其实  
   **Example:** The press release overstates the impact of the pilot program.  
   **中文：** 这份新闻稿夸大了试点项目的影响。

21. **pledge** /pledʒ/  
   **English definition:** to make a serious or formal promise to do something  
   **中文释义：** 承诺；保证；誓言  
   **Example:** The supplier pledged to replace the faulty components.  
   **中文：** 供应商承诺更换有缺陷的零部件。

22. **prolong** /prəˈlɔːŋ/  
   **English definition:** to make something continue for a longer time than usual or necessary  
   **中文释义：** 延长；拖长  
   **Example:** The dispute prolonged the approval process.  
   **中文：** 这场争议拖长了审批流程。

23. **recur** /rɪˈkɜːr/  
   **English definition:** to happen again, often at intervals or after seeming to stop  
   **中文释义：** 再次发生；复发；反复出现  
   **Example:** The warning light recurred despite the software update.  
   **中文：** 尽管更新了软件，警示灯还是再次亮起。

24. **sparse** /spɑːrs/  
   **English definition:** thinly spread or present in small numbers; not dense or plentiful  
   **中文释义：** 稀疏的；稀少的；零星的  
   **Example:** Internet coverage is sparse in the mountain villages.  
   **中文：** 山区村庄的网络覆盖较为稀疏。

25. **thrive** /θraɪv/  
   **English definition:** to grow, develop, or succeed well  
   **中文释义：** 茁壮成长；兴旺；蓬勃发展  
   **Example:** Small businesses thrive when reliable transport connects them to customers.  
   **中文：** 可靠的交通将小企业与客户连接起来时，它们更容易蓬勃发展。
