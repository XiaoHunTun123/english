# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-11

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **apathy** /ˈæpəθi/ — a lack of interest, enthusiasm, or concern；缺乏兴趣、热情或关心；冷漠
   - **Example:** Public apathy made it difficult to build support for the reform.
   - **中文：** 公众的冷漠使这项改革难以获得支持。

2. **benevolent** /bəˈnevələnt/ — kind and well-meaning；善良且出于善意的；仁慈的
   - **Example:** A benevolent donor funded the community health center.
   - **中文：** 一位仁慈的捐赠者资助了社区健康中心。

3. **bombastic** /bɑːmˈbæstɪk/ — using grand words to sound important；使用夸张华丽的词语来显得重要的；浮夸的
   - **Example:** The candidate’s bombastic speech contained few practical proposals.
   - **中文：** 候选人的浮夸演讲几乎没有包含实际可行的提议。

4. **caveat** /ˈkæviæt/ — a warning or condition that limits an agreement；限制某项协议的警告或条件；注意事项
   - **Example:** The plan is promising, with one important caveat: it depends on stable funding.
   - **中文：** 这项计划很有希望，但有一个重要的注意事项：它依赖稳定的资金。

5. **concurrent** /kənˈkɜːrənt/ — happening or existing at the same time；同时发生或存在的；并行的
   - **Example:** The company launched two concurrent investigations into the incident.
   - **中文：** 公司同时对这起事件展开了两项调查。

6. **desolate** /ˈdesələt/ — empty, lonely, and without signs of life；空旷、孤寂且没有生命迹象的；荒凉的
   - **Example:** The abandoned factory stood in a desolate industrial district.
   - **中文：** 这座废弃工厂坐落在一片荒凉的工业区里。

7. **dilapidated** /dɪˈlæpɪdeɪtɪd/ — in poor condition because of age or neglect；因老旧或疏于维护而状况很差的；破败的
   - **Example:** They renovated a dilapidated house near the old market.
   - **中文：** 他们翻修了旧市场附近的一栋破败房屋。

8. **duplicity** /duːˈplɪsəti/ — dishonest behavior involving deceit or pretending；涉及欺骗或伪装的不诚实行为；两面派；欺诈
   - **Example:** The investigation revealed a pattern of duplicity within the organization.
   - **中文：** 调查揭示了该组织内部一贯的欺诈行为。

9. **epitome** /ɪˈpɪtəmi/ — a perfect example of a particular quality or type；某种品质或类型的完美典范；缩影；典范
   - **Example:** Her calm response was the epitome of professionalism.
   - **中文：** 她镇定的回应是专业精神的典范。

10. **equitable** /ˈekwɪtəbəl/ — fair and impartial；公平且不偏不倚的；公正的
    - **Example:** The committee is seeking an equitable distribution of resources.
    - **中文：** 委员会正在寻求公平的资源分配方式。

11. **erratic** /ɪˈrætɪk/ — unpredictable or inconsistent；不可预测或不稳定的；反复无常的
    - **Example:** Erratic internet access disrupted the online training session.
    - **中文：** 不稳定的网络连接打断了线上培训。

12. **exasperate** /ɪɡˈzæspəreɪt/ — make someone extremely annoyed；使某人极其恼怒；激怒
    - **Example:** Repeated software failures exasperated the support team.
    - **中文：** 软件反复出故障让支持团队极其恼怒。

13. **fervor** /ˈfɜːrvər/ — intense enthusiasm or passion；强烈的热情或激情；热忱
    - **Example:** Volunteers worked with great fervor during the relief campaign.
    - **中文：** 志愿者们在救援活动中满怀热情地工作。

14. **hypocrisy** /hɪˈpɑːkrəsi/ — pretending to have moral standards that one does not follow；假装拥有自己并不遵守的道德标准；虚伪
    - **Example:** Voters were quick to criticize the official’s hypocrisy.
    - **中文：** 选民很快就批评了那名官员的虚伪。

15. **impeccable** /ɪmˈpekəbəl/ — without any faults or mistakes；没有任何缺点或错误的；无可挑剔的
    - **Example:** The chef is known for impeccable attention to detail.
    - **中文：** 这位厨师以无可挑剔的细节把控而闻名。

16. **impertinent** /ɪmˈpɜːrtɪnənt/ — rude or not relevant to the matter；无礼或与所讨论之事无关的；无礼的；不相干的
    - **Example:** The reporter’s impertinent question made the interview uncomfortable.
    - **中文：** 记者无礼的问题让采访气氛变得尴尬。

17. **insolent** /ˈɪnsələnt/ — rude and disrespectful toward someone；对某人无礼且不尊重的；傲慢无礼的
    - **Example:** The manager warned the employee about his insolent behavior.
    - **中文：** 经理警告那名员工注意自己的傲慢无礼行为。

18. **integrity** /ɪnˈteɡrəti/ — honesty and strong moral principles；诚实以及坚定的道德原则；正直
    - **Example:** The auditor was trusted because of her professional integrity.
    - **中文：** 这位审计员因其职业操守而受到信任。

19. **intermittent** /ˌɪntərˈmɪtənt/ — occurring at irregular intervals rather than continuously；以不规则间隔发生而非持续发生的；间歇的
    - **Example:** Intermittent rain delayed the construction work.
    - **中文：** 间歇性的降雨推迟了施工。

20. **irate** /aɪˈreɪt/ — extremely angry；极其生气的；愤怒的
    - **Example:** An irate customer demanded to speak with the supervisor.
    - **中文：** 一位愤怒的顾客要求与主管谈话。

21. **jubilant** /ˈdʒuːbɪlənt/ — feeling or expressing great joy；感到或表现出极大喜悦的；欢欣的
    - **Example:** Jubilant supporters celebrated the team’s unexpected victory.
    - **中文：** 欢欣鼓舞的支持者庆祝了球队出人意料的胜利。

22. **placate** /ˈpleɪkeɪt/ — make someone less angry by calming or satisfying them；通过安抚或满足某人使其不再生气；安抚
    - **Example:** The airline offered compensation to placate passengers affected by the delay.
    - **中文：** 航空公司提供赔偿，以安抚受延误影响的乘客。

23. **platitude** /ˈplætɪtuːd/ — a common remark that has been used so often it lacks meaning；因过度使用而缺乏意义的常见说法；陈词滥调
    - **Example:** The speech offered platitudes instead of a concrete plan.
    - **中文：** 这篇演讲只说了些陈词滥调，没有提出具体计划。

24. **precipitous** /prɪˈsɪpɪtəs/ — very steep or happening suddenly and unexpectedly；非常陡峭或突然且出人意料发生的；陡峭的；骤然的
    - **Example:** The company suffered a precipitous decline in revenue.
    - **中文：** 公司的收入骤然下降。

25. **repudiate** /rɪˈpjuːdieɪt/ — reject or refuse to accept something；拒绝或否认接受某事；拒绝；否认
    - **Example:** The minister repudiated the allegation as completely unfounded.
    - **中文：** 部长否认了这项指控，称其完全没有根据。
