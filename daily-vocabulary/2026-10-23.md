# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-23

以下 25 个词汇兼顾实用性与拓展性，适合工作、新闻和正式表达场景。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accountability** /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti/  
   **English definition:** the responsibility to explain your actions or results and accept the consequences  
   **中文释义：** 责任；问责；承担后果的义务  
   **Example:** Clear roles improve accountability when a project encounters problems.  
   **中文：** 项目遇到问题时，明确的职责分工有助于落实问责。

2. **acknowledgment** /əkˈnɑːlɪdʒmənt/  
   **English definition:** an act of recognizing that something exists, is true, or has been received; also spelled “acknowledgement” in British English  
   **中文释义：** 承认；确认收到；致谢  
   **Example:** Please send an acknowledgment when you receive the revised contract.  
   **中文：** 收到修订后的合同后，请回复确认。

3. **administer** /ədˈmɪnɪstər/  
   **English definition:** to manage or organize an institution or process; to give a medicine or treatment  
   **中文释义：** 管理；施行；给予（药物或治疗）  
   **Example:** A trained nurse will administer the treatment and record the patient's response.  
   **中文：** 受过培训的护士将实施治疗并记录患者的反应。

4. **ally** /ˈælaɪ/  
   **English definition:** a person or group that cooperates with another for a shared purpose  
   **中文释义：** 盟友；支持者；合作伙伴  
   **Example:** Local organizations became valuable allies in the public health campaign.  
   **中文：** 当地组织成为这场公共卫生宣传活动的重要伙伴。

5. **ambiguity** /ˌæmbɪˈɡjuːəti/  
   **English definition:** the quality of having more than one possible meaning or interpretation  
   **中文释义：** 模糊；歧义；不明确  
   **Example:** The editor removed ambiguity from the instructions before publication.  
   **中文：** 编辑在发布前消除了说明中的歧义。

6. **apparel** /əˈperəl/  
   **English definition:** clothing, especially when considered as a product category or industry  
   **中文释义：** 衣服；服装（尤指商品类别或行业）  
   **Example:** The retailer expanded its online apparel range this season.  
   **中文：** 这家零售商本季扩大了线上服装品类。

7. **appeal** /əˈpiːl/  
   **English definition:** a serious or formal request; the quality of being attractive or interesting  
   **中文释义：** 呼吁；上诉；吸引力  
   **Example:** The decision is final, so the applicant cannot appeal it further.  
   **中文：** 该决定是最终决定，因此申请人不能再提出上诉。

8. **assignment** /əˈsaɪnmənt/  
   **English definition:** a task or piece of work given to someone; the act of allocating something  
   **中文释义：** 任务；作业；分配  
   **Example:** Each analyst received an assignment to review one section of the report.  
   **中文：** 每位分析师都接到一项任务，负责审查报告中的一个部分。

9. **attendance** /əˈtendəns/  
   **English definition:** the act or fact of being present at a place or event  
   **中文释义：** 出席；到场；出勤  
   **Example:** Attendance at the safety briefing is required for all new staff.  
   **中文：** 所有新员工都必须参加安全说明会。

10. **baseline** /ˈbeɪslaɪn/  
    **English definition:** a starting level used as a point of comparison  
    **中文释义：** 基准；基线；起始水平  
    **Example:** We recorded baseline energy use before installing the new equipment.  
    **中文：** 安装新设备前，我们记录了能源使用的基准数据。

11. **bureaucratic** /ˌbjʊrəˈkrætɪk/  
    **English definition:** involving complicated official rules, procedures, or layers of administration  
    **中文释义：** 官僚的；手续繁琐的  
    **Example:** Small businesses said the application process was too bureaucratic.  
    **中文：** 小企业表示，申请流程过于繁琐。

12. **capability** /ˌkeɪpəˈbɪləti/  
    **English definition:** the ability or power to do something  
    **中文释义：** 能力；性能；本领  
    **Example:** The software has the capability to process several formats at once.  
    **中文：** 这款软件具备同时处理多种格式的能力。

13. **cautious** /ˈkɔːʃəs/  
    **English definition:** careful to avoid danger, mistakes, or unnecessary risk  
    **中文释义：** 谨慎的；小心的  
    **Example:** Investors remained cautious until the company released its full results.  
    **中文：** 在公司公布完整业绩前，投资者仍持谨慎态度。

14. **centralize** /ˈsentrəlaɪz/  
    **English definition:** to bring control, activity, or decision-making into one central place or organization  
    **中文释义：** 集中；使集中管理  
    **Example:** The agency plans to centralize its records in a secure database.  
    **中文：** 该机构计划将档案集中存放在一个安全数据库中。

15. **characteristic** /ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪk/  
    **English definition:** a quality or feature that is typical of a person, place, or thing  
    **中文释义：** 特征；特点；典型的  
    **Example:** Reliability is a characteristic customers value in essential services.  
    **中文：** 在基础服务中，可靠性是顾客看重的一项特征。

16. **chronological** /ˌkrɑːnəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl/  
    **English definition:** arranged in the order in which events happened  
    **中文释义：** 按时间顺序排列的  
    **Example:** The witness gave a chronological account of the events.  
    **中文：** 目击者按时间顺序叙述了事件经过。

17. **clearance** /ˈklɪrəns/  
    **English definition:** official permission; the removal of objects or obstacles; the amount of open space around something  
    **中文释义：** 许可；清除；间隙  
    **Example:** The equipment cannot be shipped until it receives customs clearance.  
    **中文：** 设备获得海关放行后才能发运。

18. **collaboration** /kəˌlæbəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the act of working together with others on a shared activity or goal  
    **中文释义：** 合作；协作  
    **Example:** The project depends on close collaboration between designers and engineers.  
    **中文：** 这个项目有赖于设计师与工程师的密切协作。

19. **commodity** /kəˈmɑːdəti/  
    **English definition:** a basic product, often a raw material, that can be bought and sold  
    **中文释义：** 商品；（尤指）大宗商品或原材料  
    **Example:** Coffee is a major commodity in several countries' export markets.  
    **中文：** 咖啡是多个国家出口市场中的重要大宗商品。

20. **competence** /ˈkɑːmpɪtəns/  
    **English definition:** the ability and knowledge needed to do something successfully  
    **中文释义：** 能力；胜任力  
    **Example:** The role requires competence in both budgeting and staff management.  
    **中文：** 这个岗位要求具备预算管理和人员管理两方面的能力。

21. **configuration** /kənˌfɪɡjəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the arrangement or setup of parts, equipment, or settings  
    **中文释义：** 配置；布局；结构  
    **Example:** This configuration allows the system to keep running if one server fails.  
    **中文：** 这种配置使系统在一台服务器故障时仍能继续运行。

22. **contradict** /ˌkɑːntrəˈdɪkt/  
    **English definition:** to state the opposite of something; to be inconsistent with a fact or statement  
    **中文释义：** 反驳；与……矛盾  
    **Example:** The latest figures contradict the assumption that demand is falling.  
    **中文：** 最新数据与“需求正在下降”这一假设相矛盾。

23. **correlation** /ˌkɔːrəˈleɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a relationship in which two things change or occur together; this does not by itself prove causation  
    **中文释义：** 相关性；关联（本身不等于因果关系）  
    **Example:** The study found a correlation between sleep duration and concentration.  
    **中文：** 研究发现睡眠时长与专注力之间存在相关性。

24. **diversify** /daɪˈvɜːrsɪfaɪ/  
    **English definition:** to make something more varied, or to develop a wider range of products or activities  
    **中文释义：** 使多样化；拓展经营范围  
    **Example:** The company plans to diversify its suppliers to reduce disruption risks.  
    **中文：** 公司计划拓展供应商来源，以降低供应中断的风险。

25. **expenditure** /ɪkˈspendɪtʃər/  
    **English definition:** the amount of money spent, or the act of spending it  
    **中文释义：** 支出；开支  
    **Example:** The department must report its travel expenditure each quarter.  
    **中文：** 该部门必须每季度报告差旅支出。
