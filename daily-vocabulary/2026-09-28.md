# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-28

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **acuity** /əˈkjuːəti/  
   **English definition:** the ability to think, understand, or notice things quickly and accurately  
   **中文释义：** 敏锐；敏捷；洞察力  
   **Example:** The analyst’s financial acuity helped the company avoid a costly mistake.  
   **中文：** 这位分析师敏锐的金融判断力帮助公司避免了一次代价高昂的错误。

2. **axiomatic** /ˌæksiəˈmætɪk/  
   **English definition:** accepted as obviously true and not needing to be proved  
   **中文释义：** 不言自明的；公理性的  
   **Example:** It is axiomatic that clear communication improves teamwork.  
   **中文：** 清晰沟通能改善团队合作，这是不言自明的道理。

3. **bastion** /ˈbæstiən/  
   **English definition:** a strong supporter or protected center of a particular principle or group  
   **中文释义：** 堡垒；坚固的支持中心  
   **Example:** The library remains a bastion of independent research in the region.  
   **中文：** 这座图书馆仍是该地区独立研究的坚实堡垒。

4. **devious** /ˈdiːviəs/  
   **English definition:** dishonest or indirect in order to achieve a goal  
   **中文释义：** 狡猾的；不正当的；迂回的  
   **Example:** The investigation uncovered a devious attempt to hide the company’s losses.  
   **中文：** 调查发现有人企图用不正当手段掩盖公司的亏损。

5. **encomium** /enˈkoʊmiəm/  
   **English definition:** a speech or piece of writing that strongly praises someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 颂词；高度赞扬  
   **Example:** The award ceremony included an encomium to the scientist’s lifetime of work.  
   **中文：** 颁奖典礼上有一篇颂扬这位科学家毕生工作的致辞。

6. **extirpate** /ˈekstərpeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to destroy or remove something completely  
   **中文释义：** 根除；彻底消灭  
   **Example:** The program aims to extirpate corruption from the procurement process.  
   **中文：** 该项目旨在从采购流程中根除腐败。

7. **fractal** /ˈfræktəl/  
   **English definition:** a complex shape or pattern whose parts resemble the whole at different scales  
   **中文释义：** 分形的；分形图形  
   **Example:** The researchers used a fractal model to describe the branching pattern of the river system.  
   **中文：** 研究人员用分形模型描述河流系统的分支模式。

8. **genial** /ˈdʒiːniəl/  
   **English definition:** friendly, cheerful, and pleasant  
   **中文释义：** 和蔼的；亲切愉快的  
   **Example:** The genial host made newcomers feel welcome immediately.  
   **中文：** 亲切的主人让新来的人立刻感到受欢迎。

9. **impugn** /ɪmˈpjuːn/  
   **English definition:** to challenge the truth, honesty, or reputation of someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 质疑；指责；怀疑……的真实性  
   **Example:** The lawyer tried to impugn the reliability of the witness’s account.  
   **中文：** 律师试图质疑证词的可靠性。

10. **ingratiate** /ɪnˈɡreɪʃieɪt/  
    **English definition:** to make someone like you by deliberately trying to please them  
    **中文释义：** 讨好；奉承以取得好感  
    **Example:** He tried to ingratiate himself with the new supervisor by volunteering for extra work.  
    **中文：** 他主动承担额外工作，试图讨好新主管。

11. **iniquity** /ɪˈnɪkwəti/  
    **English definition:** very unfair or wicked behavior  
    **中文释义：** 不公；邪恶行为  
    **Example:** The novel exposes the social iniquity created by unequal access to education.  
    **中文：** 这部小说揭露了教育机会不平等造成的社会不公。

12. **intercede** /ˌɪntərˈsiːd/  
    **English definition:** to intervene on behalf of someone in difficulty or conflict  
    **中文释义：** 调解；为某人说情  
    **Example:** A senior colleague interceded to prevent the dispute from escalating.  
    **中文：** 一位资深同事出面调解，防止争端升级。

13. **litany** /ˈlɪtəni/  
    **English definition:** a long list or series, especially of complaints or criticisms  
    **中文释义：** 一长串；冗长的列举  
    **Example:** The report contains a litany of failures in the public transport system.  
    **中文：** 报告列举了公共交通系统的一长串问题。

14. **modicum** /ˈmɑːdɪkəm/  
    **English definition:** a small amount of something, especially something desirable or necessary  
    **中文释义：** 少量；一点点  
    **Example:** The plan has not shown even a modicum of financial discipline.  
    **中文：** 这项计划甚至没有表现出一丁点财务纪律。

15. **noxious** /ˈnɑːkʃəs/  
    **English definition:** harmful, poisonous, or very unpleasant  
    **中文释义：** 有毒有害的；令人不快的  
    **Example:** The factory was fined for releasing noxious fumes into the air.  
    **中文：** 这家工厂因向空气中排放有害烟雾而被罚款。

16. **odious** /ˈoʊdiəs/  
    **English definition:** extremely unpleasant or morally offensive  
    **中文释义：** 令人憎恶的；极其讨厌的  
    **Example:** The organization condemned the odious treatment of migrant workers.  
    **中文：** 该组织谴责了对移民工人的恶劣对待。

17. **penchant** /ˈpɑːntʃɑːnt/  
    **English definition:** a strong natural liking for something  
    **中文释义：** 偏好；嗜好  
    **Example:** She has a penchant for solving problems that others consider impossible.  
    **中文：** 她偏爱解决别人认为不可能解决的问题。

18. **perfidious** /pərˈfɪdiəs/  
    **English definition:** deliberately deceitful and disloyal  
    **中文释义：** 背信弃义的；奸诈的  
    **Example:** The negotiator was dismissed after making a perfidious attempt to leak confidential terms.  
    **中文：** 这名谈判者试图泄露机密条款，因而被解雇。

19. **perjury** /ˈpɜːrdʒəri/  
    **English definition:** the crime of deliberately lying while giving evidence in court  
    **中文释义：** 伪证罪；作伪证  
    **Example:** The witness was charged with perjury after investigators found contradictory records.  
    **中文：** 调查人员发现相互矛盾的记录后，这名证人被控作伪证。

20. **piquant** /ˈpiːkənt/  
    **English definition:** pleasantly sharp or spicy in taste; interestingly provocative  
    **中文释义：** 辛辣开胃的；引人兴趣的  
    **Example:** The article offered a piquant observation about the changing nature of fame.  
    **中文：** 这篇文章对名望不断变化的性质提出了一个耐人寻味的观点。

21. **polemic** /pəˈlemɪk/  
    **English definition:** a strongly argued written or spoken attack on an opinion or system  
    **中文释义：** 争论性文章；强烈抨击  
    **Example:** The book is a forceful polemic against unchecked corporate power.  
    **中文：** 这本书强烈抨击了不受约束的企业权力。

22. **portentous** /pɔːrˈtentəs/  
    **English definition:** suggesting that something important or unpleasant is about to happen  
    **中文释义：** 预示重大事件的；不祥的  
    **Example:** Dark clouds gathered in a portentous sign of the approaching storm.  
    **中文：** 乌云聚集，预示着暴风雨即将来临。

23. **repartee** /ˌrepərˈtiː/  
    **English definition:** quick and witty exchanges in conversation  
    **中文释义：** 机敏的应答；妙语连珠  
    **Example:** The interview was memorable for the repartee between the host and the author.  
    **中文：** 主持人与作者之间机敏风趣的应答让这场采访令人难忘。

24. **reprobate** /ˈreprəbeɪt/  
    **English definition:** a morally unprincipled person; morally unacceptable  
    **中文释义：** 道德败坏的人；应受谴责的  
    **Example:** The novel’s reprobate protagonist gradually learns to take responsibility for his actions.  
    **中文：** 小说中那个道德败坏的主人公逐渐学会为自己的行为负责。

25. **subterfuge** /ˈsʌbtərfjuːdʒ/  
    **English definition:** a secret or dishonest method used to hide the truth or achieve a goal  
    **中文释义：** 欺骗手段；诡计  
    **Example:** The company used subterfuge to avoid disclosing the environmental risks.  
    **中文：** 这家公司用欺骗手段避免披露环境风险。
