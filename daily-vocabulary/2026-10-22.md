# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-22

以下 25 个词汇偏进阶但适用于日常阅读、工作和正式表达，兼顾实用性与拓展性。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abundance** /əˈbʌndəns/  
   **English definition:** a very large quantity of something  
   **中文释义：** 大量；充裕；丰富  
   **Example:** The region has an abundance of skilled workers and technical resources.  
   **中文：** 这个地区拥有大量熟练工人和技术资源。

2. **accelerate** /əkˈseləreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to happen or make something happen faster  
   **中文释义：** 加快；加速  
   **Example:** Automating routine checks could accelerate the review process.  
   **中文：** 将常规检查自动化可以加快审核流程。

3. **accomplish** /əˈkɑːmplɪʃ/  
   **English definition:** to succeed in doing or completing something, especially after effort  
   **中文释义：** 完成；实现；达成  
   **Example:** The team accomplished its main objective ahead of schedule.  
   **中文：** 团队提前完成了主要目标。

4. **accustomed** /əˈkʌstəmd/  
   **English definition:** familiar with something through repeated experience  
   **中文释义：** 习惯于……的；适应了的  
   **Example:** New staff may need time to become accustomed to the reporting system.  
   **中文：** 新员工可能需要一些时间来适应这套报告系统。

5. **acquisition** /ˌækwɪˈzɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** the act of obtaining something; a company or asset that has been bought  
   **中文释义：** 获得；购置；收购的公司或资产  
   **Example:** The acquisition gave the company access to a new distribution network.  
   **中文：** 这次收购使公司获得了一个新的分销网络。

6. **adaptable** /əˈdæptəbəl/  
   **English definition:** able to change or be changed easily to suit new conditions  
   **中文释义：** 适应力强的；可调整的  
   **Example:** An adaptable plan can accommodate changes in demand.  
   **中文：** 灵活的计划能够适应需求变化。

7. **administrative** /ədˈmɪnɪstreɪtɪv/  
   **English definition:** relating to the organization and management of a business or institution  
   **中文释义：** 行政的；管理事务的  
   **Example:** The online form has reduced the administrative work required to process applications.  
   **中文：** 这份在线表格减少了处理申请所需的行政工作。

8. **advancement** /ədˈvænsmənt/  
   **English definition:** progress or development; promotion to a higher position  
   **中文释义：** 进步；发展；晋升  
   **Example:** The award recognizes her advancement of medical research.  
   **中文：** 这项奖励表彰了她对医学研究发展的推动。

9. **affection** /əˈfekʃən/  
   **English definition:** a feeling of liking or caring for someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 喜爱；关爱；钟爱  
   **Example:** He has a strong affection for the neighborhood where he grew up.  
   **中文：** 他非常喜欢自己长大的那个社区。

10. **allegation** /ˌæləˈɡeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a claim that someone has done something wrong, which has not yet been proven  
    **中文释义：** 指控；未经证实的说法  
    **Example:** The company denied the allegation and agreed to an independent review.  
    **中文：** 公司否认了这项指控，并同意接受独立审查。

11. **alliance** /əˈlaɪəns/  
    **English definition:** a formal agreement or partnership between people, organizations, or countries  
    **中文释义：** 联盟；结盟；合作关系  
    **Example:** The two research centers formed an alliance to share data and equipment.  
    **中文：** 两家研究中心结成合作联盟，共享数据和设备。

12. **alteration** /ˌɔːltəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a change made to something, often to improve or adapt it  
    **中文释义：** 改动；修改；变更  
    **Example:** Any alteration to the design must be recorded in the project log.  
    **中文：** 对设计所做的任何改动都必须记录在项目日志中。

13. **amendment** /əˈmendmənt/  
    **English definition:** a change made to a law, contract, or official document  
    **中文释义：** 修正案；修订；修改条款  
    **Example:** Both parties signed an amendment extending the delivery period.  
    **中文：** 双方签署了一份延长交付期限的修订条款。

14. **amid** /əˈmɪd/  
    **English definition:** in the middle of or surrounded by something, especially an event or situation  
    **中文释义：** 在……之中；处于……包围中  
    **Example:** The service remained available amid widespread network disruptions.  
    **中文：** 在网络大范围中断期间，这项服务仍保持可用。

15. **anonymous** /əˈnɑːnəməs/  
    **English definition:** not identified by name; having an unknown or hidden identity  
    **中文释义：** 匿名的；不具名的  
    **Example:** The survey allows employees to submit anonymous feedback.  
    **中文：** 这项调查允许员工匿名提交反馈。

16. **anticipation** /ænˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the act of expecting something to happen; the feeling of excitement or concern beforehand  
    **中文释义：** 预期；预料；期待  
    **Example:** In anticipation of higher demand, the supplier increased its stock.  
    **中文：** 由于预期需求会上升，供应商增加了库存。

17. **applicant** /ˈæplɪkənt/  
    **English definition:** a person who formally asks for a job, place, or service  
    **中文释义：** 申请人；求职者  
    **Example:** Each applicant must provide two professional references.  
    **中文：** 每位申请人都必须提供两位职业推荐人。

18. **appraisal** /əˈpreɪzəl/  
    **English definition:** a judgment of the value, quality, or performance of something  
    **中文释义：** 评估；估价；考核  
    **Example:** The annual appraisal includes feedback from both the employee and the manager.  
    **中文：** 年度考核会收集员工和经理双方的反馈。

19. **artifact** /ˈɑːrtɪfækt/  
    **English definition:** an object made or changed by people, often one of historical or cultural interest  
    **中文释义：** 人工制品；（尤指）历史文物  
    **Example:** The artifact was carefully preserved because it offers evidence of early trade.  
    **中文：** 这件文物得到了妥善保存，因为它为早期贸易提供了证据。

20. **assurance** /əˈʃʊrəns/  
    **English definition:** a promise or statement that gives confidence that something is true or will happen  
    **中文释义：** 保证；确信；担保  
    **Example:** Customers received assurance that their personal data would remain confidential.  
    **中文：** 客户得到保证，其个人数据会继续保密。

21. **bargain** /ˈbɑːrɡən/  
    **English definition:** an agreement between people; something bought for less than its usual price  
    **中文释义：** 协议；交易；便宜货  
    **Example:** They struck a bargain that gave both suppliers predictable orders.  
    **中文：** 他们达成了一项协议，使双方供应商都能获得稳定的订单。

22. **beneficial** /ˌbenəˈfɪʃəl/  
    **English definition:** producing a helpful or positive effect  
    **中文释义：** 有益的；有利的  
    **Example:** Regular breaks can be beneficial to concentration and accuracy.  
    **中文：** 定时休息有助于保持专注和准确。

23. **breakdown** /ˈbreɪkdaʊn/  
    **English definition:** a failure to work; a detailed division or explanation of something  
    **中文释义：** 故障；崩溃；细分说明  
    **Example:** The report includes a cost breakdown for each phase of the project.  
    **中文：** 报告列出了项目各阶段的成本明细。

24. **burden** /ˈbɜːrdən/  
    **English definition:** a difficult or heavy responsibility, duty, or load  
    **中文释义：** 负担；重担；责任  
    **Example:** The revised process reduced the paperwork burden on small businesses.  
    **中文：** 修订后的流程减轻了小型企业的文书负担。

25. **calculation** /ˌkælkjəˈleɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the process of working out an amount or result using numbers; a carefully considered estimate  
    **中文释义：** 计算；估算；盘算  
    **Example:** A revised calculation showed that the project could finish within budget.  
    **中文：** 重新计算后发现，项目可以在预算范围内完成。
