# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-18

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abnegation** /ˌæbnɪˈɡeɪʃən/ — the act of giving up one’s own wishes or interests；放弃自己的愿望或利益的行为；克制；自我牺牲
   - **Example:** Her abnegation of personal comfort allowed the team to meet the deadline.
   - **中文：** 她牺牲个人舒适，帮助团队赶上了截止日期。

2. **accretion** /əˈkriːʃən/ — a gradual increase caused by the addition of layers or parts；由层层增加或部分累积造成的逐渐增长；积累；增大
   - **Example:** The company’s success was the result of years of accretion rather than one sudden breakthrough.
   - **中文：** 公司的成功源于多年的积累，而不是某一次突然的突破。

3. **aesthetic** /esˈθetɪk/ — concerned with beauty or the appreciation of art；与美或艺术欣赏有关的；美学的；审美的
   - **Example:** The redesign improves both the website’s function and its aesthetic appeal.
   - **中文：** 改版既提升了网站的功能，也增强了其审美吸引力。

4. **allegiance** /əˈliːdʒəns/ — loyalty or commitment to a person, group, or cause；对某人、团体或事业的忠诚或承诺；忠诚
   - **Example:** The officer pledged allegiance to the constitution.
   - **中文：** 这名官员宣誓效忠宪法。

5. **amelioration** /əˌmiːliəˈreɪʃən/ — an improvement in a bad or difficult situation；对糟糕或困难状况的改善；改善
   - **Example:** The new policy led to a modest amelioration of working conditions.
   - **中文：** 新政策使工作条件得到了一定改善。

6. **aperture** /ˈæpərtʃər/ — an opening or hole, especially one that allows light through；开口或孔，尤其是允许光线通过的；孔径；开口
   - **Example:** Adjusting the camera’s aperture changed the depth of field.
   - **中文：** 调整相机光圈改变了景深。

7. **arid** /ˈærɪd/ — extremely dry or lacking interest and imagination；极其干燥或缺乏趣味和想象力的；干旱的；枯燥的
   - **Example:** The report was technically accurate but stylistically arid.
   - **中文：** 这份报告在技术上很准确，但文风枯燥。

8. **assiduity** /ˌæsɪˈduːəti/ — constant and careful effort；持续而细致的努力；勤勉
   - **Example:** Her assiduity in learning the language impressed her colleagues.
   - **中文：** 她学习这门语言时表现出的勤勉给同事留下了深刻印象。

9. **atrocious** /əˈtroʊʃəs/ — extremely bad, unpleasant, or cruel；极其糟糕、令人不快或残酷的；糟糕的；残暴的
   - **Example:** The hotel received complaints about its atrocious service.
   - **中文：** 这家酒店因糟糕的服务收到了投诉。

10. **audacity** /ɔːˈdæsəti/ — boldness that may seem rude or reckless；可能显得无礼或鲁莽的大胆；厚颜；大胆
    - **Example:** He had the audacity to demand a refund after missing the deadline.
    - **中文：** 他错过截止日期后，竟厚着脸皮要求退款。

11. **augury** /ˈɔːɡjəri/ — a sign of what may happen in the future；预示未来可能发生之事的迹象；预兆
    - **Example:** Rising demand is a favorable augury for the industry.
    - **中文：** 不断增长的需求是这个行业的有利预兆。

12. **avert** /əˈvɜːrt/ — prevent something bad from happening；防止坏事发生；避免
    - **Example:** Quick action averted a much larger financial loss.
    - **中文：** 迅速采取行动避免了更大的经济损失。

13. **belligerence** /bəˈlɪdʒərəns/ — aggressive or hostile behavior；具有攻击性或敌意的行为；好战；敌对
    - **Example:** The negotiator’s belligerence made a peaceful settlement less likely.
    - **中文：** 谈判者的敌对态度降低了和平解决的可能性。

14. **capitulate** /kəˈpɪtʃəleɪt/ — stop resisting and agree to demands；停止抵抗并同意对方要求；屈服；投降
    - **Example:** After weeks of discussion, the supplier capitulated to the revised terms.
    - **中文：** 经过数周讨论，供应商最终接受了修订后的条款。

15. **chagrin** /ˈʃæɡrɪn/ — distress or embarrassment caused by failure or disappointment；因失败或失望而产生的懊恼或尴尬；懊恼
    - **Example:** To his chagrin, he discovered that the file had never been saved.
    - **中文：** 令他懊恼的是，他发现文件从未保存过。

16. **conjectural** /kənˈdʒektʃərəl/ — based on guesses rather than firm evidence；基于猜测而非确凿证据的；推测性的
    - **Example:** The explanation remains conjectural until the researchers find more evidence.
    - **中文：** 在研究人员找到更多证据之前，这个解释仍只是推测性的。

17. **consolidate** /kənˈsɑːlɪdeɪt/ — combine or strengthen something into a more effective whole；把某事物合并或加强成更有效的整体；巩固；整合
    - **Example:** The merger will consolidate the company’s position in the market.
    - **中文：** 合并将巩固公司在市场中的地位。

18. **credence** /ˈkriːdəns/ — belief that something is true or trustworthy；相信某事真实或可信；信任；相信
    - **Example:** The new evidence lends credence to the witness’s account.
    - **中文：** 新证据增加了人们对证人陈述的可信度。

19. **demarcate** /ˈdiːmɑːrkeɪt/ — set or mark the limits of something；设定或标出某事物的界限；划界
    - **Example:** The fence demarcates the private land from the public path.
    - **中文：** 栅栏将私人土地与公共小路分隔开来。

20. **derisive** /dɪˈraɪsɪv/ — expressing mockery or contempt；表达嘲笑或轻蔑的；嘲讽的
    - **Example:** His derisive laughter discouraged others from sharing their ideas.
    - **中文：** 他嘲讽的笑声让其他人不敢分享自己的想法。

21. **diligence** /ˈdɪlɪdʒəns/ — careful and persistent effort；认真而坚持不懈的努力；勤勉
    - **Example:** The investigation required patience, diligence, and attention to detail.
    - **中文：** 这项调查需要耐心、勤勉以及对细节的关注。

22. **effrontery** /ɪˈfrʌntəri/ — shameless boldness or impudence；不知羞耻的大胆或厚颜无耻；厚颜
    - **Example:** She was shocked by the contractor’s effrontery in demanding extra payment.
    - **中文：** 承包商厚颜无耻地要求额外付款，让她感到震惊。

23. **elucidate** /ɪˈluːsɪdeɪt/ — make something clear by explaining it；通过解释使某事清楚；阐明
    - **Example:** The diagram helps elucidate the relationship between the two processes.
    - **中文：** 这张图有助于阐明两个过程之间的关系。

24. **evasive** /ɪˈveɪsɪv/ — avoiding a direct answer or clear commitment；回避直接回答或明确承诺的；闪烁其词的
    - **Example:** The official gave an evasive response when asked about the budget.
    - **中文：** 当被问及预算时，这名官员给出了闪烁其词的回应。

25. **finesse** /fɪˈnes/ — skillful and delicate handling of a difficult situation；巧妙而细致地处理困难局面的能力；技巧；手腕
    - **Example:** She handled the sensitive negotiation with considerable finesse.
    - **中文：** 她以高超的技巧处理了这场敏感的谈判。
