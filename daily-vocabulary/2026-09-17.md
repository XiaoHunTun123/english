# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-17

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abstention** /əbˈstenʃən/ — the act of choosing not to vote or participate；选择不投票或不参与的行为；弃权
   - **Example:** The motion passed despite several abstentions.
   - **中文：** 尽管有几人弃权，这项动议仍然通过了。

2. **admonition** /ˌædməˈnɪʃən/ — a warning or mild reprimand；警告或温和的责备；告诫
   - **Example:** The teacher’s admonition reminded the class to cite its sources.
   - **中文：** 老师的告诫提醒全班要注明资料来源。

3. **amalgam** /əˈmælɡəm/ — a mixture or combination of different elements；不同元素的混合物或结合体；混合物
   - **Example:** The course is an amalgam of theory, practice, and field research.
   - **中文：** 这门课程融合了理论、实践和实地研究。

4. **ambidextrous** /ˌæmbɪˈdekstrəs/ — able to use both hands equally well；能够同样熟练地使用双手的；双手灵活的
   - **Example:** The ambidextrous player can pass accurately with either hand.
   - **中文：** 这名双手灵活的运动员用哪只手都能准确传球。

5. **anachronism** /əˈnækrənɪzəm/ — something belonging to a different time period；属于另一个时代的事物；时代错误
   - **Example:** A smartphone in the historical drama was an obvious anachronism.
   - **中文：** 历史剧中的智能手机明显是一个时代错误。

6. **ardor** /ˈɑːrdər/ — intense enthusiasm or passion；强烈的热情或激情；热忱
   - **Example:** She pursued the project with the ardor of a first-time entrepreneur.
   - **中文：** 她以初创企业家般的热忱推进这个项目。

7. **asperity** /əˈsperəti/ — harshness in manner, speech, or conditions；态度、言语或环境上的严厉；尖刻
   - **Example:** He spoke with unexpected asperity when the deadline was missed.
   - **中文：** 截止日期被错过时，他说话出人意料地尖刻。

8. **blatant** /ˈbleɪtənt/ — very obvious and often offensively wrong；非常明显且通常错误得令人反感的；明目张胆的
   - **Example:** The audit uncovered a blatant attempt to mislead investors.
   - **中文：** 审计揭露了一次明目张胆地误导投资者的企图。

9. **confluent** /ˈkɑːnfluənt/ — flowing or coming together；流到一起或汇聚到一起的；汇流的
   - **Example:** The village lies near the confluent streams of two mountain rivers.
   - **中文：** 这个村庄位于两条山河汇流处附近。

10. **deprecatory** /ˈdeprəkətɔːri/ — expressing disapproval or belittling someone or something；表示不赞成或贬低某人某事的；贬低的
    - **Example:** His deprecatory comments discouraged the junior researchers.
    - **中文：** 他贬低性的评论打击了年轻研究人员的积极性。

11. **disillusioned** /ˌdɪsɪˈluːʒənd/ — disappointed after discovering that something is not as good as believed；发现某事并非想象中那么好后感到失望的；幻灭的
    - **Example:** Many employees became disillusioned after repeated promises were broken.
    - **中文：** 一再的承诺被打破后，许多员工感到幻灭。

12. **draconian** /dreɪˈkoʊniən/ — excessively harsh or severe；过分严厉或苛刻的；严苛的
    - **Example:** Critics argued that the draconian rules would punish minor mistakes too severely.
    - **中文：** 批评者认为，这些严苛的规定会过度惩罚小错误。

13. **duplicitous** /duːˈplɪsɪtəs/ — deceitful or deliberately two-faced；欺骗性的或故意两面三刀的；欺诈的
    - **Example:** The negotiator’s duplicitous behavior damaged trust between the parties.
    - **中文：** 谈判者欺诈性的行为损害了双方之间的信任。

14. **fallacy** /ˈfæləsi/ — a mistaken belief or faulty line of reasoning；错误的信念或有缺陷的推理；谬误
    - **Example:** The argument rests on the fallacy that correlation always proves causation.
    - **中文：** 这个论点建立在“相关性总能证明因果关系”这一谬误之上。

15. **haughty** /ˈhɔːti/ — arrogantly believing oneself superior to others；傲慢地认为自己高人一等的；傲慢的
    - **Example:** Her haughty manner made collaboration unnecessarily difficult.
    - **中文：** 她傲慢的态度让合作变得不必要地困难。

16. **impasse** /ˈɪmpæs/ — a situation in which no progress is possible；无法取得进展的局面；僵局
    - **Example:** The negotiations reached an impasse over the question of funding.
    - **中文：** 谈判在资金问题上陷入了僵局。

17. **introspection** /ˌɪntrəˈspekʃən/ — careful examination of one’s own thoughts and feelings；对自己思想和情感的仔细审视；反省；内省
    - **Example:** The setback prompted a period of honest introspection.
    - **中文：** 这次挫折促使他进行了一段诚实的自我反省。

18. **malign** /məˈlaɪn/ — speak about someone or something in a harmful and unfair way；以有害且不公正的方式谈论某人或某事；诋毁
    - **Example:** The campaign tried to malign its opponent rather than address the evidence.
    - **中文：** 这场竞选试图诋毁对手，而不是回应证据。

19. **negligent** /ˈneɡlɪdʒənt/ — failing to give proper care or attention；未给予适当照顾或注意的；疏忽的
    - **Example:** The company was found negligent in maintaining the emergency equipment.
    - **中文：** 调查发现这家公司在维护应急设备方面存在疏忽。

20. **quandary** /ˈkwɑːndəri/ — a difficult situation requiring a choice between problems；需要在多个难题之间作出选择的困境；两难处境
    - **Example:** The manager was in a quandary over whether to delay the launch.
    - **中文：** 经理面临是否推迟发布的两难处境。

21. **reproach** /rɪˈproʊtʃ/ — express disapproval or disappointment toward someone；对某人表示不赞成或失望；责备
    - **Example:** She reproached herself for overlooking the warning signs.
    - **中文：** 她责备自己忽视了那些警示信号。

22. **subdue** /səbˈduː/ — overcome, control, or bring under control；战胜、控制或使处于控制之下；制服；抑制
    - **Example:** The firefighters worked quickly to subdue the blaze.
    - **中文：** 消防员迅速行动，控制住了火势。

23. **supercilious** /ˌsuːpərˈsɪliəs/ — behaving as though one is superior to others；表现得仿佛高人一等的；傲慢的
    - **Example:** The consultant’s supercilious tone alienated the local staff.
    - **中文：** 顾问傲慢的语气疏远了当地员工。

24. **tedious** /ˈtiːdiəs/ — boring because it is repetitive or takes too long；因重复或耗时太久而令人厌烦的；枯燥的
    - **Example:** Automating the process removed several tedious manual steps.
    - **中文：** 自动化这个流程省去了几个枯燥的手工步骤。

25. **vociferous** /voʊˈsɪfərəs/ — expressing opinions loudly and forcefully；大声且强烈地表达意见的；喧嚷的
    - **Example:** Vociferous critics demanded greater transparency from the agency.
    - **中文：** 喧嚷的批评者要求该机构提高透明度。
