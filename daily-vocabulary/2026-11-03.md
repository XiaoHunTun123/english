# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-11-03

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **allocation** /ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** the act of distributing money, time, or other resources for particular purposes  
   **中文释义：** 分配；配置；拨款  
   **Example:** The committee reviewed the allocation of funds across the three programs.  
   **中文：** 委员会审查了三个项目之间的资金分配。

2. **bureaucracy** /bjʊˈrɑːkrəsi/  
   **English definition:** a system of government or administration with many rules and levels of authority  
   **中文释义：** 官僚体制；繁琐的行政程序  
   **Example:** The small grant was delayed by several layers of bureaucracy.  
   **中文：** 这笔小额拨款被层层繁琐的行政程序耽搁了。

3. **disruptive** /dɪsˈrʌptɪv/  
   **English definition:** causing interruption or disorder; changing the way an established system works  
   **中文释义：** 造成混乱的；颠覆性的  
   **Example:** Road closures during the festival may be disruptive to local businesses.  
   **中文：** 节日期间的道路封闭可能会影响当地商家的正常经营。

4. **diversity** /daɪˈvɜːrsəti/  
   **English definition:** the presence of people or things that differ from one another  
   **中文释义：** 多样性；多元化  
   **Example:** The museum's collection reflects the diversity of the city's communities.  
   **中文：** 这家博物馆的藏品体现了该市各个社区的多样性。

5. **enduring** /ɪnˈdʊrɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** lasting for a long time or continuing despite difficulty  
   **中文释义：** 持久的；长期存在的  
   **Example:** The partnership had an enduring effect on both organizations.  
   **中文：** 这次合作对两个组织都产生了持久影响。

6. **inefficient** /ˌɪnɪˈfɪʃənt/  
   **English definition:** wasting time, effort, or resources while producing a result  
   **中文释义：** 效率低的；浪费资源的  
   **Example:** Manually entering the same data twice is inefficient and increases errors.  
   **中文：** 手动重复录入同一份数据既低效，也会增加出错的可能。

7. **interpersonal** /ˌɪntərˈpɜːrsənəl/  
   **English definition:** relating to communication or relationships between people  
   **中文释义：** 人际之间的；人际交往的  
   **Example:** The course focuses on interpersonal skills such as listening and negotiation.  
   **中文：** 这门课程侧重倾听、谈判等人际交往技能。

8. **invaluable** /ɪnˈvæljəbəl/  
   **English definition:** extremely useful or helpful  
   **中文释义：** 极有用的；无价的  
   **Example:** Her experience was invaluable when the team handled its first major outage.  
   **中文：** 团队首次处理重大服务中断时，她的经验发挥了极大作用。

9. **likelihood** /ˈlaɪkliˌhʊd/  
   **English definition:** the chance that something will happen or be true  
   **中文释义：** 可能性；概率  
   **Example:** Regular maintenance reduces the likelihood of unexpected failures.  
   **中文：** 定期维护能降低意外故障发生的可能性。

10. **misleading** /ˌmɪsˈliːdɪŋ/  
    **English definition:** causing someone to believe something that is not true or complete  
    **中文释义：** 误导性的；使人产生错误认识的  
    **Example:** The chart is misleading because it leaves out the first six months of data.  
    **中文：** 这张图表具有误导性，因为它漏掉了前六个月的数据。

11. **noteworthy** /ˈnoʊtwɜːrði/  
    **English definition:** important or interesting enough to deserve attention  
    **中文释义：** 值得注意的；显著的  
    **Example:** One noteworthy improvement is the shorter response time.  
    **中文：** 一项值得注意的改进是响应时间缩短了。

12. **overestimate** /ˌoʊvərˈestɪmeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to judge that something is greater, more important, or more likely than it really is  
    **中文释义：** 高估；过高估计  
    **Example:** We overestimated how many volunteers would be available on a weekday.  
    **中文：** 我们高估了工作日能来参加的志愿者人数。

13. **persistent** /pərˈsɪstənt/  
    **English definition:** continuing firmly despite difficulty, or lasting longer than expected  
    **中文释义：** 坚持不懈的；持续的  
    **Example:** Persistent requests from users led the developers to add an offline mode.  
    **中文：** 用户持续不断的请求促使开发人员增加了离线模式。

14. **presumably** /prɪˈzuːməbli/  
    **English definition:** used to say that something is likely true based on what is known  
    **中文释义：** 据推测；大概  
    **Example:** The office is empty, so the staff have presumably left for the day.  
    **中文：** 办公室里没人，员工大概已经下班了。

15. **prolonged** /prəˈlɔːŋd/  
    **English definition:** continuing for a longer time than usual or expected  
    **中文释义：** 延长的；持续很久的  
    **Example:** Prolonged exposure to loud noise can damage hearing.  
    **中文：** 长时间暴露在噪声中可能损伤听力。

16. **proportionate** /prəˈpɔːrʃənət/  
    **English definition:** appropriate in size or degree when compared with something else  
    **中文释义：** 相称的；成比例的；适度的  
    **Example:** The response should be proportionate to the actual risk.  
    **中文：** 应对措施应与实际风险相称。

17. **recession** /rɪˈseʃən/  
    **English definition:** a period when economic activity and production decline  
    **中文释义：** 经济衰退  
    **Example:** During the recession, many households postponed large purchases.  
    **中文：** 经济衰退期间，许多家庭推迟了大额消费。

18. **remainder** /rɪˈmeɪndər/  
    **English definition:** the part that is left after the rest has been used, taken, or dealt with  
    **中文释义：** 剩余部分；余数  
    **Example:** We used half the budget in spring and reserved the remainder for autumn.  
    **中文：** 我们春季用了预算的一半，把剩余部分留到秋季使用。

19. **retrospect** /ˈretrəspekt/  
    **English definition:** consideration of something that happened in the past, usually in the phrase “in retrospect”  
    **中文释义：** 回顾；追溯（常用于 in retrospect）  
    **Example:** In retrospect, launching the service before testing it fully was a mistake.  
    **中文：** 现在回想起来，服务还没充分测试就上线是个错误。

20. **scarcity** /ˈskersəti/  
    **English definition:** a situation in which something is available only in limited amounts  
    **中文释义：** 缺乏；稀缺  
    **Example:** Water scarcity has encouraged farms to adopt more efficient irrigation.  
    **中文：** 水资源短缺促使农场采用更高效的灌溉方式。

21. **whereby** /werˈbaɪ/  
    **English definition:** by which or through which a particular result or process happens  
    **中文释义：** 凭借；借以；通过这一方式  
    **Example:** They created a review process whereby staff can report safety concerns anonymously.  
    **中文：** 他们建立了一套审查流程，员工可借此匿名报告安全隐患。

22. **withdraw** /wɪðˈdrɔː/  
    **English definition:** to remove or take back something; to stop participating in an activity  
    **中文释义：** 撤回；取出；退出  
    **Example:** The candidate decided to withdraw from the election.  
    **中文：** 这名候选人决定退出选举。

23. **encompass** /ɪnˈkʌmpəs/  
    **English definition:** to include or cover a wide range of things  
    **中文释义：** 包含；涵盖  
    **Example:** The role encompasses customer support, training, and product feedback.  
    **中文：** 这个职位涵盖客户支持、培训和产品反馈等工作。

24. **inclination** /ˌɪnklɪˈneɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a natural tendency or preference to do or think something  
    **中文释义：** 倾向；爱好；意愿  
    **Example:** She has an inclination to check every detail before making a decision.  
    **中文：** 她倾向于在做决定前核查每个细节。

25. **scrutiny** /ˈskruːtəni/  
    **English definition:** careful and detailed examination  
    **中文释义：** 仔细审查；详细检查  
    **Example:** The proposed contract came under close scrutiny from the legal team.  
    **中文：** 法务团队对拟定的合同进行了严格审查。
