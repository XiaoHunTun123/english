# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-27

25 个兼顾实用与拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **abbreviate** /əˈbriːvieɪt/  
   **English definition:** to make a word or text shorter, often by using only part of it  
   **中文释义：** 缩写；缩短  
   **Example:** In informal notes, people often abbreviate “department” to “dept.”  
   **中文：** 在非正式笔记中，人们常把 “department” 缩写成 “dept.”。

2. **absurd** /əbˈsɜːrd/  
   **English definition:** completely unreasonable or foolish; obviously not true  
   **中文释义：** 荒谬的；不合理的  
   **Example:** It would be absurd to expect everyone to reply within five minutes.  
   **中文：** 指望所有人都在五分钟内回复，实在不合理。

3. **apparent** /əˈpærənt/  
   **English definition:** easy to see or understand; seeming to be true, though not certain  
   **中文释义：** 明显的；表面上的  
   **Example:** The apparent simplicity of the task hid several technical challenges.  
   **中文：** 这项任务表面上很简单，实际却隐藏着几个技术难点。

4. **barrier** /ˈbæriər/  
   **English definition:** something that blocks movement or makes progress difficult  
   **中文释义：** 障碍；壁垒  
   **Example:** High transport costs remain a barrier to local businesses.  
   **中文：** 高昂的运输成本仍是当地企业面临的一道障碍。

5. **briefing** /ˈbriːfɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** a short meeting or report that gives essential information  
   **中文释义：** 简报；情况说明会  
   **Example:** The team received a safety briefing before entering the site.  
   **中文：** 进入现场前，团队接受了安全情况说明。

6. **capacity** /kəˈpæsəti/  
   **English definition:** the ability to do or contain something; the maximum amount something can hold  
   **中文释义：** 能力；容量；容纳量  
   **Example:** The clinic is operating at full capacity this week.  
   **中文：** 这家诊所本周已满负荷运转。

7. **competent** /ˈkɑːmpɪtənt/  
   **English definition:** having enough skill or knowledge to do something successfully  
   **中文释义：** 有能力的；称职的  
   **Example:** She is a competent editor who catches errors without changing the writer's voice.  
   **中文：** 她是一位称职的编辑，能发现错误，同时保留作者的表达风格。

8. **considerable** /kənˈsɪdərəbəl/  
   **English definition:** large or important enough to be noticed  
   **中文释义：** 相当大的；相当重要的  
   **Example:** The redesign required considerable time and coordination.  
   **中文：** 这次重新设计需要投入相当多的时间并做好协调。

9. **contract** /ˈkɑːntrækt/  
   **English definition:** a formal agreement between people or organizations  
   **中文释义：** 合同；契约  
   **Example:** Read the renewal terms carefully before signing the contract.  
   **中文：** 签合同前，请仔细阅读续约条款。

10. **currency** /ˈkɜːrənsi/  
    **English definition:** the system of money used in a particular country  
    **中文释义：** 货币；通货  
    **Example:** The payment platform supports several foreign currencies.  
    **中文：** 这个支付平台支持多种外币。

11. **dedicate** /ˈdedɪkeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to give time, effort, or resources to a particular purpose  
    **中文释义：** 投入；致力于；献给  
    **Example:** He dedicates one evening a week to volunteering.  
    **中文：** 他每周拿出一个晚上做志愿服务。

12. **domestic** /dəˈmestɪk/  
    **English definition:** relating to a person's home or family, or to one's own country  
    **中文释义：** 家庭的；国内的  
    **Example:** Domestic demand for electric buses has grown steadily.  
    **中文：** 国内对电动公交车的需求稳步增长。

13. **draft** /dræft/  
    **English definition:** an early version of a piece of writing, plan, or design  
    **中文释义：** 草稿；初稿  
    **Example:** I shared the first draft so the team could comment on the structure.  
    **中文：** 我分享了初稿，让团队可以对结构提出意见。

14. **dynamic** /daɪˈnæmɪk/  
    **English definition:** constantly changing or developing; energetic  
    **中文释义：** 不断变化的；有活力的  
    **Example:** The company works in a dynamic market where customer needs shift quickly.  
    **中文：** 这家公司身处一个不断变化的市场，客户需求变化很快。

15. **exposure** /ɪkˈspoʊʒər/  
    **English definition:** the state of being in contact with something; experience that helps people learn about something  
    **中文释义：** 接触；体验；暴露  
    **Example:** Regular exposure to different accents can improve listening skills.  
    **中文：** 经常接触不同口音有助于提高听力。

16. **feasibility** /ˌfiːzəˈbɪləti/  
    **English definition:** the possibility of doing something successfully and practically  
    **中文释义：** 可行性  
    **Example:** The report examines the feasibility of a late-night bus service.  
    **中文：** 这份报告评估了开通深夜公交线路的可行性。

17. **incidence** /ˈɪnsɪdəns/  
    **English definition:** the rate or frequency at which something, especially a problem or disease, occurs  
    **中文释义：** 发生率；发生频次  
    **Example:** Better lighting was followed by a decline in the incidence of bicycle theft.  
    **中文：** 照明改善后，自行车盗窃的发生率有所下降。

18. **induce** /ɪnˈduːs/  
    **English definition:** to cause something to happen, or to persuade someone to do something  
    **中文释义：** 引起；导致；劝使  
    **Example:** This medicine may induce drowsiness, so avoid driving after taking it.  
    **中文：** 这种药可能会引起困倦，服用后请避免开车。

19. **initiative** /ɪˈnɪʃətɪv/  
    **English definition:** a new plan or action intended to solve a problem or achieve a goal  
    **中文释义：** 倡议；新举措  
    **Example:** The library launched an initiative to lend laptops to students.  
    **中文：** 图书馆推出了一项新举措，向学生出借笔记本电脑。

20. **insight** /ˈɪnsaɪt/  
    **English definition:** a clear and deep understanding of a situation or problem  
    **中文释义：** 深刻理解；洞察力  
    **Example:** Interviews with users gave us valuable insight into why the feature was confusing.  
    **中文：** 对用户的访谈让我们深入了解了这项功能令人困惑的原因。

21. **manipulate** /məˈnɪpjəleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to control or influence something skillfully, sometimes unfairly  
    **中文释义：** 操纵；巧妙处理  
    **Example:** The analyst was accused of manipulating the figures to make the results look better.  
    **中文：** 这名分析师被指控操纵数据，让结果看起来更好。

22. **methodology** /ˌmeθəˈdɑːlədʒi/  
    **English definition:** a system of methods used in a particular area of study or work  
    **中文释义：** 方法论；一套方法  
    **Example:** The paper explains its methodology so other researchers can assess the findings.  
    **中文：** 论文阐述了研究方法，以便其他研究者评估其结论。

23. **perspective** /pərˈspektɪv/  
    **English definition:** a particular way of thinking about or understanding something  
    **中文释义：** 视角；观点  
    **Example:** Talking to residents gave the planners a different perspective on the project.  
    **中文：** 与居民交谈让规划者从不同角度看待这个项目。

24. **protocol** /ˈproʊtəkɔːl/  
    **English definition:** a set of official rules or procedures for handling a situation  
    **中文释义：** 规程；议定程序  
    **Example:** Staff must follow the emergency protocol if the alarm sounds.  
    **中文：** 警报响起时，员工必须遵守应急规程。

25. **refine** /rɪˈfaɪn/  
    **English definition:** to improve something by making small changes  
    **中文释义：** 改进；完善；提炼  
    **Example:** We refined the instructions after testing them with new users.  
    **中文：** 我们与新用户测试后，对说明内容作了完善。
