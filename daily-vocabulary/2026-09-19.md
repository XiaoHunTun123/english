# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-19

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **adjacent** /əˈdʒeɪsənt/ — next to or very close to something；紧挨或非常接近某物的；相邻的
   - **Example:** The meeting room is adjacent to the main reception area.
   - **中文：** 会议室紧邻主接待区。

2. **alloy** /ˈælɔɪ/ — a mixture of metals or a combination of different elements；金属混合物或不同元素的结合；合金；融合体
   - **Example:** The new material is an alloy of strength and flexibility.
   - **中文：** 这种新材料融合了强度和柔韧性。

3. **anecdote** /ˈænɪkdoʊt/ — a short, interesting story about a real event or person；关于真实事件或人物的简短有趣故事；轶事
   - **Example:** He opened the presentation with a personal anecdote.
   - **中文：** 他用一则个人轶事开始了演讲。

4. **antiquated** /ˈæntɪkweɪtɪd/ — old-fashioned and no longer suitable for modern use；过时且不再适合现代使用的；陈旧的
   - **Example:** The office still relies on an antiquated filing system.
   - **中文：** 这间办公室仍在使用过时的文件归档系统。

5. **assimilate** /əˈsɪməleɪt/ — absorb information or adapt to a new culture or environment；吸收信息或适应新的文化、环境；吸收；融入
   - **Example:** It took her time to assimilate the large amount of technical information.
   - **中文：** 她花了一些时间才吸收大量技术信息。

6. **banter** /ˈbæntər/ — playful and friendly teasing；友好而带玩笑性质的调侃；戏谑
   - **Example:** Their light-hearted banter made the long journey more enjoyable.
   - **中文：** 他们轻松的戏谑让漫长的旅途变得更愉快。

7. **belabor** /bɪˈleɪbər/ — explain, criticize, or work on something at excessive length；过度冗长地解释、批评或处理某事；反复强调；痛击
   - **Example:** I will not belabor the point because the evidence is already clear.
   - **中文：** 证据已经很清楚了，我就不再反复强调这一点。

8. **beset** /bɪˈset/ — trouble or threaten someone or something persistently；持续困扰或威胁某人某事；困扰
   - **Example:** The project was beset by delays from the very beginning.
   - **中文：** 这个项目从一开始就不断受到延误的困扰。

9. **candor** /ˈkændər/ — honesty and openness in expressing thoughts；表达想法时的诚实和坦率；坦诚
   - **Example:** I appreciate your candor, even though the feedback is difficult to hear.
   - **中文：** 我感谢你的坦率，尽管这些反馈听起来很难受。

10. **collate** /kəˈleɪt/ — collect and compare information in an organized way；有条理地收集和比较信息；核对；整理
    - **Example:** The assistant will collate the survey responses before the meeting.
    - **中文：** 助理会在会议前整理并核对调查回复。

11. **defer** /dɪˈfɜːr/ — postpone something or yield respectfully to another person’s judgment；推迟某事或恭敬地服从他人的判断；推迟；听从
    - **Example:** We decided to defer the decision until more facts were available.
    - **中文：** 我们决定等更多事实明确后再作决定。

12. **disillusion** /ˌdɪsɪˈluːʒən/ — cause someone to lose a belief that was too idealistic；使某人失去原本过于理想化的信念；使幻灭
    - **Example:** The scandal disillusioned many supporters of the movement.
    - **中文：** 这起丑闻使这场运动的许多支持者感到幻灭。

13. **drudgery** /ˈdrʌdʒəri/ — hard, boring, and repetitive work；艰苦、乏味且重复的工作；苦差事
    - **Example:** Automation can reduce the drudgery involved in data entry.
    - **中文：** 自动化可以减少数据录入中的苦差事。

14. **goad** /ɡoʊd/ — provoke or encourage someone to take action；刺激或促使某人采取行动；驱使；刺激
    - **Example:** The criticism goaded him into improving the proposal.
    - **中文：** 这番批评促使他改进了提案。

15. **harangue** /həˈræŋ/ — a long and forceful speech criticizing someone；长篇且强烈批评某人的演说；训斥；长篇抨击
    - **Example:** The manager delivered a harangue about missed deadlines.
    - **中文：** 经理就屡次错过截止日期发表了一番长篇训斥。

16. **munificent** /mjuːˈnɪfɪsənt/ — extremely generous, especially with money；极其慷慨的，尤其是在金钱方面；慷慨的
    - **Example:** A munificent grant allowed the laboratory to expand its research.
    - **中文：** 一笔慷慨的拨款使实验室得以扩大研究规模。

17. **oblique** /oʊˈbliːk/ — indirect or not expressed in a straightforward way；间接的或没有直截了当地表达的；含蓄的；间接的
    - **Example:** He made an oblique reference to the disagreement during the interview.
    - **中文：** 他在采访中含蓄地提到了那场分歧。

18. **opportune** /ˌɑːpərˈtuːn/ — happening at a particularly suitable or useful time；在特别合适或有利的时机发生的；适时的
    - **Example:** The funding arrived at an opportune moment for the struggling project.
    - **中文：** 这笔资金在项目困难之际及时到达了。

19. **parsimony** /ˈpɑːrsɪmoʊni/ — extreme unwillingness to spend money or use resources；极不愿花钱或使用资源；吝啬；过度节省
    - **Example:** His parsimony prevented the team from replacing outdated equipment.
    - **中文：** 他的过度节省使团队无法更换过时设备。

20. **acquiescence** /ˌækwiˈesəns/ — passive acceptance without protest；不加抗议的被动接受；默许
    - **Example:** Her silence was interpreted as acquiescence to the new arrangement.
    - **中文：** 她的沉默被理解为对新安排的默许。

21. **anomaly** /əˈnɑːməli/ — something that differs from what is usual or expected；偏离通常或预期情况的事物；异常
    - **Example:** The unusually high reading was treated as an anomaly and checked again.
    - **中文：** 这个异常高的读数被视为异常值，并再次进行了检查。

22. **barometer** /bəˈrɑːmɪtər/ — something that indicates changes in a situation；显示某种情况变化的事物；晴雨表；指标
    - **Example:** Consumer confidence is often a barometer of economic health.
    - **中文：** 消费者信心常常是经济状况的晴雨表。

23. **diffuse** /dɪˈfjuːz/ — spread widely or make something less concentrated；广泛传播或使某物变得不集中；扩散；分散
    - **Example:** The organization used several channels to diffuse the safety information.
    - **中文：** 该组织通过多个渠道传播安全信息。

24. **endemic** /enˈdemɪk/ — regularly found in a particular place or group；经常存在于某一地点或群体中的；地方性的；特有的
    - **Example:** The disease is endemic in several rural regions.
    - **中文：** 这种疾病在几个农村地区流行。

25. **forbearance** /fɔːrˈberəns/ — patient self-control and willingness to be tolerant；耐心的自我克制和宽容意愿；克制；宽容
    - **Example:** The landlord showed considerable forbearance when the tenant fell behind on payments.
    - **中文：** 租户拖欠租金时，房东表现出了相当大的宽容。
