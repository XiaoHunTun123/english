# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-07-29 (Additional Set)

本日追加词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。本文件因当天主文件已存在而单独保存，未覆盖原文件。

1. **abate** /əˈbeɪt/ — to become less strong or intense；减弱；缓和  
   *The storm finally began to abate.*  
   暴风雨终于开始减弱。

2. **adept** /əˈdept/ — highly skilled at something；熟练的；擅长的  
   *She is adept at solving complex problems.*  
   她擅长解决复杂问题。

3. **adverse** /ˈædvɜːrs/ — harmful or unfavorable；不利的；有害的  
   *The drug may have adverse effects.*  
   这种药可能会产生不良影响。

4. **bolster** /ˈboʊlstər/ — to support or strengthen；支持；加强  
   *The evidence bolstered his argument.*  
   证据加强了他的论点。

5. **catalyst** /ˈkætəlɪst/ — something that causes change；催化剂；促成因素  
   *The speech became a catalyst for reform.*  
   这次演讲成为改革的催化剂。

6. **circumvent** /ˌsɜːrkəmˈvent/ — to avoid a problem or restriction；规避；绕过  
   *They found a way to circumvent the restrictions.*  
   他们找到了规避限制的方法。

7. **conspicuous** /kənˈspɪkjuəs/ — easy to notice；显眼的；明显的  
   *The bright sign was conspicuous from a distance.*  
   那块亮色招牌从远处就很显眼。

8. **cultivate** /ˈkʌltɪveɪt/ — to develop a skill, quality, or relationship；培养；发展  
   *Reading helps cultivate critical thinking.*  
   阅读有助于培养批判性思维。

9. **deter** /dɪˈtɜːr/ — to discourage someone from doing something；阻止；威慑  
   *High penalties may deter people from speeding.*  
   高额罚款可能会阻止人们超速驾驶。

10. **dormant** /ˈdɔːrmənt/ — inactive but capable of becoming active；休眠的；暂时不活跃的  
    *The volcano has been dormant for centuries.*  
    这座火山已经休眠了几个世纪。

11. **elusive** /ɪˈluːsɪv/ — difficult to find, define, or achieve；难以捉摸的  
    *A clear solution remains elusive.*  
    明确的解决方案仍然难以找到。

12. **emulate** /ˈemjuleɪt/ — to copy someone successfully；仿效；赶超  
    *Young athletes often emulate their heroes.*  
    年轻运动员常常仿效自己的偶像。

13. **empirical** /ɪmˈpɪrɪkəl/ — based on observation or experiment；以经验或实验为依据的  
    *The theory needs more empirical evidence.*  
    这个理论需要更多实证证据。

14. **exacerbate** /ɪɡˈzæsərbeɪt/ — to make a problem worse；加剧；恶化  
    *Lack of sleep can exacerbate stress.*  
    睡眠不足会加剧压力。

15. **formidable** /ˈfɔːrmɪdəbəl/ — very difficult or impressive；强大的；难以应对的  
    *They faced a formidable opponent.*  
    他们面对着一个强大的对手。

16. **incentive** /ɪnˈsentɪv/ — something that encourages action；激励；奖励  
    *The bonus is an incentive to work harder.*  
    奖金是努力工作的激励因素。

17. **inadvertently** /ˌɪnədˈvɜːrtəntli/ — unintentionally；无意中地  
    *He inadvertently revealed the secret.*  
    他无意中泄露了秘密。

18. **integrate** /ˈɪntɪɡreɪt/ — to combine parts into a whole；整合；融入  
    *The new software integrates several useful tools.*  
    新软件整合了几个有用的工具。

19. **lucrative** /ˈluːkrətɪv/ — producing a lot of profit；获利丰厚的  
    *Online education can be a lucrative business.*  
    在线教育可能是一项利润丰厚的业务。

20. **marginal** /ˈmɑːrdʒɪnəl/ — very small or limited；微小的；边缘的  
    *The change had only a marginal effect.*  
    这项改变的影响非常有限。

21. **mediate** /ˈmiːdieɪt/ — to help settle a disagreement；调解；斡旋  
    *An experienced lawyer mediated the dispute.*  
    一位经验丰富的律师调解了这场争端。

22. **paramount** /ˈpærəmaʊnt/ — more important than anything else；最重要的  
    *Public safety is of paramount importance.*  
    公共安全至关重要。

23. **preempt** /priˈempt/ — to prevent something by acting first；预先阻止；抢先  
    *The company acted early to preempt criticism.*  
    公司提前采取行动以避免受到批评。

24. **rationale** /ˌræʃəˈnæl/ — the underlying reason for a decision；基本理由；依据  
    *The report explains the rationale behind the policy.*  
    报告解释了这项政策背后的理由。

25. **stringent** /ˈstrɪndʒənt/ — strict and demanding；严格的；严苛的  
    *The factory must meet stringent safety standards.*  
    这家工厂必须符合严格的安全标准。
