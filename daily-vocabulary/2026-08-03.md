# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-08-03

本日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。

1. **ascertain** /ˌæsərˈteɪn/ — to find out something with certainty；查明；确定  
   *We need to ascertain the cause of the problem.*  
   我们需要查明问题的原因。

2. **concede** /kənˈsiːd/ — to admit that something is true；承认；让步  
   *He conceded that the plan had weaknesses.*  
   他承认这个计划存在缺陷。

3. **conform** /kənˈfɔːrm/ — to comply with rules or standards；遵守；符合  
   *All products must conform to safety standards.*  
   所有产品都必须符合安全标准。

4. **derive** /dɪˈraɪv/ — to obtain something from a source；获得；源自  
   *Many English words derive from Latin.*  
   许多英语单词源自拉丁语。

5. **dissuade** /dɪˈsweɪd/ — to persuade someone not to do something；劝阻  
   *Nothing could dissuade her from pursuing her goal.*  
   没有什么能劝阻她追求自己的目标。

6. **diverge** /daɪˈvɜːrdʒ/ — to develop in different directions；分歧；分开  
   *Their opinions began to diverge.*  
   他们的观点开始出现分歧。

7. **diligent** /ˈdɪlɪdʒənt/ — hardworking and careful；勤奋的；认真仔细的  
   *She is diligent in her research.*  
   她做研究非常认真勤奋。

8. **discreet** /dɪˈskriːt/ — careful not to attract attention；谨慎的；低调的  
   *Please be discreet about this matter.*  
   请对此事保持谨慎低调。

9. **extrapolate** /ɪkˈstræpəleɪt/ — to estimate from known information；推断；外推  
   *Researchers extrapolated future trends from the data.*  
   研究人员根据数据推断了未来趋势。

10. **frugal** /ˈfruːɡəl/ — careful about spending money or resources；节俭的  
    *A frugal lifestyle can reduce financial stress.*  
    节俭的生活方式可以减轻经济压力。

11. **genuine** /ˈdʒenjuɪn/ — real and sincere；真实的；真诚的  
    *She showed genuine concern for her colleagues.*  
    她真诚地关心同事。

12. **impede** /ɪmˈpiːd/ — to slow down or block progress；阻碍  
    *Heavy traffic impeded the delivery.*  
    拥堵的交通阻碍了货物配送。

13. **juxtapose** /ˌdʒʌkstəˈpoʊz/ — to place two things side by side for comparison；并置；对照  
    *The exhibition juxtaposes old and modern art.*  
    这场展览将古代艺术与现代艺术并置对照。

14. **negotiate** /nɪˈɡoʊʃieɪt/ — to discuss in order to reach an agreement；协商；谈判  
    *The two sides negotiated a new contract.*  
    双方协商达成了一份新合同。

15. **offset** /ˈɔːfset/ — to balance or counteract something；抵消；补偿  
    *Trees can help offset carbon emissions.*  
    植树有助于抵消碳排放。

16. **precarious** /prɪˈkeriəs/ — unstable and likely to become dangerous；不稳定的；危险的  
    *The hikers stood on a precarious ledge.*  
    徒步者站在一处危险不稳的岩架上。

17. **proficient** /prəˈfɪʃənt/ — skilled and competent；熟练的；精通的  
    *He is proficient in several programming languages.*  
    他熟练掌握几种编程语言。

18. **reinforce** /ˌriːɪnˈfɔːrs/ — to strengthen or support；加强；巩固  
    *The results reinforce the original conclusion.*  
    这些结果进一步支持了原来的结论。

19. **synthesize** /ˈsɪnθəsaɪz/ — to combine ideas into a whole；综合；合成  
    *The essay synthesizes several important theories.*  
    这篇文章综合了几个重要理论。

20. **transient** /ˈtrænziənt/ — lasting only a short time；短暂的；暂时的  
    *The discomfort was transient.*  
    这种不适是暂时的。

21. **unanimous** /juˈnænɪməs/ — agreed by everyone；一致同意的  
    *The committee reached a unanimous decision.*  
    委员会达成了一致决定。

22. **validate** /ˈvælɪdeɪt/ — to confirm that something is valid；验证；证实  
    *The experiment validated the theory.*  
    这项实验验证了该理论。

23. **wary** /ˈweri/ — cautious and watchful；谨慎的；警惕的  
    *Investors are wary of sudden market changes.*  
    投资者对市场突然变化保持警惕。

24. **withhold** /wɪðˈhoʊld/ — to refuse to give or reveal something；扣留；隐瞒  
    *The company withheld important information.*  
    公司隐瞒了重要信息。

25. **yield** /jiːld/ — to produce or provide；产生；提供  
    *The investment yielded a modest return.*  
    这项投资带来了适度的回报。
