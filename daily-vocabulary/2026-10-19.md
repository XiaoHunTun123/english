# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-19

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **acquaint** /əˈkweɪnt/  
   **English definition:** to make someone familiar with a person, subject, or situation  
   **中文释义：** 使熟悉；使了解  
   **Example:** Please acquaint yourself with the updated safety guide before the site visit.  
   **中文：** 现场走访前，请先熟悉更新后的安全指南。

2. **adhere** /ədˈhɪr/  
   **English definition:** to follow a rule, agreement, or belief closely; to stick firmly to a surface  
   **中文释义：** 遵守；坚持；粘附  
   **Example:** All vendors must adhere to the data-handling requirements.  
   **中文：** 所有供应商都必须遵守数据处理要求。

3. **affluent** /ˈæfluənt/  
   **English definition:** having a great deal of money or valuable resources  
   **中文释义：** 富裕的；富足的  
   **Example:** The survey included households from both affluent and lower-income neighborhoods.  
   **中文：** 这项调查涵盖了富裕社区和低收入社区的家庭。

4. **agenda** /əˈdʒendə/  
   **English definition:** a list of matters to be discussed at a meeting; a set of aims someone is pursuing  
   **中文释义：** 议程；计划；（隐含的）目标  
   **Example:** The first item on the agenda is the launch timeline.  
   **中文：** 议程上的第一项是产品发布的时间安排。

5. **aggravate** /ˈæɡrəveɪt/  
   **English definition:** to make a problem or condition worse, or to annoy someone  
   **中文释义：** 加重；恶化；激怒  
   **Example:** Delaying the repair could aggravate the leak.  
   **中文：** 延迟维修可能会使漏水情况恶化。

6. **agile** /ˈædʒəl/  
   **English definition:** able to move quickly and easily, or to adapt rapidly to change  
   **中文释义：** 灵活的；敏捷的；适应力强的  
   **Example:** An agile team can adjust its plans when new evidence appears.  
   **中文：** 灵活的团队能够在出现新证据时及时调整计划。

7. **ample** /ˈæmpəl/  
   **English definition:** enough or more than enough for a particular purpose  
   **中文释义：** 充足的；足够的  
   **Example:** We left ample time for questions after the presentation.  
   **中文：** 我们在演示结束后留出了充足的提问时间。

8. **analogy** /əˈnælədʒi/  
   **English definition:** a comparison that explains something by showing how it is similar to something else  
   **中文释义：** 类比；比拟  
   **Example:** The trainer used an analogy to explain how the caching system works.  
   **中文：** 培训师用一个类比解释缓存系统的工作方式。

9. **asset** /ˈæset/  
   **English definition:** a useful or valuable quality, skill, person, or resource  
   **中文释义：** 优势；有价值的人或物；资产  
   **Example:** Her ability to explain technical issues clearly is a real asset to the team.  
   **中文：** 她清楚解释技术问题的能力是团队的一项宝贵优势。

10. **associate** /əˈsoʊʃieɪt/  
    **English definition:** to connect one person, thing, or idea with another in your mind  
    **中文释义：** 联想；把……联系起来  
    **Example:** Many customers associate the green label with lower energy use.  
    **中文：** 许多顾客会把绿色标签与较低的能耗联系起来。

11. **atmosphere** /ˈætməsfɪr/  
    **English definition:** the feeling or mood of a place or situation; the layer of gases around a planet  
    **中文释义：** 气氛；氛围；大气层  
    **Example:** The open layout created a relaxed atmosphere for collaboration.  
    **中文：** 开放式布局营造了轻松的协作氛围。

12. **betray** /bɪˈtreɪ/  
    **English definition:** to reveal information or a feeling unintentionally, or to be disloyal to someone  
    **中文释义：** 暴露（情绪或信息）；背叛  
    **Example:** His hurried reply betrayed his concern about the deadline.  
    **中文：** 他匆忙的回复暴露了他对截止日期的担忧。

13. **candidate** /ˈkændɪdeɪt/  
    **English definition:** a person being considered for a job, position, award, or election  
    **中文释义：** 候选人；求职者；申请者  
    **Example:** Each candidate completed the same practical exercise.  
    **中文：** 每位候选人都完成了相同的实践任务。

14. **colleague** /ˈkɑːliːɡ/  
    **English definition:** someone you work with, especially in a profession or organization  
    **中文释义：** 同事；同行  
    **Example:** I asked a colleague to review the draft before it went out.  
    **中文：** 在草稿发出前，我请一位同事帮忙审阅。

15. **commercial** /kəˈmɜːrʃəl/  
    **English definition:** related to business or intended to make a profit; an advertisement on radio or television  
    **中文释义：** 商业的；营利性的；商业广告  
    **Example:** The agreement allows commercial use of the photographs.  
    **中文：** 这份协议允许将这些照片用于商业用途。

16. **commission** /kəˈmɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** to formally ask someone to create or do something; a group given a particular responsibility  
    **中文释义：** 委托创作或完成；委员会；佣金  
    **Example:** The museum commissioned a local artist to design the poster.  
    **中文：** 博物馆委托一位当地艺术家设计这张海报。

17. **comprise** /kəmˈpraɪz/  
    **English definition:** to consist of particular parts or members; to make up a whole  
    **中文释义：** 由……组成；构成  
    **Example:** The panel comprises experts from five different fields.  
    **中文：** 评审小组由五个不同领域的专家组成。

18. **concentrate** /ˈkɑːnsəntreɪt/  
    **English definition:** to give all your attention to something; to bring something together in a smaller area  
    **中文释义：** 集中注意力；聚集；浓缩  
    **Example:** Try to concentrate on the trend rather than one unusual result.  
    **中文：** 尽量关注整体趋势，而不是某一个异常结果。

19. **consumer** /kənˈsuːmər/  
    **English definition:** a person who buys or uses goods and services  
    **中文释义：** 消费者；用户  
    **Example:** Consumers can compare the total cost before choosing a plan.  
    **中文：** 消费者可以先比较总费用，再选择方案。

20. **controversial** /ˌkɑːntrəˈvɜːrʃəl/  
    **English definition:** causing strong public disagreement or debate  
    **中文释义：** 有争议的；引发争论的  
    **Example:** The proposed fee structure proved controversial among small businesses.  
    **中文：** 拟议中的收费结构在小型企业中引发了争议。

21. **convert** /kənˈvɜːrt/  
    **English definition:** to change something from one form, purpose, or system to another  
    **中文释义：** 转换；改造；转变  
    **Example:** The tool converts scanned pages into searchable text.  
    **中文：** 这个工具能把扫描页面转换成可搜索的文本。

22. **convince** /kənˈvɪns/  
    **English definition:** to persuade someone that something is true or that they should take action  
    **中文释义：** 使信服；说服  
    **Example:** The pilot data convinced the team to expand the trial.  
    **中文：** 试点数据说服团队扩大了试验范围。

23. **craft** /kræft/  
    **English definition:** to make or plan something carefully and skillfully  
    **中文释义：** 精心制作；巧妙拟定  
    **Example:** She crafted a concise response that addressed each concern.  
    **中文：** 她精心拟写了一份简明回复，逐一回应了各项顾虑。

24. **critical** /ˈkrɪtɪkəl/  
    **English definition:** extremely important; involving careful judgment or evaluation  
    **中文释义：** 至关重要的；批判性的；审慎评估的  
    **Example:** Accurate labeling is critical to safe storage.  
    **中文：** 准确的标记对安全存放至关重要。

25. **consideration** /kənˌsɪdəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** careful thought about something, or a factor taken into account  
    **中文释义：** 考虑；斟酌；需要考虑的因素  
    **Example:** Cost was an important consideration, but not the only one.  
    **中文：** 成本是一个重要的考虑因素，但不是唯一因素。
