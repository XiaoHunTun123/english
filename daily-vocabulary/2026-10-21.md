# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-21

以下 25 个词汇偏进阶但实用，适合阅读、工作和表达中拓展使用。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abide** /əˈbaɪd/  
   **English definition:** to accept and follow a rule, decision, or agreement, especially in “abide by”  
   **中文释义：** 遵守；依从  
   **Example:** Contractors are expected to abide by the site's safety rules.  
   **中文：** 承包商应遵守现场的安全规定。

2. **absorb** /əbˈzɔːrb/  
   **English definition:** to take in a liquid, substance, or information; to reduce the effect of an impact  
   **中文释义：** 吸收；理解并掌握；缓冲（冲击）  
   **Example:** The new material absorbs sound and reduces noise between rooms.  
   **中文：** 这种新材料能吸收声音，减少房间之间的噪声。

3. **adjustment** /əˈdʒʌstmənt/  
   **English definition:** a small change made to improve or correct something  
   **中文释义：** 调整；调节  
   **Example:** A slight adjustment to the schedule gave the team more time to test the update.  
   **中文：** 对日程稍作调整后，团队有了更多时间测试更新内容。

4. **adopt** /əˈdɑːpt/  
   **English definition:** to start using a particular method, idea, or policy; to legally take another person's child into one's family  
   **中文释义：** 采用；采纳；收养  
   **Example:** The department adopted a simpler process for approving routine requests.  
   **中文：** 部门采用了更简便的流程来审批常规申请。

5. **alter** /ˈɔːltər/  
   **English definition:** to change something, usually without replacing it completely  
   **中文释义：** 改变；修改；使有所不同  
   **Example:** The latest findings may alter our view of the project's risks.  
   **中文：** 最新发现可能会改变我们对项目风险的看法。

6. **amateur** /ˈæmətʃər/  
   **English definition:** a person who takes part in an activity for enjoyment rather than as a paid profession  
   **中文释义：** 业余爱好者；非职业人士  
   **Example:** The event welcomes amateur photographers as well as professionals.  
   **中文：** 这项活动欢迎业余摄影爱好者和专业人士参加。

7. **ambition** /æmˈbɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a strong desire to achieve something important or difficult  
   **中文释义：** 抱负；雄心；志向  
   **Example:** Her ambition is to make reliable public transport available to smaller towns.  
   **中文：** 她的抱负是让小城镇也能享有可靠的公共交通。

8. **archive** /ˈɑːrkaɪv/  
   **English definition:** a collection of historical records, or a place where such records are stored; to store records for long-term use  
   **中文释义：** 档案；档案库；归档  
   **Example:** Please archive the signed agreement with the project records.  
   **中文：** 请将签署后的协议归档到项目资料中。

9. **authentic** /ɔːˈθentɪk/  
   **English definition:** genuine and not copied, false, or misleading  
   **中文释义：** 真实的；可信的；非仿造的  
   **Example:** The museum verified that the document was authentic.  
   **中文：** 博物馆核实了这份文件是真品。

10. **behalf** /bɪˈhæf/  
    **English definition:** used in the phrase “on behalf of” to mean as a representative of someone or for their benefit  
    **中文释义：** 代表；为了……的利益  
    **Example:** I am writing on behalf of the residents who attended the meeting.  
    **中文：** 我谨代表出席会议的居民来信。

11. **clarify** /ˈklærəfaɪ/  
    **English definition:** to make an idea, statement, or situation easier to understand  
    **中文释义：** 澄清；阐明；讲清楚  
    **Example:** Could you clarify which costs are included in the estimate?  
    **中文：** 你能说明一下估算中包含哪些费用吗？

12. **commence** /kəˈmens/  
    **English definition:** to begin, especially in a formal or official context  
    **中文释义：** 开始；着手（较正式）  
    **Example:** Construction is scheduled to commence once the permit is issued.  
    **中文：** 施工计划在许可证签发后开始。

13. **comparable** /ˈkɑːmpərəbəl/  
    **English definition:** similar enough to another thing to be compared with it fairly  
    **中文释义：** 可相比较的；类似的  
    **Example:** The two studies used comparable methods and reached similar conclusions.  
    **中文：** 这两项研究采用了可比较的方法，并得出了相似的结论。

14. **complement** /ˈkɑːmpləmənt/  
    **English definition:** to add to something in a way that improves or completes it  
    **中文释义：** 补充；使更完善；与……相得益彰  
    **Example:** The training complements the practical experience employees gain on the job.  
    **中文：** 这项培训补充了员工在工作中获得的实践经验。

15. **consistent** /kənˈsɪstənt/  
    **English definition:** remaining the same in quality or behavior; in agreement with something else  
    **中文释义：** 一贯的；始终如一的；与……一致的  
    **Example:** Make sure the figures are consistent with the data in the appendix.  
    **中文：** 请确保这些数字与附录中的数据一致。

16. **constraint** /kənˈstreɪnt/  
    **English definition:** a limit or restriction that controls what can be done  
    **中文释义：** 限制；约束条件  
    **Example:** Limited storage space is the main constraint on how much stock we can keep.  
    **中文：** 存储空间有限是我们能保留多少库存的主要制约因素。

17. **contemporary** /kənˈtempəreri/  
    **English definition:** belonging to the present time; existing or happening during the same period  
    **中文释义：** 当代的；现代的；同时期的  
    **Example:** The exhibition explores how contemporary artists respond to urban change.  
    **中文：** 这场展览探讨当代艺术家如何回应城市变化。

18. **deficit** /ˈdefəsɪt/  
    **English definition:** the amount by which spending, needs, or losses are greater than available resources or income  
    **中文释义：** 赤字；亏空；不足额  
    **Example:** The organization reduced its budget deficit by postponing nonessential purchases.  
    **中文：** 该机构通过推迟非必要采购缩减了预算赤字。

19. **denote** /dɪˈnoʊt/  
    **English definition:** to indicate or represent a particular meaning, value, or thing  
    **中文释义：** 表示；指代；意味着  
    **Example:** In this chart, a dotted line denotes the projected trend.  
    **中文：** 在这张图表中，虚线表示预测趋势。

20. **devote** /dɪˈvoʊt/  
    **English definition:** to give a particular amount of time, effort, or attention to something  
    **中文释义：** 投入；献身于；致力于  
    **Example:** The team devoted two weeks to checking the translated materials.  
    **中文：** 团队花了两周时间核查翻译材料。

21. **dilemma** /dɪˈlemə/  
    **English definition:** a difficult situation in which a choice must be made between two or more options  
    **中文释义：** 困境；进退两难的局面  
    **Example:** The manager faced a dilemma: meet the deadline or complete another round of testing.  
    **中文：** 经理面临两难：是赶上截止日期，还是再完成一轮测试。

22. **displace** /dɪsˈpleɪs/  
    **English definition:** to move something from its usual position, or to take the place of a person or thing  
    **中文释义：** 移走；取代；使离开原位  
    **Example:** The updated workflow may displace several older manual steps.  
    **中文：** 更新后的工作流程可能会取代几个旧的手动步骤。

23. **emphasis** /ˈemfəsɪs/  
    **English definition:** special importance or attention given to a particular idea or feature  
    **中文释义：** 强调；重点；重视  
    **Example:** This year's course places greater emphasis on clear written communication.  
    **中文：** 今年的课程更加重视清晰的书面沟通。

24. **enable** /ɪˈneɪbəl/  
    **English definition:** to make it possible for someone to do something, or to give them the means to do it  
    **中文释义：** 使能够；使成为可能；赋予条件  
    **Example:** The shared dashboard enables teams to track changes in real time.  
    **中文：** 共享仪表板使各团队能够实时跟踪变化。

25. **entity** /ˈentəti/  
    **English definition:** something that exists as a distinct and independent unit, especially an organization  
    **中文释义：** 实体；独立存在的单位或组织  
    **Example:** Each legal entity must submit its own annual report.  
    **中文：** 每个法人实体都必须提交自己的年度报告。
