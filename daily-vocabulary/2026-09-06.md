# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-06

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **acerbic** /əˈsɜːrbɪk/ — sharp and critical in tone；语气尖锐且批判性强的；尖刻的
   - **Example:** Her acerbic review exposed several weaknesses in the proposal.
   - **中文：** 她尖刻的评论指出了这份提案中的几处弱点。

2. **amalgamate** /əˈmælɡəmeɪt/ — combine or merge into one；合并或融合成一个整体；合并
   - **Example:** The two departments will amalgamate next year.
   - **中文：** 这两个部门将于明年合并。

3. **antagonize** /ænˈtæɡənaɪz/ — make someone hostile or angry；使某人产生敌意或生气；激怒
   - **Example:** The careless remark antagonized several members of the audience.
   - **中文：** 这句不经意的话激怒了几位观众。

4. **appease** /əˈpiːz/ — calm someone by satisfying their demands；通过满足要求使某人平静；安抚
   - **Example:** The manager offered a refund to appease the dissatisfied customer.
   - **中文：** 经理提出退款，以安抚那位不满的顾客。

5. **avaricious** /ˌævəˈrɪʃəs/ — having an excessive desire for wealth；对财富有过度欲望的；贪婪的
   - **Example:** The avaricious investor ignored the environmental cost of the project.
   - **中文：** 那位贪婪的投资者忽视了项目带来的环境代价。

6. **belated** /bɪˈleɪtɪd/ — happening or arriving later than expected；发生或到达得比预期晚的；迟来的
   - **Example:** Please accept my belated congratulations on your promotion.
   - **中文：** 请接受我迟来的升职祝贺。

7. **concur** /kənˈkɜːr/ — agree with an opinion or decision；同意某个观点或决定；赞同
   - **Example:** Most experts concur that the policy needs further review.
   - **中文：** 大多数专家都赞同这项政策需要进一步审查。

8. **convivial** /kənˈvɪviəl/ — friendly, lively, and enjoyable；友好、热闹且令人愉快的；欢乐的
   - **Example:** The conference ended with a convivial dinner for all the participants.
   - **中文：** 会议以一场供所有参与者参加的欢乐晚宴结束。

9. **deplete** /dɪˈpliːt/ — use up a supply or resource；耗尽供应或资源；使枯竭
   - **Example:** Repeated delays have depleted the team’s patience.
   - **中文：** 反复的延误已经耗尽了团队的耐心。

10. **discrete** /dɪˈskriːt/ — separate and distinct；彼此分离且不同的；独立的
    - **Example:** The report divides the problem into three discrete stages.
    - **中文：** 报告把这个问题分成了三个独立阶段。

11. **dubious** /ˈduːbiəs/ — uncertain, doubtful, or not trustworthy；不确定、可疑或不可信的；可疑的
    - **Example:** The company made the claim despite having only dubious evidence.
    - **中文：** 尽管证据并不可靠，公司仍提出了这一说法。

12. **enigma** /ɪˈnɪɡmə/ — a person or thing that is difficult to understand；难以理解的人或事物；谜
    - **Example:** The sudden disappearance remains an enigma to investigators.
    - **中文：** 这场突然的失踪对调查人员来说仍是一个谜。

13. **flagrant** /ˈfleɪɡrənt/ — obviously offensive or wrong；明显令人反感或错误的；公然的
    - **Example:** The referee penalized the player for a flagrant violation of the rules.
    - **中文：** 裁判因这名球员公然违反规则而对其处罚。

14. **improvise** /ˈɪmprəvaɪz/ — create or perform without preparation；未经准备而创作或行动；即兴发挥
    - **Example:** When the microphone failed, she improvised a speech without notes.
    - **中文：** 麦克风出故障后，她没有讲稿也即兴完成了演讲。

15. **innate** /ɪˈneɪt/ — existing naturally from birth；从出生起自然存在的；天生的
    - **Example:** The child showed an innate ability to recognize musical patterns.
    - **中文：** 这个孩子表现出了识别音乐模式的天赋。

16. **insidious** /ɪnˈsɪdiəs/ — gradually harmful in a subtle way；以隐蔽方式逐渐造成伤害的；潜伏有害的
    - **Example:** The insidious spread of misinformation damaged public trust.
    - **中文：** 错误信息的隐蔽扩散损害了公众信任。

17. **meander** /miˈændər/ — wander without a clear direction or purpose；没有明确方向或目的地漫游；蜿蜒；漫无目的地走
    - **Example:** The conversation began to meander away from the main issue.
    - **中文：** 谈话开始偏离主要问题，变得漫无目的。

18. **obscure** /əbˈskjʊr/ — not well known or difficult to understand；鲜为人知或难以理解的；晦涩的
    - **Example:** The article refers to an obscure historical event.
    - **中文：** 这篇文章提到了一个鲜为人知的历史事件。

19. **omnipresent** /ˌɑːmnɪˈprezənt/ — present or seeming to be present everywhere；存在于各处或仿佛无处不在的；无处不在的
    - **Example:** Smartphones have become omnipresent in modern workplaces.
    - **中文：** 智能手机已经遍布现代工作场所。

20. **paradoxical** /ˌpærəˈdɑːksɪkəl/ — seemingly contradictory but possibly true；看似矛盾但可能真实的；悖论性的
    - **Example:** It is paradoxical that more choices can sometimes create less satisfaction.
    - **中文：** 更多选择有时反而带来更少满足感，这看似矛盾却确实可能发生。

21. **sagacious** /səˈɡeɪʃəs/ — having good judgment and practical wisdom；判断力强且富有实践智慧的；睿智的
    - **Example:** Her sagacious advice helped the company avoid a costly mistake.
    - **中文：** 她睿智的建议帮助公司避免了一次代价高昂的错误。

22. **substantiate** /səbˈstænʃieɪt/ — support a claim with evidence；用证据支持某项说法；证实
    - **Example:** The researchers could not substantiate the sensational report.
    - **中文：** 研究人员无法用证据证实那篇耸人听闻的报道。

23. **superfluous** /suːˈpɜːrfluəs/ — unnecessary or more than is needed；不必要或超过需要的；多余的
    - **Example:** The editor removed several superfluous paragraphs from the essay.
    - **中文：** 编辑从文章中删去了几段多余的内容。

24. **vigilant** /ˈvɪdʒɪlənt/ — watchful and alert to danger or problems；对危险或问题保持警惕的；警觉的
    - **Example:** Staff must remain vigilant for signs of unauthorized access.
    - **中文：** 员工必须保持警惕，留意未经授权访问的迹象。

25. **whimsical** /ˈwɪmzɪkəl/ — playfully unusual or imaginative；古怪有趣或富有想象力的；异想天开的
    - **Example:** The designer added whimsical details to the otherwise formal room.
    - **中文：** 设计师在原本正式的房间里加入了一些富有趣味的奇想细节。
