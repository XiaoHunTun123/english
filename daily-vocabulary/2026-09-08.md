# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-08

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **apprehension** /ˌæprɪˈhenʃən/ — anxiety or fear about something that may happen；对可能发生之事的焦虑或恐惧；忧虑
   - **Example:** She approached the interview with a mixture of confidence and apprehension.
   - **中文：** 她带着自信与忧虑交织的心情参加了面试。

2. **contrive** /kənˈtraɪv/ — manage to do something through careful planning；通过周密计划设法完成某事；设法
   - **Example:** They contrived to finish the project before the deadline.
   - **中文：** 他们设法在截止日期前完成了项目。

3. **decipher** /dɪˈsaɪfər/ — succeed in understanding something difficult to read or interpret；成功理解难以阅读或解释的事物；解读
   - **Example:** The analyst tried to decipher the handwritten notes.
   - **中文：** 分析师试图解读那些手写笔记。

4. **detrimental** /ˌdetrɪˈmentəl/ — causing harm or damage；造成伤害或损害的；有害的
   - **Example:** Excessive screen time can be detrimental to sleep quality.
   - **中文：** 过度使用屏幕可能会损害睡眠质量。

5. **elaborate** /ɪˈlæbərət/ — detailed and carefully developed；详细且经过精心设计的；详尽的
   - **Example:** The architect presented an elaborate plan for the public library.
   - **中文：** 建筑师为公共图书馆提出了一份详尽的方案。

6. **erudite** /ˈerudaɪt/ — highly educated and knowledgeable；受过良好教育且知识渊博的；博学的
   - **Example:** The erudite professor made a complex subject accessible to beginners.
   - **中文：** 那位博学的教授把复杂的主题讲得让初学者也能理解。

7. **fruition** /fruˈɪʃən/ — the point at which a plan or effort becomes successful；计划或努力取得成功的阶段；实现
   - **Example:** After years of research, the invention finally came to fruition.
   - **中文：** 经过多年的研究，这项发明终于实现了。

8. **gratuitous** /ɡrəˈtuːɪtəs/ — unnecessary, unwarranted, or unearned；不必要、无正当理由或无端的；多余的；无端的
   - **Example:** The report included several gratuitous personal attacks.
   - **中文：** 这份报告包含了几处毫无必要的人身攻击。

9. **indelible** /ɪnˈdeləbəl/ — impossible to remove, erase, or forget；无法移除、擦除或忘记的；不可磨灭的
   - **Example:** The experience left an indelible impression on her.
   - **中文：** 这段经历给她留下了不可磨灭的印象。

10. **inept** /ɪˈnept/ — lacking skill or ability；缺乏技能或能力的；笨拙的；无能的
    - **Example:** The inept response only made the public’s concerns worse.
    - **中文：** 这番无能的回应反而加重了公众的担忧。

11. **integral** /ˈɪntɪɡrəl/ — essential to making a whole complete；对整体完整不可或缺的；必需的
    - **Example:** Trust is integral to any successful partnership.
    - **中文：** 信任是任何成功合作关系不可或缺的一部分。

12. **laudable** /ˈlɔːdəbəl/ — deserving praise or approval；值得赞扬或认可的；值得称道的
    - **Example:** The organization’s efforts to improve access to education are laudable.
    - **中文：** 该组织为改善教育机会所作的努力值得称道。

13. **paradigm** /ˈpærədaɪm/ — a model or pattern that shapes how something is understood；影响人们理解某事物方式的模型或范式；范式
    - **Example:** The discovery challenged the prevailing paradigm in the field.
    - **中文：** 这一发现挑战了该领域占主导地位的范式。

14. **redundant** /rɪˈdʌndənt/ — unnecessary because it repeats or adds nothing；因重复或没有增加价值而不必要的；多余的
    - **Example:** The editor removed redundant sentences from the final draft.
    - **中文：** 编辑从最终稿中删去了多余的句子。

15. **sophisticated** /səˈfɪstɪkeɪtɪd/ — advanced, complex, or highly developed；先进、复杂或高度发展的；精密的；老练的
    - **Example:** The attackers used a sophisticated method to bypass the security system.
    - **中文：** 攻击者使用了一种精密的方法绕过安全系统。

16. **substantive** /ˈsʌbstəntɪv/ — important, meaningful, and based on real content；重要、有实质内容且基于事实的；实质性的
    - **Example:** The meeting produced substantive progress on the agreement.
    - **中文：** 这次会议在协议方面取得了实质性进展。

17. **surmise** /sərˈmaɪz/ — form an opinion without enough direct evidence；在缺乏充分直接证据时形成看法；推测
    - **Example:** From the silence, we could only surmise that the plan had failed.
    - **中文：** 从这片沉默中，我们只能推测计划失败了。

18. **unfounded** /ʌnˈfaʊndɪd/ — not based on facts or evidence；没有事实或证据依据的；无根据的
    - **Example:** The allegations were investigated and found to be unfounded.
    - **中文：** 这些指控经过调查后被认定是无根据的。

19. **unilateral** /ˌjuːnɪˈlætərəl/ — done or decided by one side alone；由单方面采取或决定的；单方面的
    - **Example:** The company made a unilateral decision to change the contract.
    - **中文：** 公司单方面决定修改合同。

20. **unravel** /ʌnˈrævəl/ — solve a complicated problem or separate tangled parts；解决复杂问题或解开缠结部分；解开；理清
    - **Example:** Investigators worked for months to unravel the financial scheme.
    - **中文：** 调查人员花了数月时间理清这起金融骗局。

21. **warrant** /ˈwɔːrənt/ — justify an action or make it necessary；使某一行动合理或必要；证明有必要
    - **Example:** The seriousness of the breach may warrant further investigation.
    - **中文：** 这次违规的严重性可能有必要进行进一步调查。

22. **futile** /ˈfjuːtaɪl/ — incapable of producing a useful result；无法产生有用结果的；徒劳的
    - **Example:** It would be futile to argue with someone who refuses to consider the facts.
    - **中文：** 和一个拒绝考虑事实的人争论是徒劳的。

23. **harbinger** /ˈhɑːrbɪndʒər/ — a sign that something else is likely to happen；预示某事可能发生的迹象；先兆
    - **Example:** The sudden drop in demand was a harbinger of a wider economic slowdown.
    - **中文：** 需求突然下降是经济进一步放缓的先兆。

24. **retaliate** /rɪˈtælieɪt/ — harm someone in return for an injury or attack；因受到伤害或攻击而反过来伤害对方；报复
    - **Example:** The company warned that it would not retaliate against employees who reported misconduct.
    - **中文：** 公司警告说，不会报复举报不当行为的员工。

25. **requisite** /ˈrekwəzɪt/ — required or necessary for a particular purpose；为特定目的所需或必需的；必需的
    - **Example:** Applicants must have the requisite experience before applying for the role.
    - **中文：** 申请者必须具备所需经验后才能申请这个职位。
