# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-25

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accost** /əˈkɔːst/  
   **English definition:** to approach someone and speak to them, often in a forceful or unwelcome way  
   **中文释义：** 上前搭话；纠缠  
   **Example:** A reporter accosted the actor outside the theater and asked for a comment.  
   **中文：** 一名记者在剧院外拦住那位演员，请他发表评论。

2. **agnostic** /æɡˈnɑːstɪk/  
   **English definition:** uncertain about something or not committed to a particular view  
   **中文释义：** 持不可知论的；不预设立场的  
   **Example:** The new software is platform-agnostic and can run on several operating systems.  
   **中文：** 这款新软件不受平台限制，可以在多个操作系统上运行。

3. **benign** /bɪˈnaɪn/  
   **English definition:** gentle and harmless; not likely to cause damage or danger  
   **中文释义：** 温和的；无害的；良性的  
   **Example:** The doctor confirmed that the growth was benign.  
   **中文：** 医生确认这个增生是良性的。

4. **ebullient** /ɪˈbʊliənt/  
   **English definition:** cheerful, energetic, and full of enthusiasm  
   **中文释义：** 热情洋溢的；精力充沛的  
   **Example:** The ebullient presenter quickly energized the tired audience.  
   **中文：** 热情洋溢的主持人很快让疲惫的观众振奋起来。

5. **exude** /ɪɡˈzuːd/  
   **English definition:** to show a strong quality or feeling, or to release a substance gradually  
   **中文释义：** 流露；散发；渗出  
   **Example:** The small studio exuded a calm, creative atmosphere.  
   **中文：** 这间小工作室散发出平静而富有创意的氛围。

6. **fervid** /ˈfɜːrvɪd/  
   **English definition:** showing very strong feelings or enthusiasm  
   **中文释义：** 热烈的；充满激情的  
   **Example:** The proposal attracted fervid support from environmental groups.  
   **中文：** 这项提案得到了环保组织的热烈支持。

7. **forbear** /fɔːrˈber/  
   **English definition:** to patiently refrain from doing something  
   **中文释义：** 克制；忍住不做  
   **Example:** She decided to forbear from responding until she had all the facts.  
   **中文：** 她决定先克制住不回应，直到掌握全部事实。

8. **manifest** /ˈmænɪfest/  
   **English definition:** clear or obvious; to show a quality or feeling through actions  
   **中文释义：** 明显的；显示；表现  
   **Example:** His frustration was manifest in the sharp change in his tone.  
   **中文：** 他语气的明显变化表露出他的沮丧。

9. **notwithstanding** /ˌnɑːtwɪθˈstændɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** despite something; nevertheless  
   **中文释义：** 尽管；不过  
   **Example:** Notwithstanding the limited budget, the team delivered a reliable product.  
   **中文：** 尽管预算有限，团队仍交付了一款可靠的产品。

10. **precocious** /prɪˈkoʊʃəs/  
    **English definition:** having abilities or behavior that are unusually advanced for one’s age  
    **中文释义：** 早熟的；过早展现才能的  
    **Example:** The precocious child was reading advanced science books by the age of eight.  
    **中文：** 这个早熟的孩子八岁时就开始阅读高深的科学书籍。

11. **pugnacious** /pʌɡˈneɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** eager or ready to argue or fight  
    **中文释义：** 好斗的；爱争辩的  
    **Example:** His pugnacious style made cooperation with neighboring teams difficult.  
    **中文：** 他好斗的风格使得与邻近团队合作变得困难。

12. **rancorous** /ˈræŋkərəs/  
    **English definition:** characterized by deep and lasting resentment  
    **中文释义：** 充满怨恨的；敌意深重的  
    **Example:** The dispute led to a rancorous exchange between the former partners.  
    **中文：** 这场争端导致前合作伙伴之间发生了充满怨恨的争吵。

13. **replete** /rɪˈpliːt/  
    **English definition:** filled or abundantly supplied with something  
    **中文释义：** 充满的；富含的  
    **Example:** The report is replete with practical recommendations for small businesses.  
    **中文：** 这份报告充满了针对小企业的实用建议。

14. **rife** /raɪf/  
    **English definition:** very common or widespread, especially something harmful or unwanted  
    **中文释义：** 普遍存在的；充斥的  
    **Example:** The industry was rife with rumors about an impending merger.  
    **中文：** 这个行业充斥着即将合并的传闻。

15. **soporific** /ˌsɑːpəˈrɪfɪk/  
    **English definition:** causing sleepiness or a lack of alertness  
    **中文释义：** 催眠的；令人昏昏欲睡的  
    **Example:** The lecture became soporific because it relied on repetitive slides.  
    **中文：** 这场讲座由于反复使用相同的幻灯片，变得令人昏昏欲睡。

16. **staid** /steɪd/  
    **English definition:** serious, calm, and conventional, sometimes to the point of being dull  
    **中文释义：** 古板的；稳重而传统的  
    **Example:** The magazine has a staid reputation but recently began covering emerging artists.  
    **中文：** 这本杂志一向给人古板的印象，但最近开始报道新兴艺术家。

17. **stymie** /ˈstaɪmi/  
    **English definition:** to prevent someone from making progress or achieving a goal  
    **中文释义：** 阻碍；妨碍  
    **Example:** A shortage of skilled workers could stymie the company’s expansion.  
    **中文：** 熟练工人短缺可能会阻碍公司的扩张。

18. **transgress** /trænˈsɡres/  
    **English definition:** to go beyond a moral, legal, or social limit  
    **中文释义：** 违反；越过界限  
    **Example:** The report found that the contractor had transgressed several safety regulations.  
    **中文：** 报告发现承包商违反了多项安全规定。

19. **venerable** /ˈvenərəbəl/  
    **English definition:** respected because of great age, wisdom, or historical importance  
    **中文释义：** 受尊敬的；古老而重要的  
    **Example:** The university is housed in a venerable building dating back centuries.  
    **中文：** 这所大学坐落在一座有数百年历史、备受尊敬的建筑中。

20. **vexatious** /vekˈseɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** causing annoyance, worry, or difficulty, often repeatedly  
    **中文释义：** 令人烦恼的；棘手的  
    **Example:** Resolving the vexatious billing error took several weeks.  
    **中文：** 解决这个令人烦恼的账单错误花了好几周。

21. **vindictive** /vɪnˈdɪktɪv/  
    **English definition:** having a strong desire to hurt someone in return for a perceived wrong  
    **中文释义：** 报复性的；怀恨报复的  
    **Example:** The review criticized the decision without becoming vindictive.  
    **中文：** 这篇评论批评了该决定，但没有变得怀恨报复。

22. **virulent** /ˈvɪrələnt/  
    **English definition:** extremely harmful, poisonous, or aggressive  
    **中文释义：** 剧毒的；恶性的；极具攻击性的  
    **Example:** The researchers are monitoring a virulent strain of the virus.  
    **中文：** 研究人员正在监测一种高致病性的病毒株。

23. **volatile** /ˈvɑːlətəl/  
    **English definition:** likely to change suddenly and unpredictably  
    **中文释义：** 易变的；不稳定的  
    **Example:** Investors remained cautious because the market was highly volatile.  
    **中文：** 由于市场非常不稳定，投资者仍保持谨慎。

24. **wanton** /ˈwɑːntən/  
    **English definition:** deliberate and without justification, especially when causing harm  
    **中文释义：** 恣意的；无理的；肆意造成伤害的  
    **Example:** The court condemned the wanton destruction of public property.  
    **中文：** 法院谴责了对公共财产的肆意破坏。

25. **whet** /wet/  
    **English definition:** to increase interest, desire, or appetite  
    **中文释义：** 激发兴趣；增进食欲  
    **Example:** The short preview whetted viewers’ appetite for the full documentary.  
    **中文：** 这段简短预告激发了观众观看完整纪录片的兴趣。
