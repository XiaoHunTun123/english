# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-18

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abrupt** /əˈbrʌpt/  
   **English definition:** sudden and unexpected, often in a way that seems unfriendly or unprepared  
   **中文释义：** 突然的；唐突的；生硬的  
   **Example:** The abrupt change in temperature caught the hikers off guard.  
   **中文：** 气温突然变化，让徒步者措手不及。

2. **accumulate** /əˈkjuːmjəleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to gradually collect or increase over a period of time  
   **中文释义：** 积累；逐渐增加  
   **Example:** Dust accumulates quickly on equipment that is left unused.  
   **中文：** 长期闲置的设备上很快会积满灰尘。

3. **adequate** /ˈædɪkwət/  
   **English definition:** enough or satisfactory for a particular purpose or need  
   **中文释义：** 足够的；合格的；适当的  
   **Example:** The backup generator provides adequate power for essential equipment.  
   **中文：** 备用发电机能为关键设备提供足够的电力。

4. **appoint** /əˈpɔɪnt/  
   **English definition:** to officially choose someone for a job, position, or responsibility  
   **中文释义：** 任命；指派  
   **Example:** The board appointed a new chairperson in May.  
   **中文：** 董事会在五月任命了一位新主席。

5. **assertion** /əˈsɜːrʃən/  
   **English definition:** a confident statement that something is true, though it may still need proof  
   **中文释义：** 断言；主张；明确的说法  
   **Example:** The report challenges the assertion that remote work reduces productivity.  
   **中文：** 这份报告质疑了“远程办公会降低生产力”这一说法。

6. **assumption** /əˈsʌmpʃən/  
   **English definition:** something accepted as true without being proved  
   **中文释义：** 假设；想当然的看法  
   **Example:** Our estimate rests on the assumption that costs will remain stable.  
   **中文：** 我们的估算建立在成本将保持稳定这一假设之上。

7. **attentive** /əˈtentɪv/  
   **English definition:** paying close attention to someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 留心的；专注的；体贴周到的  
   **Example:** An attentive reviewer noticed that two figures had been transposed.  
   **中文：** 一位仔细的审阅者发现有两个数字被调换了位置。

8. **autonomous** /ɔːˈtɑːnəməs/  
   **English definition:** able to act independently or govern itself  
   **中文释义：** 自主的；自治的；独立运行的  
   **Example:** The lab developed an autonomous cart that can navigate the corridors.  
   **中文：** 实验室研发了一辆能够在走廊中自主导航的小车。

9. **beneficiary** /ˌbenəˈfɪʃieri/  
   **English definition:** a person or organization that receives an advantage, payment, or benefit  
   **中文释义：** 受益人；受益机构  
   **Example:** Each scholarship beneficiary received a letter explaining the renewal requirements.  
   **中文：** 每位奖学金受益人都收到了一封说明续领要求的信。

10. **bias** /ˈbaɪəs/  
   **English definition:** an unfair preference or tendency that affects judgment; a systematic distortion  
   **中文释义：** 偏见；偏向；（数据或判断中的）偏差  
   **Example:** The team used blind scoring to reduce bias in the selection process.  
   **中文：** 团队采用盲评来减少遴选过程中的偏见。

11. **bypass** /ˈbaɪpæs/  
   **English definition:** to go around something or avoid a person, rule, or process  
   **中文释义：** 绕过；避开；绕行  
   **Example:** The new connector lets traffic bypass the town center.  
   **中文：** 新建的连接道路让车辆可以绕过镇中心。

12. **commitment** /kəˈmɪtmənt/  
   **English definition:** a promise or obligation to do something, or dedication to a cause or activity  
   **中文释义：** 承诺；义务；投入  
   **Example:** The supplier made a written commitment to deliver the parts by June.  
   **中文：** 供应商书面承诺在六月前交付零件。

13. **criteria** /kraɪˈtɪriə/  
   **English definition:** standards or principles used to judge or decide something; plural of criterion  
   **中文释义：** 标准；准则；评判依据（criterion 的复数）  
   **Example:** Price and durability were the main criteria in our comparison.  
   **中文：** 价格和耐用性是我们比较时的主要标准。

14. **deprive** /dɪˈpraɪv/  
   **English definition:** to prevent someone from having or using something important  
   **中文释义：** 剥夺；使失去  
   **Example:** The outage deprived residents of reliable access to clean water.  
   **中文：** 停电使居民无法稳定获得干净的用水。

15. **eligible** /ˈelɪdʒəbəl/  
   **English definition:** meeting the conditions required to receive or do something  
   **中文释义：** 符合资格的；具备条件的  
   **Example:** Applicants are eligible for reimbursement after completing the training.  
   **中文：** 申请者完成培训后即可符合报销资格。

16. **equivalent** /ɪˈkwɪvələnt/  
   **English definition:** equal in value, meaning, amount, or function to something else  
   **中文释义：** 等同的；相当的；等值物  
   **Example:** One cup of cooked rice is roughly equivalent to two servings.  
   **中文：** 一杯熟米饭大约相当于两份的量。

17. **extract** /ɪkˈstrækt/  
   **English definition:** to remove or obtain something from a larger whole or source  
   **中文释义：** 提取；摘录；取出  
   **Example:** The software can extract key dates from scanned invoices.  
   **中文：** 该软件能够从扫描的发票中提取关键日期。

18. **moderate** /ˈmɑːdərət/  
   **English definition:** neither extreme nor excessive; reasonable in amount or degree  
   **中文释义：** 适度的；温和的；不过分的  
   **Example:** A moderate increase in workload can be managed without hiring new staff.  
   **中文：** 工作量适度增加时，无需招聘新员工也能应对。

19. **occupy** /ˈɑːkjəpaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to take up space or time, or to hold a position or place  
   **中文释义：** 占据；占用；担任  
   **Example:** The workshop will occupy the first half of the afternoon.  
   **中文：** 工作坊将占用下午的前半段时间。

20. **proportion** /prəˈpɔːrʃən/  
   **English definition:** a part or share considered in relation to the whole  
   **中文释义：** 比例；部分；份额  
   **Example:** A large proportion of survey respondents preferred the shorter form.  
   **中文：** 很大一部分问卷受访者更喜欢较短的表格。

21. **resemble** /rɪˈzembəl/  
   **English definition:** to look like or be similar to someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 像；与……相似  
   **Example:** The new layout resembles the older version but is easier to navigate.  
   **中文：** 新版布局与旧版相似，但更容易操作。

22. **abandon** /əˈbændən/  
   **English definition:** to leave something permanently or stop supporting or doing it  
   **中文释义：** 放弃；抛弃；中止  
   **Example:** They abandoned the original route after the bridge inspection.  
   **中文：** 检查桥梁后，他们放弃了原定路线。

23. **abolish** /əˈbɑːlɪʃ/  
   **English definition:** to officially end a law, system, or practice  
   **中文释义：** 废除；废止  
   **Example:** The city voted to abolish the outdated permit requirement.  
   **中文：** 市议会投票决定废除过时的许可证要求。

24. **abound** /əˈbaʊnd/  
   **English definition:** to exist in large numbers or amounts  
   **中文释义：** 大量存在；充满；富于  
   **Example:** Examples of this pattern abound in historical records.  
   **中文：** 历史记录中大量存在这种模式的例子。

25. **abstract** /ˈæbstrækt/  
   **English definition:** based on ideas rather than physical objects; not concrete or specific  
   **中文释义：** 抽象的；理论性的；非具体的  
   **Example:** The course begins with an abstract theory and then moves to practical cases.  
   **中文：** 课程先从抽象理论讲起，再转向实际案例。
