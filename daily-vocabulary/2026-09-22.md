# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-22

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **adamant** /ˈædəmənt/  
   **English definition:** refusing to change one’s mind or give way  
   **中文释义：** 坚决的；坚定不移的  
   **Example:** The editor was adamant that the article needed stronger evidence.  
   **中文：** 编辑坚持认为，这篇文章需要更有力的证据。

2. **allay** /əˈleɪ/  
   **English definition:** to reduce fear, worry, or a problem  
   **中文释义：** 减轻；缓和，尤指恐惧或担忧  
   **Example:** The spokesperson held a briefing to allay public concerns.  
   **中文：** 发言人举行了简报会，以缓解公众的担忧。

3. **ancillary** /ˈænsəleri/  
   **English definition:** providing necessary support to a main activity or service  
   **中文释义：** 辅助的；附属的  
   **Example:** The clinic offers ancillary services such as counseling and rehabilitation.  
   **中文：** 这家诊所提供咨询和康复等辅助服务。

4. **arbitrate** /ˈɑːrbɪtreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to settle a disagreement by acting as an independent judge  
   **中文释义：** 仲裁；裁决争议  
   **Example:** An independent panel was asked to arbitrate the dispute between the two companies.  
   **中文：** 一个独立小组受邀仲裁两家公司之间的争端。

5. **assail** /əˈseɪl/  
   **English definition:** to attack someone or something strongly, in words or actions  
   **中文释义：** 猛烈抨击；攻击  
   **Example:** Critics assailed the policy for placing too much pressure on small businesses.  
   **中文：** 批评者猛烈抨击这项政策，认为它给小企业施加了过大压力。

6. **attenuate** /əˈtenjuˌeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to make something weaker, less severe, or less effective  
   **中文释义：** 减弱；削弱  
   **Example:** Trees along the road help attenuate traffic noise.  
   **中文：** 道路旁的树木有助于减弱交通噪声。

7. **callous** /ˈkæləs/  
   **English definition:** showing no sympathy or concern for other people’s suffering  
   **中文释义：** 冷酷无情的；麻木的  
   **Example:** His callous response upset the families affected by the accident.  
   **中文：** 他冷酷的回应令受事故影响的家属感到难过。

8. **chicanery** /ʃɪˈkeɪnəri/  
   **English definition:** the use of dishonest methods or clever tricks to achieve something  
   **中文释义：** 欺骗手段；诡计  
   **Example:** The audit uncovered a pattern of financial chicanery.  
   **中文：** 审计揭露了一系列财务欺诈手段。

9. **compunction** /kəmˈpʌŋkʃən/  
   **English definition:** a feeling of guilt or regret about doing something wrong  
   **中文释义：** 内疚；悔恨  
   **Example:** She felt no compunction about reporting the safety violation.  
   **中文：** 她举报那起安全违规行为时没有丝毫内疚。

10. **demagogue** /ˈdeməɡɑːɡ/  
    **English definition:** a political leader who gains support by appealing to emotions and prejudice  
    **中文释义：** 蛊惑民众的政治煽动者  
    **Example:** The candidate was criticized as a demagogue for exploiting public fears.  
    **中文：** 这名候选人因利用公众恐惧而被批评为政治煽动者。

11. **denouement** /deɪˈnuːmɑːn/  
    **English definition:** the final part of a story in which the main events are resolved  
    **中文释义：** 结局；收场  
    **Example:** The novel’s denouement reveals who was responsible for the betrayal.  
    **中文：** 小说的结局揭示了谁应对这场背叛负责。

12. **droll** /droʊl/  
    **English definition:** amusing in an unusual, understated, or slightly odd way  
    **中文释义：** 古怪逗趣的；冷幽默的  
    **Example:** His droll observations made the long meeting more bearable.  
    **中文：** 他古怪而有趣的观察让漫长的会议变得更容易忍受。

13. **eschew** /ɪˈʃuː/  
    **English definition:** to deliberately avoid something  
    **中文释义：** 避开；有意不采用  
    **Example:** The report eschews technical jargon so that general readers can follow it.  
    **中文：** 这份报告避免使用专业术语，以便普通读者理解。

14. **exculpate** /ˈekskʌlpeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to show or declare that someone is not responsible for a wrongdoing  
    **中文释义：** 证明无罪；为……开脱  
    **Example:** New evidence may exculpate the employee who was wrongly accused.  
    **中文：** 新证据可能证明那名被错误指控的员工无罪。

15. **extant** /ˈekstənt/  
    **English definition:** still existing, especially after the disappearance of similar things  
    **中文释义：** 现存的；尚存的  
    **Example:** Only three extant copies of the original manuscript are known.  
    **中文：** 已知现存的原始手稿只有三份。

16. **facetious** /fəˈsiːʃəs/  
    **English definition:** treating a serious subject with inappropriate humor  
    **中文释义：** 轻浮的；不恰当地开玩笑的  
    **Example:** His facetious comment was poorly received during the formal meeting.  
    **中文：** 他在正式会议上的轻浮玩笑没有得到好的回应。

17. **glib** /ɡlɪb/  
    **English definition:** fluent and confident but lacking careful thought or sincerity  
    **中文释义：** 油嘴滑舌的；轻率流利的  
    **Example:** The interviewer wanted a detailed plan rather than a glib promise.  
    **中文：** 面试官想要的是详细计划，而不是轻率的口头承诺。

18. **indefatigable** /ˌɪndɪˈfætɪɡəbəl/  
    **English definition:** having great energy and never seeming to get tired  
    **中文释义：** 不知疲倦的；精力充沛的  
    **Example:** Her indefatigable efforts kept the community project moving forward.  
    **中文：** 她不知疲倦的努力推动了社区项目不断前进。

19. **innocuous** /ɪˈnɑːkjuəs/  
    **English definition:** harmless and unlikely to cause offense or damage  
    **中文释义：** 无害的；不会冒犯人的  
    **Example:** What appeared to be an innocuous remark sparked a lengthy debate.  
    **中文：** 一句看似无害的话引发了一场长时间的辩论。

20. **intimation** /ˌɪntɪˈmeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** an indirect suggestion or sign that something is true or likely  
    **中文释义：** 暗示；提示  
    **Example:** The announcement was the first intimation that the company might relocate.  
    **中文：** 这则公告首次暗示了公司可能搬迁。

21. **invective** /ɪnˈvektɪv/  
    **English definition:** insulting or abusive language used to attack someone  
    **中文释义：** 谩骂；攻击性言辞  
    **Example:** The debate descended into personal invective instead of reasoned discussion.  
    **中文：** 辩论变成了人身谩骂，而不是理性讨论。

22. **lacuna** /ləˈkuːnə/  
    **English definition:** a missing part or gap, especially in a document or argument  
    **中文释义：** 空白；缺漏  
    **Example:** The researchers identified a significant lacuna in the historical record.  
    **中文：** 研究人员发现历史记录中存在一个重要缺漏。

23. **officious** /əˈfɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** too eager to give advice or help when it is not wanted  
    **中文释义：** 好管闲事的；自作主张提供帮助的  
    **Example:** An officious assistant kept interrupting the meeting with irrelevant suggestions.  
    **中文：** 一名爱管闲事的助理不断用无关建议打断会议。

24. **opprobrium** /əˈproʊbriəm/  
    **English definition:** harsh criticism or strong public disapproval  
    **中文释义：** 严厉谴责；公愤  
    **Example:** The decision to cut essential services met with widespread opprobrium.  
    **中文：** 削减基本服务的决定遭到了广泛的严厉谴责。

25. **tantamount** /ˈtæntəmaʊnt/  
    **English definition:** having the same meaning or effect as something else  
    **中文释义：** 等同于；实质上相当于  
    **Example:** Refusing to share the data would be tantamount to admitting that the results were unreliable.  
    **中文：** 拒绝共享数据实质上等于承认结果不可靠。
