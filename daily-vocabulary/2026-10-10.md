# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-10

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **albeit** /ɔːlˈbiːɪt/  
   **English definition:** although; used to introduce a fact that makes a previous statement less straightforward  
   **中文释义：** 尽管；虽然  
   **Example:** The plan is feasible, albeit more expensive than we expected.  
   **中文：** 这个方案可行，尽管成本比我们预想的更高。

2. **amend** /əˈmend/  
   **English definition:** to change a document, law, or statement in order to improve or correct it  
   **中文释义：** 修订；修改（文件、法律或陈述）  
   **Example:** Our team amended the proposal after reviewing the client’s feedback.  
   **中文：** 团队审阅客户的反馈后修订了提案。

3. **accountable** /əˈkaʊntəbəl/  
   **English definition:** responsible for an action or decision and expected to explain or justify it  
   **中文释义：** 负有责任的；应作出解释的  
   **Example:** Managers are accountable for how they allocate the team’s budget.  
   **中文：** 管理者需要为团队预算的分配方式负责。

4. **cohesive** /koʊˈhiːsɪv/  
   **English definition:** forming a united, well-connected whole  
   **中文释义：** 有凝聚力的；连贯统一的  
   **Example:** A shared goal helped turn the separate departments into a cohesive team.  
   **中文：** 共同的目标帮助原本各自独立的部门组成了一支有凝聚力的团队。

5. **convene** /kənˈviːn/  
   **English definition:** to arrange for people to come together for a meeting or formal discussion  
   **中文释义：** 召集；召开（会议）  
   **Example:** The board will convene next week to discuss the merger.  
   **中文：** 董事会将于下周召开会议讨论合并事宜。

6. **counterpart** /ˈkaʊntərpɑːrt/  
   **English definition:** a person or thing with a corresponding role or function in another group or system  
   **中文释义：** 职位或作用相当的人；对应物  
   **Example:** I discussed the schedule with my counterpart at the partner company.  
   **中文：** 我与合作公司的对接负责人讨论了日程安排。

7. **deem** /diːm/  
   **English definition:** to consider or judge someone or something to be a particular way  
   **中文释义：** 认为；视为（常用于正式语境）  
   **Example:** The committee deemed the evidence insufficient to support the claim.  
   **中文：** 委员会认为这些证据不足以支持该主张。

8. **disrupt** /dɪsˈrʌpt/  
   **English definition:** to interrupt the normal progress or operation of something  
   **中文释义：** 扰乱；中断；使无法正常运行  
   **Example:** A brief power outage disrupted service across the building.  
   **中文：** 短暂的停电导致整栋楼的服务中断。

9. **falter** /ˈfɔːltər/  
   **English definition:** to become weaker or less certain; to hesitate or lose momentum  
   **中文释义：** 衰退；动摇；踌躇  
   **Example:** The launch began smoothly, but sales faltered after the first month.  
   **中文：** 产品上市之初进展顺利，但一个月后销量开始下滑。

10. **forthcoming** /ˌfɔːrθˈkʌmɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** willing to provide information; also, expected or about to happen  
   **中文释义：** 坦诚提供信息的；即将到来的  
   **Example:** The manager was forthcoming about the risks and answered every question.  
   **中文：** 经理坦诚说明了风险，并回答了所有问题。

11. **incur** /ɪnˈkɜːr/  
   **English definition:** to become subject to an expense, debt, or other unwelcome consequence  
   **中文释义：** 招致；承担（费用、债务或不利后果）  
   **Example:** You may incur extra fees by paying the invoice late.  
   **中文：** 如果逾期支付发票，你可能会产生额外费用。

12. **leverage** /ˈlevərɪdʒ/  
   **English definition:** to use an advantage, resource, or skill to achieve a better result  
   **中文释义：** 善用；借助（优势、资源或技能）  
   **Example:** We can leverage customer feedback to improve the next version.  
   **中文：** 我们可以利用客户反馈来改进下一个版本。

13. **misgiving** /mɪsˈɡɪvɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** a feeling of doubt or concern about whether something is right or wise  
   **中文释义：** 疑虑；顾虑  
   **Example:** She had misgivings about signing a contract without reading the terms.  
   **中文：** 她对不看条款就签合同感到顾虑。

14. **prone** /proʊn/  
   **English definition:** likely to experience or do something, especially something undesirable  
   **中文释义：** 易于……的；有……倾向的  
   **Example:** This coastal road is prone to flooding during winter storms.  
   **中文：** 这条沿海道路在冬季风暴期间容易发生洪水。

15. **provisional** /prəˈvɪʒənəl/  
   **English definition:** arranged for the present but not yet final or officially confirmed  
   **中文释义：** 暂定的；临时的；有待确认的  
   **Example:** They reached a provisional agreement, subject to approval from both boards.  
   **中文：** 双方达成了一项暂定协议，但仍需两家董事会批准。

16. **reassess** /ˌriːəˈses/  
   **English definition:** to examine or evaluate something again, often in light of new information  
   **中文释义：** 重新评估；重新审视  
   **Example:** After the first trial, the engineers reassessed the design.  
   **中文：** 首轮测试后，工程师重新评估了设计方案。

17. **reimburse** /ˌriːɪmˈbɜːrs/  
   **English definition:** to repay someone for money they have spent  
   **中文释义：** 报销；偿还（已支出的费用）  
   **Example:** The company will reimburse employees for approved travel expenses.  
   **中文：** 公司将为员工报销经批准的差旅费用。

18. **refrain** /rɪˈfreɪn/  
   **English definition:** to deliberately stop yourself from doing something  
   **中文释义：** 克制；避免（做某事）  
   **Example:** Please refrain from sharing confidential files outside the team.  
   **中文：** 请不要将机密文件分享给团队以外的人。

19. **remedy** /ˈremədi/  
   **English definition:** a way of correcting or improving a problem; to correct a problem  
   **中文释义：** 补救办法；纠正；解决  
   **Example:** The update is intended to remedy a security flaw in the app.  
   **中文：** 这次更新旨在修复应用中的一个安全漏洞。

20. **revoke** /rɪˈvoʊk/  
   **English definition:** to officially cancel or withdraw a permission, decision, or document  
   **中文释义：** 撤销；废止（许可、决定或文件）  
   **Example:** The agency can revoke the permit if safety rules are ignored.  
   **中文：** 如果安全规定遭到无视，该机构可以撤销许可证。

21. **tactful** /ˈtæktfəl/  
   **English definition:** careful about what you say or do so that you do not upset or offend others  
   **中文释义：** 圆通得体的；注意分寸的  
   **Example:** He gave tactful feedback that identified the issue without embarrassing her.  
   **中文：** 他给出了得体的反馈，指出问题的同时没有让她难堪。

22. **underlie** /ˌʌndərˈlaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to form the basis of something or be a fundamental cause of it  
   **中文释义：** 构成……的基础；是……的根本原因  
   **Example:** Trust and transparency underlie a strong working relationship.  
   **中文：** 信任与透明是稳固工作关系的基础。

23. **undergo** /ˌʌndərˈɡoʊ/  
   **English definition:** to experience or be subjected to a process, change, or difficult event  
   **中文释义：** 经历；接受（过程、变化或考验）  
   **Example:** The old bridge will undergo repairs throughout the summer.  
   **中文：** 这座旧桥整个夏天都将进行维修。

24. **outweigh** /ˌaʊtˈweɪ/  
   **English definition:** to be more important or valuable than something else  
   **中文释义：** 比……更重要；超过……的价值  
   **Example:** For many customers, the convenience outweighs the slightly higher price.  
   **中文：** 对许多顾客来说，便利性比略高的价格更重要。

25. **uphold** /ʌpˈhoʊld/  
   **English definition:** to support or maintain a rule, principle, or decision  
   **中文释义：** 维护；支持；维持（原则、规定或裁决）  
   **Example:** The court upheld the original ruling after reviewing the appeal.  
   **中文：** 法院审查上诉后维持了原判。
