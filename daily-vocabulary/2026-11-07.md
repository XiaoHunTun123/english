# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-11-07

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **adjust** /əˈdʒʌst/  
   **English definition:** to change something slightly so that it works better or suits a new situation  
   **中文释义：** 调整；适应  
   **Example:** We adjusted the meeting time to accommodate colleagues in different time zones.  
   **中文：** 为了照顾不同时区的同事，我们调整了会议时间。

2. **afford** /əˈfɔːrd/  
   **English definition:** to have enough money, time, or ability to do something  
   **中文释义：** 买得起；有时间或能力做  
   **Example:** We can't afford to delay the safety inspection any longer.  
   **中文：** 我们不能再拖延安全检查了。

3. **approve** /əˈpruːv/  
   **English definition:** to officially accept or agree to a plan, request, or action  
   **中文释义：** 批准；认可  
   **Example:** The director approved the revised budget this morning.  
   **中文：** 主管今天上午批准了修订后的预算。

4. **assume** /əˈsuːm/  
   **English definition:** to accept something as true without having proof  
   **中文释义：** 假定；想当然地认为  
   **Example:** Don't assume every customer has reliable internet access.  
   **中文：** 不要想当然地认为每位顾客都有稳定的网络。

5. **combine** /kəmˈbaɪn/  
   **English definition:** to join two or more things so that they form or work as one  
   **中文释义：** 结合；合并  
   **Example:** The researchers combined interview results with data from the survey.  
   **中文：** 研究人员把访谈结果与调查数据结合起来。

6. **criticize** /ˈkrɪtɪsaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to express disapproval of someone or something, or point out faults  
   **中文释义：** 批评；指责；评论不足  
   **Example:** The report criticized the plan for overlooking the needs of rural residents.  
   **中文：** 报告批评这项计划忽视了农村居民的需求。

7. **finance** /fəˈnæns/  
   **English definition:** to provide money for a project or activity  
   **中文释义：** 为……提供资金；资助  
   **Example:** The expansion will be financed through a combination of savings and a loan.  
   **中文：** 扩建资金将由存款和贷款共同提供。

8. **imply** /ɪmˈplaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to suggest something without saying it directly  
   **中文释义：** 暗示；意味着  
   **Example:** Her brief reply seemed to imply that the proposal needed more work.  
   **中文：** 她简短的回复似乎暗示这份提案还需要进一步完善。

9. **indicate** /ˈɪndɪkeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to show that something is likely, true, or present  
   **中文释义：** 表明；显示；指出  
   **Example:** Early results indicate that the new method is more accurate.  
   **中文：** 初步结果表明，新方法更加准确。

10. **obtain** /əbˈteɪn/  
    **English definition:** to get something, especially through effort or a formal process  
    **中文释义：** 获得；取得  
    **Example:** Applicants must obtain written permission before using the photographs.  
    **中文：** 申请者使用这些照片前必须取得书面许可。

11. **occur** /əˈkɜːr/  
    **English definition:** to happen, or to exist in a particular place or situation  
    **中文释义：** 发生；出现；存在  
    **Example:** The error occurs only when several users sign in at once.  
    **中文：** 只有多名用户同时登录时才会出现这个错误。

12. **potential** /pəˈtenʃəl/  
    **English definition:** the possibility that someone or something will develop or become successful  
    **中文释义：** 潜力；可能性；潜在的  
    **Example:** The trial revealed the potential of the tool to save staff time.  
    **中文：** 试用显示，这款工具有望节省员工的时间。

13. **promote** /prəˈmoʊt/  
    **English definition:** to encourage or support the development, use, or success of something  
    **中文释义：** 促进；推动；宣传  
    **Example:** The city is promoting cycling by adding protected bike lanes.  
    **中文：** 这座城市通过增设受保护的自行车道来推动骑行。

14. **rely** /rɪˈlaɪ/  
    **English definition:** to depend on someone or something for help or support  
    **中文释义：** 依靠；依赖  
    **Example:** Small clinics often rely on volunteers to provide translation support.  
    **中文：** 小型诊所常依靠志愿者提供翻译支持。

15. **significant** /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/  
    **English definition:** important or large enough to have a noticeable effect  
    **中文释义：** 重要的；显著的；相当大的  
    **Example:** The update produced a significant reduction in loading time.  
    **中文：** 这次更新显著缩短了加载时间。

16. **survey** /ˈsɜːrveɪ/  
    **English definition:** a set of questions used to collect information from a group of people  
    **中文释义：** 调查；问卷  
    **Example:** The library conducted a survey to learn which hours visitors preferred.  
    **中文：** 图书馆开展了一项调查，了解访客更喜欢哪些开放时段。

17. **technique** /tekˈniːk/  
    **English definition:** a particular way of doing something, especially one that requires skill  
    **中文释义：** 技巧；方法  
    **Example:** This breathing technique can help speakers stay calm before a presentation.  
    **中文：** 这种呼吸技巧能帮助演讲者在演示前保持镇定。

18. **incumbent** /ɪnˈkʌmbənt/  
    **English definition:** currently holding a particular official position  
    **中文释义：** 现任的；在职者  
    **Example:** The incumbent chair will remain in the role until the next election.  
    **中文：** 现任主席将继续任职，直到下一次选举。

19. **inoculate** /ɪˈnɑːkjəleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to protect someone from a disease by giving them a vaccine  
    **中文释义：** 给……接种疫苗；使免受感染  
    **Example:** The clinic will inoculate eligible residents against seasonal influenza.  
    **中文：** 诊所将为符合条件的居民接种季节性流感疫苗。

20. **nuanced** /ˈnuːɑːnst/  
    **English definition:** showing or considering small but important differences  
    **中文释义：** 有细微差别的；考虑周全的  
    **Example:** The article offers a nuanced view of the debate rather than a simple yes-or-no answer.  
    **中文：** 这篇文章对争论给出了细致的看法，而不是简单地回答是或否。

21. **outlook** /ˈaʊtlʊk/  
    **English definition:** expectations about the future, or a person's general attitude toward life  
    **中文释义：** 前景；展望；态度  
    **Example:** The latest figures have improved the economic outlook for the region.  
    **中文：** 最新数据改善了该地区的经济前景。

22. **simultaneous** /ˌsaɪməlˈteɪniəs/  
    **English definition:** happening or being done at the same time  
    **中文释义：** 同时发生的；同步的  
    **Example:** The app supports simultaneous editing by several team members.  
    **中文：** 这款应用支持多名团队成员同时编辑。

23. **solely** /ˈsoʊlli/  
    **English definition:** only; with no other person or thing involved  
    **中文释义：** 仅仅；唯一地；完全  
    **Example:** The decision was based solely on the published results.  
    **中文：** 这个决定完全依据已公布的结果作出。

24. **synergy** /ˈsɪnərdʒi/  
    **English definition:** a greater effect produced when people or things work together than when they work separately  
    **中文释义：** 协同效应；协作增效  
    **Example:** Combining the design and research teams created a useful synergy.  
    **中文：** 将设计团队与研究团队合并，产生了良好的协同效应。

25. **trigger** /ˈtrɪɡər/  
    **English definition:** to cause something to happen or make a process begin  
    **中文释义：** 引发；触发  
    **Example:** A sudden drop in pressure can trigger an automatic safety shutdown.  
    **中文：** 压力突然下降可能触发自动安全停机。
