# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-13

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accommodate** /əˈkɑːmədeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to provide enough space for someone or something, or to adjust to a need or change  
   **中文释义：** 容纳；适应；满足（需求）  
   **Example:** The software can accommodate teams of different sizes.  
   **中文：** 这款软件可以适应不同规模的团队。

2. **amplify** /ˈæmplɪfaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to make a sound, effect, or message stronger or more noticeable  
   **中文释义：** 放大；增强；扩大（影响或效果）  
   **Example:** Repeated posts amplified the rumor before the facts emerged.  
   **中文：** 事实水落石出之前，反复传播的帖子扩大了这则传闻的影响。

3. **applicable** /ˈæplɪkəbəl/  
   **English definition:** relevant or suitable for a particular situation, person, or purpose  
   **中文释义：** 适用的；适当的；可应用的  
   **Example:** These rules are applicable to contractors as well as employees.  
   **中文：** 这些规定不仅适用于员工，也适用于承包商。

4. **assert** /əˈsɜːrt/  
   **English definition:** to state something confidently or firmly, often to make it clear that it is true  
   **中文释义：** 坚称；断言；明确主张  
   **Example:** She asserted that the figures had been independently checked.  
   **中文：** 她坚称这些数据已经过独立核查。

5. **attribute** /əˈtrɪbjuːt/  
   **English definition:** to regard something as caused by or resulting from a particular factor  
   **中文释义：** 把……归因于；认为……是……所致  
   **Example:** Analysts attribute the recovery to steady demand.  
   **中文：** 分析人士将这次复苏归因于稳定的需求。

6. **collaborate** /kəˈlæbəreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to work jointly with others on a task or project  
   **中文释义：** 合作；协作  
   **Example:** The designers collaborated with teachers to test the materials.  
   **中文：** 设计人员与教师合作测试了这些材料。

7. **confront** /kənˈfrʌnt/  
   **English definition:** to face and deal directly with a difficult situation or problem  
   **中文释义：** 面对并处理；直面  
   **Example:** We need to confront the delays before they affect customers.  
   **中文：** 我们需要在延误影响客户之前正面处理这个问题。

8. **conserve** /kənˈsɜːrv/  
   **English definition:** to protect something from loss or damage, or use it carefully so it lasts  
   **中文释义：** 保护；保存；节约使用  
   **Example:** The new irrigation system helps conserve water.  
   **中文：** 新的灌溉系统有助于节约用水。

9. **coordinate** /koʊˈɔːrdəneɪt/  
   **English definition:** to organize people or activities so that they work together effectively  
   **中文释义：** 协调；统筹安排  
   **Example:** A shared calendar helps coordinate shifts across three sites.  
   **中文：** 共享日历有助于协调三个地点的轮班安排。

10. **deduce** /dɪˈduːs/  
   **English definition:** to reach a conclusion by reasoning from evidence or known facts  
   **中文释义：** 推断；演绎得出  
   **Example:** From the timestamps, investigators deduced that the file was uploaded overnight.  
   **中文：** 调查人员根据时间戳推断，文件是在夜间上传的。

11. **deficient** /dɪˈfɪʃənt/  
   **English definition:** lacking something necessary or not meeting an expected standard  
   **中文释义：** 缺乏的；不足的；有缺陷的  
   **Example:** The inspection found the wiring deficient in several areas.  
   **中文：** 检查发现多处线路不符合标准。

12. **eliminate** /ɪˈlɪməneɪt/  
   **English definition:** to remove or get rid of something completely  
   **中文释义：** 消除；排除；彻底去掉  
   **Example:** The redesign eliminates several unnecessary steps.  
   **中文：** 重新设计去掉了几个不必要的步骤。

13. **encounter** /ɪnˈkaʊntər/  
   **English definition:** to meet, experience, or come across someone or something, often unexpectedly  
   **中文释义：** 遇到；遭遇；碰到  
   **Example:** Travelers may encounter long waits during the holiday period.  
   **中文：** 假期期间，旅客可能会遇到长时间等候的情况。

14. **exaggerate** /ɪɡˈzædʒəreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to describe something as greater, worse, or more important than it really is  
   **中文释义：** 夸大；言过其实  
   **Example:** The headline exaggerates the study’s conclusions.  
   **中文：** 这个标题夸大了研究的结论。

15. **execute** /ˈeksɪkjuːt/  
   **English definition:** to carry out a plan, task, or instruction  
   **中文释义：** 执行；实施；完成（计划或任务）  
   **Example:** The team executed the system migration over the weekend.  
   **中文：** 团队在周末完成了系统迁移。

16. **exhaust** /ɪɡˈzɔːst/  
   **English definition:** to use up a supply completely, or to make someone extremely tired  
   **中文释义：** 耗尽；用完；使筋疲力尽  
   **Example:** The long inspection exhausted the technicians.  
   **中文：** 长时间的检查让技术人员筋疲力尽。

17. **exhibit** /ɪɡˈzɪbɪt/  
   **English definition:** to show or display a quality, behavior, or feature  
   **中文释义：** 显示；表现出；展示  
   **Example:** The material exhibits unusual resistance to heat.  
   **中文：** 这种材料表现出不同寻常的耐热性。

18. **flexible** /ˈfleksəbəl/  
   **English definition:** able to change or adapt easily to different conditions or needs  
   **中文释义：** 灵活的；可变通的；适应性强的  
   **Example:** The flexible schedule lets employees avoid peak commuting hours.  
   **中文：** 灵活的日程安排让员工能够避开通勤高峰。

19. **obstacle** /ˈɑːbstəkəl/  
   **English definition:** something that makes progress difficult or prevents a person from achieving a goal  
   **中文释义：** 障碍；阻碍  
   **Example:** A lack of reliable data remains the main obstacle to approval.  
   **中文：** 缺乏可靠数据仍是获批的主要障碍。

20. **precaution** /prɪˈkɔːʃən/  
   **English definition:** an action taken in advance to prevent harm, trouble, or an accident  
   **中文释义：** 预防措施；谨慎的做法  
   **Example:** As a precaution, save a copy before updating the system.  
   **中文：** 为防万一，更新系统前先保存一份副本。

21. **preserve** /prɪˈzɜːrv/  
   **English definition:** to keep something in its existing condition or protect it from harm or loss  
   **中文释义：** 保存；保护；维持原状  
   **Example:** The archive preserves letters that would otherwise be lost.  
   **中文：** 这家档案馆保存了原本可能遗失的信件。

22. **procedure** /prəˈsiːdʒər/  
   **English definition:** an established or official way of doing something, especially a series of steps  
   **中文释义：** 程序；流程；手续  
   **Example:** Follow the standard procedure for reporting a damaged package.  
   **中文：** 按照标准流程报告包裹受损情况。

23. **restrict** /rɪˈstrɪkt/  
   **English definition:** to limit the size, amount, range, or freedom of something  
   **中文释义：** 限制；约束；限定  
   **Example:** The permit restricts access to the northern trail after dark.  
   **中文：** 许可证规定，天黑后不得进入北部步道。

24. **withstand** /wɪðˈstænd/  
   **English definition:** to remain undamaged or unaffected despite force, pressure, or difficult conditions  
   **中文释义：** 承受；经受住；抵挡  
   **Example:** This coating can withstand high temperatures.  
   **中文：** 这种涂层能够耐受高温。

25. **worthwhile** /ˌwɜːrθˈwaɪl/  
   **English definition:** worth the time, effort, or money spent on it  
   **中文释义：** 值得花时间、精力或金钱的  
   **Example:** The training takes an hour, but the improvement makes it worthwhile.  
   **中文：** 培训需要一小时，但带来的提升让这段时间花得很值。
