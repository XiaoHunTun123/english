# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-07-29

本日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。

1. **pragmatic** /præɡˈmætɪk/ — focused on practical results；务实的；实用主义的  
   *We need a pragmatic approach to the problem.*  
   我们需要用务实的方法解决这个问题。

2. **concise** /kənˈsaɪs/ — brief but clear；简洁的；简明的  
   *Please keep your answer concise.*  
   请让你的回答简明扼要。

3. **robust** /roʊˈbʌst/ — strong and able to withstand difficulty；强健的；稳固的  
   *The system is robust enough for heavy use.*  
   这个系统足以应对高强度使用。

4. **sporadic** /spəˈrædɪk/ — happening occasionally and irregularly；零星的；不规律的  
   *There were sporadic power cuts during the storm.*  
   暴风雨期间偶尔会停电。

5. **susceptible** /səˈseptəbəl/ — easily affected or influenced；易受影响的  
   *Young plants are susceptible to extreme temperatures.*  
   幼苗容易受到极端温度的影响。

6. **reconcile** /ˈrekənsaɪl/ — to make two ideas or groups compatible；调和；使一致  
   *It is difficult to reconcile the two accounts.*  
   很难调和这两种说法。

7. **devise** /dɪˈvaɪz/ — to invent or plan something carefully；设计；制定  
   *They devised a new method of testing.*  
   他们设计了一种新的测试方法。

8. **attain** /əˈteɪn/ — to achieve something after effort；达到；获得  
   *She attained her goal through steady practice.*  
   她通过持续练习实现了目标。

9. **retain** /rɪˈteɪn/ — to keep or continue to have something；保留；保持  
   *The material retains heat for a long time.*  
   这种材料能够长时间保温。

10. **diminish** /dɪˈmɪnɪʃ/ — to become or make less；减少；减弱  
    *The pain gradually diminished.*  
    疼痛逐渐减轻了。

11. **enhance** /ɪnˈhæns/ — to improve the quality or value of something；提高；增强  
    *The lighting enhances the atmosphere.*  
    灯光增强了氛围感。

12. **facilitate** /fəˈsɪləteɪt/ — to make an action or process easier；促进；使便利  
    *The app facilitates communication between users.*  
    这款应用方便了用户之间的交流。

13. **fluctuate** /ˈflʌktʃueɪt/ — to change irregularly；波动；起伏  
    *Prices fluctuate with supply and demand.*  
    价格会随供求关系波动。

14. **evoke** /ɪˈvoʊk/ — to bring a feeling or memory to mind；唤起；引发  
    *The song evokes memories of childhood.*  
    这首歌唤起了童年的回忆。

15. **justify** /ˈdʒʌstəfaɪ/ — to show that something is reasonable；证明……合理  
    *Nothing can justify such rude behavior.*  
    没有什么能为如此粗鲁的行为辩护。

16. **persist** /pərˈsɪst/ — to continue despite difficulty；坚持；持续存在  
    *If the symptoms persist, see a doctor.*  
    如果症状持续，请去看医生。

17. **intricate** /ˈɪntrɪkət/ — complicated and containing many details；复杂精细的  
    *The machine has an intricate design.*  
    这台机器的设计非常复杂精细。

18. **impartial** /ɪmˈpɑːrʃəl/ — fair and not favoring either side；公正的；不偏不倚的  
    *The judge must remain impartial.*  
    法官必须保持公正。

19. **prudent** /ˈpruːdənt/ — wise and careful about the future；谨慎的；明智的  
    *It would be prudent to save some money.*  
    存一些钱是明智的做法。

20. **mandatory** /ˈmændətɔːri/ — required by a rule or law；强制性的；必须的  
    *Safety training is mandatory for all staff.*  
    所有员工都必须接受安全培训。

21. **preliminary** /prɪˈlɪməneri/ — happening before the main event；初步的；预备的  
    *The team released its preliminary findings.*  
    团队公布了初步调查结果。

22. **subsequent** /ˈsʌbsɪkwənt/ — happening after something else；随后的；后来的  
    *Subsequent tests confirmed the diagnosis.*  
    随后的测试证实了诊断结果。

23. **surplus** /ˈsɜːrplʌs/ — an amount more than what is needed；过剩；剩余  
    *The surplus food was donated to a shelter.*  
    多余的食物被捐给了收容所。

24. **compensate** /ˈkɑːmpenseɪt/ — to make up for a loss or disadvantage；补偿；弥补  
    *The company compensated customers for the delay.*  
    公司因延误向顾客提供了补偿。

25. **contemplate** /ˈkɑːntəmpleɪt/ — to think about something deeply；仔细考虑；沉思  
    *She is contemplating a change of career.*  
    她正在认真考虑转行。
