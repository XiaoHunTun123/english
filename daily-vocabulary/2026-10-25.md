# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-25

以下 25 个词汇兼顾阅读、工作和正式表达中的实用性，整体偏进阶但不冷僻。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abolition** /ˌæbəˈlɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** the official ending of a law, system, or practice  
   **中文释义：** 废除；废止  
   **Example:** The campaign called for the abolition of outdated licensing rules.  
   **中文：** 这场运动呼吁废除过时的许可规定。

2. **accommodation** /əˌkɑːməˈdeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a place to stay; an adjustment made to meet someone's needs  
   **中文释义：** 住宿；适应性安排；迁就  
   **Example:** The conference team arranged accommodation for participants with accessibility needs.  
   **中文：** 会务团队为有无障碍需求的参会者安排了住宿。

3. **advisory** /ədˈvaɪzəri/  
   **English definition:** intended to give advice or a warning; a formal notice that provides guidance  
   **中文释义：** 咨询性的；建议性的；公告  
   **Example:** The agency issued an advisory about possible delays on the rail network.  
   **中文：** 该机构发布公告，提醒铁路网络可能出现延误。

4. **alleged** /əˈledʒd/  
   **English definition:** said to be true or to have happened, but not yet proved  
   **中文释义：** 被指称的；据称的；尚未证实的  
   **Example:** The report examined the alleged misuse of public funds.  
   **中文：** 这份报告调查了有关公共资金遭滥用的指称。

5. **altitude** /ˈæltɪtuːd/  
   **English definition:** height above sea level or above the ground  
   **中文释义：** 海拔；高度  
   **Example:** At this altitude, visitors are advised to rest and drink water.  
   **中文：** 在这个海拔高度，建议游客休息并补充饮水。

6. **architecture** /ˈɑːrkɪtektʃər/  
   **English definition:** the design of buildings; the basic structure of a computer system or organization  
   **中文释义：** 建筑学；建筑设计；架构  
   **Example:** The engineers simplified the system architecture to make maintenance easier.  
   **中文：** 工程师简化了系统架构，以便于维护。

7. **authorization** /ˌɔːθərəˈzeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** official permission to do, use, or access something  
   **中文释义：** 授权；批准；许可  
   **Example:** Access to the archive requires written authorization.  
   **中文：** 查阅档案需要书面授权。

8. **broadcast** /ˈbrɔːdkæst/  
   **English definition:** to send a program or message to many people by radio, television, or the internet  
   **中文释义：** 广播；播送；传播  
   **Example:** The results will be broadcast live after the final round.  
   **中文：** 最后一轮结束后，结果将进行现场直播。

9. **caliber** /ˈkælɪbər/  
   **English definition:** the quality or standard of a person or thing; also, the internal diameter of a gun barrel  
   **中文释义：** 水准；品质；口径  
   **Example:** The panel was impressed by the caliber of the applicants.  
   **中文：** 评审小组对申请者的水平印象深刻。

10. **collectively** /kəˈlektɪvli/  
    **English definition:** as a group or considered together  
    **中文释义：** 集体地；共同地；总计  
    **Example:** The three departments collectively manage more than fifty projects.  
    **中文：** 这三个部门共同管理着五十多个项目。

11. **compound** /ˈkɑːmpaʊnd/  
    **English definition:** a substance made from two or more elements joined chemically  
    **中文释义：** 化合物；复合物  
    **Example:** Researchers tested the compound for stability under heat.  
    **中文：** 研究人员测试了这种化合物在高温下的稳定性。

12. **converse** /kənˈvɜːrs/  
    **English definition:** to have a conversation, especially in a formal or thoughtful way  
    **中文释义：** 交谈；谈话  
    **Example:** The delegates met privately to converse about the proposal.  
    **中文：** 代表们私下会面，讨论这项提案。

13. **correspond** /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːnd/  
    **English definition:** to be similar or match; to communicate by writing  
    **中文释义：** 相符；对应；通信  
    **Example:** The figures in the summary should correspond to those in the full report.  
    **中文：** 摘要中的数字应与完整报告中的数据相符。

14. **curb** /kɜːrb/  
    **English definition:** to control or limit something harmful or unwanted  
    **中文释义：** 控制；抑制；限制  
    **Example:** The new measures are designed to curb unnecessary energy use.  
    **中文：** 这些新措施旨在减少不必要的能源消耗。

15. **customary** /ˈkʌstəmeri/  
    **English definition:** usual or traditional in a particular place, group, or situation  
    **中文释义：** 惯常的；习俗上的；合乎惯例的  
    **Example:** It is customary to acknowledge the original source when reusing an image.  
    **中文：** 再次使用图片时，注明原始来源是惯例。

16. **decisive** /dɪˈsaɪsɪv/  
    **English definition:** settling an issue or producing a clear result; able to make decisions confidently  
    **中文释义：** 决定性的；果断的  
    **Example:** Reliable evidence was decisive in resolving the dispute.  
    **中文：** 可靠证据对解决这场争议起到了决定性作用。

17. **decree** /dɪˈkriː/  
    **English definition:** an official order or decision made by a government or authority  
    **中文释义：** 法令；政令；裁定  
    **Example:** A royal decree established the protected status of the forest.  
    **中文：** 一项王室法令确立了这片森林的保护地位。

18. **deliberate** /dɪˈlɪbərət/  
    **English definition:** intentional and carefully considered rather than accidental or rushed  
    **中文释义：** 故意的；审慎的；深思熟虑的  
    **Example:** The team made a deliberate choice to delay the launch until testing was complete.  
    **中文：** 团队经过慎重考虑，决定等测试完成后再发布。

19. **deviation** /ˌdiːviˈeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a difference from what is usual, expected, or officially planned  
    **中文释义：** 偏离；偏差；背离  
    **Example:** Any deviation from the approved procedure must be documented.  
    **中文：** 任何偏离获批流程的情况都必须记录在案。

20. **discard** /dɪˈskɑːrd/  
    **English definition:** to get rid of something because it is no longer useful or wanted  
    **中文释义：** 丢弃；抛弃；不再采用  
    **Example:** The team discarded the first draft after receiving detailed feedback.  
    **中文：** 收到详细反馈后，团队放弃了初稿。

21. **discriminate** /dɪˈskrɪməneɪt/  
    **English definition:** to treat a person or group unfairly; to recognize a difference between things  
    **中文释义：** 歧视；区别；辨别  
    **Example:** The policy prohibits employers from discriminating on the basis of age.  
    **中文：** 该政策禁止雇主因年龄而歧视员工。

22. **distinction** /dɪˈstɪŋkʃən/  
    **English definition:** a difference between things; special recognition for achievement  
    **中文释义：** 区别；差异；荣誉  
    **Example:** It is important to make a distinction between correlation and causation.  
    **中文：** 区分相关性和因果关系很重要。

23. **estimate** /ˈestəmeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to make a reasonable judgment about an amount, cost, size, or time  
    **中文释义：** 估计；估算  
    **Example:** Contractors estimate that repairs will take about three weeks.  
    **中文：** 承包商估计维修大约需要三周。

24. **liability** /ˌlaɪəˈbɪləti/  
    **English definition:** legal responsibility for something; a person or thing that creates a disadvantage or risk  
    **中文释义：** 法律责任；负债；不利因素  
    **Example:** The agreement clarifies each party's liability if equipment is damaged.  
    **中文：** 协议明确了设备受损时各方应承担的责任。

25. **prospect** /ˈprɑːspekt/  
    **English definition:** the possibility that something will happen; a chance of future success  
    **中文释义：** 可能性；前景；成功机会  
    **Example:** The company sees strong prospects for growth in regional markets.  
    **中文：** 公司看好其在区域市场的发展前景。
