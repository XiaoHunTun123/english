# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-20

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **anodyne** /ˈænədaɪn/ — unlikely to offend or cause disagreement；不太可能冒犯或引起争议的；温和的；无害的
   - **Example:** The minister gave an anodyne response that avoided taking a clear position.
   - **中文：** 部长给出了温和但回避明确立场的回应。

2. **coercion** /koʊˈɜːrʒən/ — the use of force or threats to make someone act；使用武力或威胁迫使某人行动；强迫；胁迫
   - **Example:** The contract is invalid if it was signed under coercion.
   - **中文：** 如果合同是在胁迫下签署的，它就是无效的。

3. **compliant** /kəmˈplaɪənt/ — willing to obey rules or requests；愿意遵守规则或服从要求的；顺从的；合规的
   - **Example:** The software is compliant with the latest privacy regulations.
   - **中文：** 这款软件符合最新的隐私法规。

4. **conformity** /kənˈfɔːrməti/ — behavior that follows accepted rules or standards；遵循公认规则或标准的行为；遵从；一致
   - **Example:** The design was changed for conformity with safety standards.
   - **中文：** 设计经过修改，以符合安全标准。

5. **connoisseur** /ˌkɑːnəˈsɜːr/ — an expert judge of quality, especially in art or food；鉴别质量的专家，尤其指艺术或食品方面；鉴赏家
   - **Example:** As a connoisseur of tea, she can identify the region where it was grown.
   - **中文：** 作为茶叶鉴赏家，她能辨认出茶叶的产地。

6. **consensus** /kənˈsensəs/ — general agreement among a group；群体中的普遍同意；共识
   - **Example:** The committee reached a consensus after several rounds of discussion.
   - **中文：** 经过几轮讨论，委员会达成了共识。

7. **contiguous** /kənˈtɪɡjuəs/ — sharing a border or touching along an edge；共享边界或边缘相接的；相邻的
   - **Example:** The two offices occupy contiguous rooms on the second floor.
   - **中文：** 两间办公室位于二楼相邻的房间内。

8. **conventional** /kənˈvenʃənəl/ — following traditional or commonly accepted ways；遵循传统或普遍接受方式的；传统的；惯常的
   - **Example:** The company moved away from conventional advertising and focused on online communities.
   - **中文：** 公司不再采用传统广告，而是把重点放在网络社区上。

9. **cumulative** /ˈkjuːmjələtɪv/ — increasing as successive amounts or effects are added；随着各部分或影响相加而增加的；累积的
   - **Example:** The cumulative effect of small delays was substantial.
   - **中文：** 多次小延误的累积影响十分显著。

10. **divergent** /daɪˈvɜːrdʒənt/ — developing in different directions or differing in opinion；朝不同方向发展或观点不同的；分歧的
    - **Example:** The two studies reached divergent conclusions.
    - **中文：** 两项研究得出了不同的结论。

11. **equilibrium** /ˌiːkwɪˈlɪbriəm/ — a state of balance between opposing forces or influences；相反力量或影响之间的平衡状态；均衡
    - **Example:** The new policy aims to restore equilibrium between growth and environmental protection.
    - **中文：** 新政策旨在恢复经济增长与环境保护之间的平衡。

12. **fatuous** /ˈfætʃuəs/ — foolish and lacking intelligence or purpose；愚蠢且缺乏智慧或目的的；愚昧的；空洞的
    - **Example:** The proposal was dismissed as a fatuous attempt to solve a complex problem.
    - **中文：** 这项提议被认为是用愚蠢方式解决复杂问题的尝试。

13. **forensic** /fəˈrensɪk/ — relating to scientific methods used in legal investigation；与法律调查中使用的科学方法有关的；法医的；取证的
    - **Example:** Forensic analysis of the device revealed when the files were altered.
    - **中文：** 对设备进行取证分析后，查明了文件被修改的时间。

14. **incredulous** /ɪnˈkredʒələs/ — unwilling or unable to believe something；不愿或无法相信某事的；怀疑的
    - **Example:** The audience looked incredulous when the result was announced.
    - **中文：** 结果公布时，观众们显得难以置信。

15. **inference** /ˈɪnfərəns/ — a conclusion reached from evidence and reasoning；根据证据和推理得出的结论；推断
    - **Example:** The conclusion is a reasonable inference from the available data.
    - **中文：** 这个结论是从现有数据中得出的合理推断。

16. **impropriety** /ˌɪmprəˈpraɪəti/ — improper or unacceptable behavior；不恰当或不可接受的行为；不当行为
    - **Example:** The review found no evidence of financial impropriety.
    - **中文：** 审查没有发现财务不当行为的证据。

17. **nondescript** /ˌnɑːndɪˈskrɪpt/ — ordinary and not distinctive in appearance or character；外表或特征普通且不起眼的；不起眼的
    - **Example:** The office was hidden behind a nondescript gray door.
    - **中文：** 办公室藏在一扇不起眼的灰色门后面。

18. **restive** /ˈrestɪv/ — unable to remain still or patient；无法保持安静或耐心的；不安的；躁动的
    - **Example:** Passengers grew restive after the train remained stopped for an hour.
    - **中文：** 火车停了一个小时后，乘客们变得焦躁不安。

19. **genteel** /dʒenˈtiːl/ — polite, refined, and socially respectable；礼貌、文雅且体面的；文雅的
    - **Example:** The restaurant has a genteel atmosphere suited to formal dinners.
    - **中文：** 这家餐厅氛围文雅，适合正式晚宴。

20. **jocular** /ˈdʒɑːkjələr/ — humorous and playful；幽默且爱开玩笑的；诙谐的
    - **Example:** His jocular remark eased the tension in the room.
    - **中文：** 他诙谐的话缓和了房间里的紧张气氛。

21. **malfeasance** /mælˈfiːzəns/ — wrongdoing, especially by a public official；错误或违法行为，尤其指公职人员的不当行为；渎职；违法行为
    - **Example:** The investigation focused on allegations of official malfeasance.
    - **中文：** 调查重点关注公职人员渎职的指控。

22. **solicitous** /səˈlɪsɪtəs/ — showing concern for someone’s welfare；表现出对某人福祉的关心；关切的；体贴的
    - **Example:** The nurse was solicitous of every patient’s needs.
    - **中文：** 护士体贴地关照每位病人的需求。

23. **torrid** /ˈtɔːrɪd/ — extremely hot or emotionally intense；极其炎热或情感强烈的；炽热的；激烈的
    - **Example:** The team endured a torrid summer while working on the construction site.
    - **中文：** 团队在建筑工地上熬过了炽热的夏天。

24. **vacuous** /ˈvækjuəs/ — lacking ideas, intelligence, or meaningful content；缺乏思想、智慧或有意义内容的；空洞的
    - **Example:** The interview was filled with vacuous slogans instead of practical answers.
    - **中文：** 采访中充斥着空洞的口号，而不是实际回答。

25. **verbose** /vɜːrˈboʊs/ — using more words than necessary；使用多于必要数量词语的；冗长的
    - **Example:** The original report was accurate but too verbose for a public summary.
    - **中文：** 原报告很准确，但对于公众摘要来说过于冗长。
