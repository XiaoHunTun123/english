# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-11-02

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **alternative** /ɔːlˈtɜːrnətɪv/  
   **English definition:** another choice or possibility that can replace the first one  
   **中文释义：** 可替代的选择；替代方案  
   **Example:** If the direct train is full, there is an alternative route through Bristol.  
   **中文：** 如果直达列车满员，还可以选择经布里斯托尔中转的路线。

2. **approach** /əˈproʊtʃ/  
   **English definition:** a way of dealing with a task or problem  
   **中文释义：** 方法；处理方式  
   **Example:** We need a more practical approach to reducing food waste.  
   **中文：** 我们需要用更实际的方法来减少食物浪费。

3. **capable** /ˈkeɪpəbəl/  
   **English definition:** having the ability, skill, or qualities needed to do something  
   **中文释义：** 有能力的；能胜任的  
   **Example:** The new system is capable of processing thousands of requests per minute.  
   **中文：** 这个新系统每分钟能够处理数千条请求。

4. **channel** /ˈtʃænəl/  
   **English definition:** a method or system for communicating, distributing, or directing something  
   **中文释义：** 渠道；途径  
   **Example:** Email remains the most reliable channel for official notices.  
   **中文：** 电子邮件仍是发送正式通知最可靠的渠道。

5. **coherence** /koʊˈhɪrəns/  
   **English definition:** the quality of being logical, consistent, and easy to understand  
   **中文释义：** 连贯性；条理性；一致性  
   **Example:** Adding clear transitions improved the coherence of the presentation.  
   **中文：** 加入清晰的过渡语后，演示内容更加连贯。

6. **compete** /kəmˈpiːt/  
   **English definition:** to try to be more successful than others who want the same thing  
   **中文释义：** 竞争；角逐  
   **Example:** Small firms often compete by offering more personal service.  
   **中文：** 小公司常通过提供更个性化的服务来参与竞争。

7. **complex** /kəmˈpleks/  
   **English definition:** made up of many connected parts and therefore not simple  
   **中文释义：** 复杂的；由多部分构成的  
   **Example:** The issue is complex, so one quick fix is unlikely to solve it.  
   **中文：** 这个问题很复杂，因此一个简单的快速修补恐怕解决不了。

8. **concept** /ˈkɑːnsept/  
   **English definition:** a general idea or principle  
   **中文释义：** 概念；观念  
   **Example:** The concept is simple: customers pay only for what they use.  
   **中文：** 这个理念很简单：顾客只为实际使用的部分付费。

9. **confirm** /kənˈfɜːrm/  
   **English definition:** to show that something is true, or make an arrangement definite  
   **中文释义：** 确认；证实  
   **Example:** Please confirm your attendance by Thursday afternoon.  
   **中文：** 请在周四下午前确认是否出席。

10. **context** /ˈkɑːntekst/  
    **English definition:** the situation or surrounding information that helps explain something  
    **中文释义：** 背景；上下文；语境  
    **Example:** The quote sounds harsh when removed from its original context.  
    **中文：** 这句话脱离原本语境后听起来显得很尖刻。

11. **contradiction** /ˌkɑːntrəˈdɪkʃən/  
    **English definition:** a statement or fact that is opposite to another, so both cannot be true  
    **中文释义：** 矛盾；自相矛盾之处  
    **Example:** There is a contradiction between the written policy and what staff were told.  
    **中文：** 书面政策与员工收到的口头指示之间存在矛盾。

12. **contribute** /kənˈtrɪbjuːt/  
    **English definition:** to give something, such as time or effort, or help cause a result  
    **中文释义：** 贡献；捐助；促成  
    **Example:** Several factors contributed to the project's delay.  
    **中文：** 多种因素共同导致了项目延期。

13. **determination** /dɪˌtɜːrmɪˈneɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the quality of continuing firmly despite difficulty  
    **中文释义：** 决心；毅力  
    **Example:** Her determination helped the team finish the work after setbacks.  
    **中文：** 遇到挫折后，她的坚定决心帮助团队完成了工作。

14. **determine** /dɪˈtɜːrmɪn/  
    **English definition:** to discover or decide something by examining the facts  
    **中文释义：** 查明；确定；决定  
    **Example:** Further testing will determine whether the material is safe.  
    **中文：** 进一步测试将确定这种材料是否安全。

15. **device** /dɪˈvaɪs/  
    **English definition:** a piece of equipment designed for a particular purpose  
    **中文释义：** 设备；装置  
    **Example:** The device records temperature every ten seconds.  
    **中文：** 这台设备每十秒记录一次温度。

16. **economy** /ɪˈkɑːnəmi/  
    **English definition:** the system by which a country or region produces, buys, and sells goods and services  
    **中文释义：** 经济；经济体系  
    **Example:** Tourism provides a significant share of the island's economy.  
    **中文：** 旅游业在这座岛屿的经济中占有相当大的比重。

17. **ensure** /ɪnˈʃʊr/  
    **English definition:** to make certain that something happens or is true  
    **中文释义：** 确保；保证  
    **Example:** Check the address twice to ensure the parcel reaches the right office.  
    **中文：** 请核对两遍地址，确保包裹送到正确的办公室。

18. **evaluate** /ɪˈvæljueɪt/  
    **English definition:** to judge the quality, usefulness, or importance of something  
    **中文释义：** 评估；评价  
    **Example:** The school will evaluate the program at the end of the year.  
    **中文：** 学校将在年底评估这项计划。

19. **evidence** /ˈevɪdəns/  
    **English definition:** facts or information that help show whether something is true  
    **中文释义：** 证据；依据  
    **Example:** The researchers gathered evidence from interviews and field observations.  
    **中文：** 研究人员通过访谈和实地观察收集了证据。

20. **expand** /ɪkˈspænd/  
    **English definition:** to become larger, or make something larger or more extensive  
    **中文释义：** 扩大；扩展；扩充  
    **Example:** The company plans to expand its repair service to three more cities.  
    **中文：** 公司计划把维修服务扩展到另外三个城市。

21. **feature** /ˈfiːtʃər/  
    **English definition:** an important or noticeable part or quality of something  
    **中文释义：** 特征；特色；功能  
    **Example:** Offline access is a useful feature for people who travel often.  
    **中文：** 对经常出行的人来说，离线访问是一项实用功能。

22. **mechanism** /ˈmekənɪzəm/  
    **English definition:** a process or system through which something works or happens  
    **中文释义：** 机制；原理；运作方式  
    **Example:** The agreement includes a mechanism for resolving future disputes.  
    **中文：** 这份协议包含解决未来争议的机制。

23. **objective** /əbˈdʒektɪv/  
    **English definition:** a goal that someone is trying to achieve  
    **中文释义：** 目标；目的  
    **Example:** Our main objective is to make the service easier to access.  
    **中文：** 我们的主要目标是让这项服务更容易获得。

24. **reliable** /rɪˈlaɪəbəl/  
    **English definition:** able to be trusted to work well or behave as expected  
    **中文释义：** 可靠的；可信赖的  
    **Example:** We need a reliable source before publishing the figures.  
    **中文：** 发布这些数据前，我们需要找到可靠的来源。

25. **resolve** /rɪˈzɑːlv/  
    **English definition:** to solve a problem or settle a disagreement  
    **中文释义：** 解决；处理；消除  
    **Example:** The support team resolved the billing issue within a day.  
    **中文：** 客服团队在一天内解决了账单问题。
