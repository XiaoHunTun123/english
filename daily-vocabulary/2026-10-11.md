# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-11

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accrue** /əˈkruː/  
   **English definition:** to accumulate or increase over time, especially in reference to interest, costs, or benefits  
   **中文释义：** 逐渐积累；（利息、费用或收益）累积  
   **Example:** Late fees accrue daily until the outstanding balance is paid.  
   **中文：** 未结清的欠款每天都会累积滞纳金。

2. **aggregate** /ˈæɡrɪɡeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to collect information or items and combine them into a whole or total  
   **中文释义：** 汇总；聚合  
   **Example:** The dashboard aggregates data from all regional offices.  
   **中文：** 这个仪表板汇总了所有地区办事处的数据。

3. **aptitude** /ˈæptɪtuːd/  
   **English definition:** a natural ability to learn or do a particular kind of work  
   **中文释义：** 天赋；才能；学习某类技能的能力  
   **Example:** Her aptitude for languages became clear after just a few lessons.  
   **中文：** 只上了几节课，她的语言天赋就显现出来了。

4. **backlash** /ˈbæklæʃ/  
   **English definition:** a strong negative reaction to an action, decision, or public statement  
   **中文释义：** 强烈反对；负面反弹  
   **Example:** The company faced a public backlash after announcing the closure.  
   **中文：** 公司宣布关闭业务后，遭到了公众的强烈反对。

5. **benchmark** /ˈbentʃmɑːrk/  
   **English definition:** a standard or point of reference used to judge or compare performance  
   **中文释义：** 基准；衡量标准  
   **Example:** We use last year’s results as a benchmark for the new campaign.  
   **中文：** 我们用去年的成果作为评估新宣传活动的基准。

6. **buffer** /ˈbʌfər/  
   **English definition:** something that reduces the effect of a shock or provides a protective margin  
   **中文释义：** 缓冲；缓冲物；保护余量  
   **Example:** Keep a cash buffer to cover unexpected repairs.  
   **中文：** 留出一笔现金储备，以应付意外维修开支。

7. **comply** /kəmˈplaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to act according to a rule, request, or instruction  
   **中文释义：** 遵守；依从  
   **Example:** All suppliers must comply with the updated safety standards.  
   **中文：** 所有供应商都必须遵守更新后的安全标准。

8. **dwindle** /ˈdwɪndəl/  
   **English definition:** to gradually become smaller, fewer, or less important  
   **中文释义：** 逐渐减少；变小；衰减  
   **Example:** As winter continued, the town’s food supplies began to dwindle.  
   **中文：** 随着冬季持续，镇上的食物储备开始逐渐减少。

9. **embark** /ɪmˈbɑːrk/  
   **English definition:** to begin a significant activity, journey, or course of action  
   **中文释义：** 着手；开始（重要活动或旅程）  
   **Example:** She embarked on a year-long research project.  
   **中文：** 她着手开展一项为期一年的研究项目。

10. **endorse** /ɪnˈdɔːrs/  
   **English definition:** to publicly express support for someone, something, or a proposal  
   **中文释义：** 公开支持；认可；背书  
   **Example:** Several doctors endorsed the public health campaign.  
   **中文：** 几位医生公开支持这项公共卫生宣传活动。

11. **exempt** /ɪɡˈzempt/  
   **English definition:** free from a duty, rule, or payment that applies to others  
   **中文释义：** 免除……的；获豁免的  
   **Example:** Small charities may be exempt from this fee.  
   **中文：** 小型慈善机构可能可以免缴这项费用。

12. **foresee** /fɔːrˈsiː/  
   **English definition:** to recognize or expect that something is likely to happen in the future  
   **中文释义：** 预见；预料  
   **Example:** We didn’t foresee the shipping delay when setting the launch date.  
   **中文：** 制定上市日期时，我们没有预料到运输会延误。

13. **friction** /ˈfrɪkʃən/  
   **English definition:** disagreement or tension between people or groups; resistance between surfaces  
   **中文释义：** 摩擦；不和；（物理）摩擦力  
   **Example:** Unclear responsibilities created friction between the two teams.  
   **中文：** 职责不明确导致两个团队之间产生了摩擦。

14. **implement** /ˈɪmpləment/  
   **English definition:** to put a plan, decision, or system into effect  
   **中文释义：** 实施；执行；落实  
   **Example:** The school will implement the new attendance policy in September.  
   **中文：** 学校将在九月实施新的考勤规定。

15. **insulate** /ˈɪnsəleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to protect something from heat, sound, or outside influence  
   **中文释义：** 使隔热；使隔音；使免受外界影响  
   **Example:** The editor tried to insulate the review process from political pressure.  
   **中文：** 编辑试图让评审过程免受政治压力的影响。

16. **intervene** /ˌɪntərˈviːn/  
   **English definition:** to become involved in a situation in order to change its course or prevent a problem  
   **中文释义：** 干预；介入  
   **Example:** A mediator intervened before the disagreement escalated.  
   **中文：** 在分歧进一步升级之前，一位调解人介入了。

17. **intrinsic** /ɪnˈtrɪnsɪk/  
   **English definition:** belonging naturally to something and forming an essential part of it  
   **中文释义：** 内在的；固有的；本质的  
   **Example:** Curiosity has intrinsic value, even when it leads to no immediate reward.  
   **中文：** 好奇心本身就有价值，即使它没有带来即时回报。

18. **liable** /ˈlaɪəbəl/  
   **English definition:** legally responsible for something, or likely to experience a particular condition  
   **中文释义：** 有法律责任的；易于……的  
   **Example:** The carrier may be liable for damage caused during transit.  
   **中文：** 承运方可能需要对运输过程中造成的损坏承担责任。

19. **obligation** /ˌɑːbləˈɡeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a duty or commitment required by law, agreement, or a sense of responsibility  
   **中文释义：** 义务；责任；承诺  
   **Example:** We have an obligation to inform customers when their data is exposed.  
   **中文：** 客户数据遭到泄露时，我们有义务通知他们。

20. **oversee** /ˌoʊvərˈsiː/  
   **English definition:** to supervise a task, process, or group and make sure it is carried out properly  
   **中文释义：** 监督；监管；负责管理  
   **Example:** A senior engineer will oversee the safety inspection.  
   **中文：** 一位资深工程师将负责监督这次安全检查。

21. **premise** /ˈpremɪs/  
   **English definition:** an idea or statement accepted as the basis for an argument or plan  
   **中文释义：** 前提；假设；（论证或计划的）基础  
   **Example:** The proposal rests on the premise that demand will keep growing.  
   **中文：** 这项提案建立在需求将持续增长这一前提之上。

22. **prioritize** /praɪˈɔːrətaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to treat something as more important than other things and deal with it first  
   **中文释义：** 优先处理；确定优先次序  
   **Example:** We need to prioritize the repairs that affect public safety.  
   **中文：** 我们需要优先处理影响公共安全的维修工作。

23. **proactive** /ˌproʊˈæktɪv/  
   **English definition:** taking action in advance to prevent problems or create a desired result  
   **中文释义：** 积极主动的；先发制人的  
   **Example:** A proactive maintenance schedule can reduce unexpected downtime.  
   **中文：** 主动制定维护计划可以减少意外停机时间。

24. **safeguard** /ˈseɪfɡɑːrd/  
   **English definition:** a measure that protects something; to protect something from harm or loss  
   **中文释义：** 保障措施；保护；捍卫  
   **Example:** Two-factor authentication helps safeguard customer accounts.  
   **中文：** 双重验证有助于保护客户账户。

25. **threshold** /ˈθreʃhoʊld/  
   **English definition:** a level or point at which something begins, changes, or becomes true  
   **中文释义：** 门槛；界限；临界点  
   **Example:** Once sales pass the threshold, the project will become profitable.  
   **中文：** 销量一旦超过这个临界点，项目就会开始盈利。
