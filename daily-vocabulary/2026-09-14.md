# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-14

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abhor** /əbˈhɔːr/ — regard something with intense dislike or hatred；极其不喜欢或憎恨某事；憎恶
   - **Example:** Many people abhor cruelty to animals.
   - **中文：** 许多人憎恶虐待动物的行为。

2. **abrasive** /əˈbreɪsɪv/ — harsh or irritating in manner or language；在态度或语言上粗鲁、令人不快的；尖刻的
   - **Example:** His abrasive communication style caused unnecessary tension.
   - **中文：** 他尖刻的沟通方式造成了不必要的紧张。

3. **ambivalence** /æmˈbɪvələns/ — mixed or contradictory feelings about something；对某事复杂或矛盾的感受；矛盾心理
   - **Example:** She felt ambivalence about accepting the overseas assignment.
   - **中文：** 她对是否接受海外任务感到矛盾。

4. **antagonist** /ænˈtæɡənɪst/ — a person who opposes or competes with someone；反对或与某人竞争的人；对手；敌手
   - **Example:** The novel’s antagonist is more complex than he first appears.
   - **中文：** 这部小说中的反派比最初看起来更加复杂。

5. **arrogate** /ˈærəɡeɪt/ — take or claim something without having the right to do so；无权取得或声称拥有某物；擅自占有
   - **Example:** No department should arrogate to itself the authority to approve the entire budget.
   - **中文：** 任何部门都不应擅自把批准全部预算的权力据为己有。

6. **augment** /ɔːɡˈment/ — increase the size, value, or effectiveness of something；增加某物的规模、价值或效力；扩大；增强
   - **Example:** The company plans to augment its customer-support team.
   - **中文：** 公司计划扩大客户支持团队。

7. **bellicose** /ˈbelɪkoʊs/ — aggressive and eager to fight；好斗且急于争斗的；好战的
   - **Example:** The leader adopted a less bellicose tone during the negotiations.
   - **中文：** 谈判期间，这位领导采取了不那么好战的语气。

8. **consternation** /ˌkɑːnstərˈneɪʃən/ — a feeling of shock, worry, or confusion；震惊、担忧或困惑的感觉；惊愕；惊恐
   - **Example:** To the staff’s consternation, the project was canceled without warning.
   - **中文：** 项目毫无预警地被取消，让员工们十分惊愕。

9. **contradictory** /ˌkɑːntrəˈdɪktəri/ — containing statements or ideas that cannot both be true；包含不可能同时为真的陈述或观点的；矛盾的
   - **Example:** The witness gave contradictory accounts of what had happened.
   - **中文：** 证人对发生的事情作出了相互矛盾的陈述。

10. **copious** /ˈkoʊpiəs/ — abundant in quantity；数量丰富的；大量的
    - **Example:** The researcher took copious notes during the experiment.
    - **中文：** 研究人员在实验期间做了大量笔记。

11. **culminate** /ˈkʌlmɪneɪt/ — reach the highest point or final stage；达到最高点或最后阶段；达到顶点
    - **Example:** Months of preparation culminated in a successful public demonstration.
    - **中文：** 数月的准备最终以一次成功的公开演示达到高潮。

12. **debunk** /diːˈbʌŋk/ — expose a false idea or claim as untrue；揭露错误想法或说法是虚假的；揭穿
    - **Example:** The article debunks several myths about nutrition.
    - **中文：** 这篇文章揭穿了几个关于营养的误区。

13. **digress** /daɪˈɡres/ — move away from the main subject while speaking or writing；说话或写作时偏离主题；离题
    - **Example:** I will not digress from the main question during this presentation.
    - **中文：** 这次演讲中我不会偏离主要问题。

14. **discretion** /dɪˈskreʃən/ — careful judgment about what to do or say；对该做什么或说什么的谨慎判断；谨慎；裁量权
    - **Example:** Employees should use discretion when sharing confidential information.
    - **中文：** 员工分享机密信息时应当谨慎。

15. **disingenuous** /ˌdɪsɪnˈdʒenjuəs/ — not sincere or honest, especially while pretending to be so；不真诚或不诚实，尤其是装作真诚的；虚伪的
    - **Example:** It would be disingenuous to claim that the decision had no risks.
    - **中文：** 声称这个决定没有风险是不真诚的。

16. **dogged** /ˈdɔːɡɪd/ — showing persistent determination；表现出持续坚定决心的；顽强的
    - **Example:** Her dogged efforts eventually secured funding for the project.
    - **中文：** 她坚持不懈的努力最终为项目争取到了资金。

17. **duress** /duˈres/ — threats or pressure used to force someone to act；用来迫使某人行动的威胁或压力；胁迫
    - **Example:** The witness said that the agreement had been signed under duress.
    - **中文：** 证人说这份协议是在胁迫下签署的。

18. **edify** /ˈedɪfaɪ/ — improve someone morally, intellectually, or spiritually；在道德、智力或精神方面提升某人；启迪
    - **Example:** The lecture was designed to inform and edify the audience.
    - **中文：** 这场讲座旨在向听众传递知识并启迪他们。

19. **endeavor** /ɪnˈdevər/ — try hard to achieve something；努力实现某事；努力；尝试
    - **Example:** We will endeavor to respond to every request within two days.
    - **中文：** 我们将努力在两天内回复每一项请求。

20. **ensue** /ɪnˈsuː/ — happen after or as a result of something；在某事之后或因某事而发生；随之发生
    - **Example:** Confusion ensued after the instructions were changed at the last minute.
    - **中文：** 指示在最后一刻改变后，混乱随之发生。

21. **eradicate** /ɪˈrædɪkeɪt/ — destroy or remove something completely；彻底摧毁或清除某物；根除
    - **Example:** The campaign aims to eradicate the disease from the region.
    - **中文：** 这项运动旨在根除该地区的这种疾病。

22. **extraneous** /ɪkˈstreɪniəs/ — irrelevant or unnecessary to the matter；与事情无关或不必要的；无关的；多余的
    - **Example:** Remove extraneous details so that the main argument is clear.
    - **中文：** 删除无关细节，以便主要论点更加清晰。

23. **instigate** /ˈɪnstɪɡeɪt/ — encourage or provoke an action, especially a harmful one；鼓动或挑起某种行动，尤其是有害行动；煽动
    - **Example:** The group was accused of trying to instigate unrest.
    - **中文：** 这个团体被指责试图煽动动乱。

24. **prescient** /ˈpreʃənt/ — showing knowledge of events before they happen；在事件发生前就表现出预知能力的；有先见之明的
    - **Example:** Her prescient warning proved accurate a few months later.
    - **中文：** 她有先见之明的警告几个月后被证明是准确的。

25. **propensity** /prəˈpensəti/ — a natural tendency to behave in a particular way；以某种方式行事的自然倾向；倾向
    - **Example:** The device has a propensity to overheat when used continuously.
    - **中文：** 这台设备连续使用时容易过热。
