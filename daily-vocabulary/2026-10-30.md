# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-30

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **analog** /ˈænəlɔːɡ/  
   **English definition:** using continuously changing physical signals rather than digital values; also, something comparable to another thing  
   **中文释义：** 模拟的；类似物  
   **Example:** The studio still records some instruments on analog tape.  
   **中文：** 这间录音室仍会用模拟磁带录制某些乐器。

2. **apparatus** /ˌæpəˈrætəs/  
   **English definition:** a set of equipment used for a particular purpose  
   **中文释义：** 设备；仪器；装置  
   **Example:** The laboratory upgraded its safety apparatus before starting the experiment.  
   **中文：** 实验开始前，实验室升级了安全设备。

3. **audit** /ˈɔːdɪt/  
   **English definition:** an official examination of accounts, records, or procedures  
   **中文释义：** 审计；审核  
   **Example:** An independent audit found no major errors in the annual accounts.  
   **中文：** 独立审计没有在年度账目中发现重大错误。

4. **backbone** /ˈbækboʊn/  
   **English definition:** the main support or strength of a system or organization  
   **中文释义：** 支柱；骨干；核心支撑  
   **Example:** Small suppliers form the backbone of the region's food industry.  
   **中文：** 小型供应商构成了该地区食品行业的支柱。

5. **bandwidth** /ˈbændwɪdθ/  
   **English definition:** the amount of data a network can transmit, or the time and capacity available for a task  
   **中文释义：** 带宽；可用精力或处理能力  
   **Example:** I can help review the proposal, but I don't have the bandwidth to lead the project.  
   **中文：** 我可以帮忙审阅提案，但没有足够精力负责这个项目。

6. **blueprint** /ˈbluːprɪnt/  
   **English definition:** a detailed plan that shows how something will be designed or carried out  
   **中文释义：** 蓝图；详细计划  
   **Example:** The pilot program became a blueprint for expanding the service nationwide.  
   **中文：** 这项试点计划后来成为在全国推广该服务的蓝图。

7. **compensation** /ˌkɑːmpənˈseɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** money or another benefit given in return for work, loss, or injury  
   **中文释义：** 补偿；赔偿；报酬  
   **Example:** The airline offered compensation for the passengers' extended delay.  
   **中文：** 航空公司为乘客长时间延误提供了补偿。

8. **criterion** /kraɪˈtɪriən/  
   **English definition:** a standard used to judge or decide something  
   **中文释义：** 标准；准则  
   **Example:** Reliability is the main criterion we use when choosing a supplier.  
   **中文：** 选择供应商时，我们主要以可靠性作为评判标准。

9. **database** /ˈdeɪtəˌbeɪs/  
   **English definition:** an organized collection of information stored so it can be searched and used  
   **中文释义：** 数据库  
   **Example:** The clinic keeps appointment records in a secure database.  
   **中文：** 诊所将预约记录保存在安全的数据库中。

10. **deduction** /dɪˈdʌkʃən/  
    **English definition:** a conclusion reached by reasoning from evidence or known facts  
    **中文释义：** 推论；演绎结论  
    **Example:** From the muddy footprints, we made the deduction that someone had entered through the garden.  
    **中文：** 根据那些泥脚印，我们推断有人从花园进入了。

11. **deficiency** /dɪˈfɪʃənsi/  
    **English definition:** a lack of something necessary or desirable  
    **中文释义：** 缺乏；不足；缺陷  
    **Example:** The review identified a deficiency in the team's emergency training.  
    **中文：** 审查发现团队的应急培训存在不足。

12. **distortion** /dɪˈstɔːrʃən/  
    **English definition:** a change that makes something seem inaccurate or different from its original form  
    **中文释义：** 扭曲；失真；歪曲  
    **Example:** The summary was so brief that it created a distortion of the original argument.  
    **中文：** 这份摘要过于简短，以致歪曲了原论点。

13. **equity** /ˈekwəti/  
    **English definition:** fairness and impartial treatment, especially when people have different needs  
    **中文释义：** 公平；公正  
    **Example:** The policy aims to improve equity in access to health services.  
    **中文：** 这项政策旨在提升获得医疗服务机会的公平性。

14. **exploit** /ɪkˈsplɔɪt/  
    **English definition:** to use a resource or opportunity effectively; sometimes, to use someone unfairly for personal benefit  
    **中文释义：** 利用；开发；剥削  
    **Example:** The team exploited a brief window of good weather to finish the repairs.  
    **中文：** 团队利用短暂的好天气窗口完成了维修。

15. **fluctuation** /ˌflʌktʃuˈeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a rise and fall, or repeated change in level or amount  
    **中文释义：** 波动；起伏  
    **Example:** Seasonal fluctuations in demand make staffing difficult to plan.  
    **中文：** 需求的季节性波动让人员安排难以规划。

16. **fragile** /ˈfrædʒəl/  
    **English definition:** easily broken, damaged, or weakened  
    **中文释义：** 易碎的；脆弱的  
    **Example:** The package contains fragile glassware, so handle it carefully.  
    **中文：** 包裹里有易碎的玻璃器皿，请小心搬运。

17. **hierarchy** /ˈhaɪərɑːrki/  
    **English definition:** a system in which people or things are ranked by level or importance  
    **中文释义：** 层级；等级制度  
    **Example:** The company reduced its management hierarchy to speed up decisions.  
    **中文：** 公司精简了管理层级，以加快决策速度。

18. **holistic** /hoʊˈlɪstɪk/  
    **English definition:** considering a whole system or person rather than only separate parts  
    **中文释义：** 整体的；全面的  
    **Example:** A holistic approach considers sleep, nutrition, and stress together.  
    **中文：** 整体性的方法会把睡眠、营养和压力一并考虑。

19. **increment** /ˈɪŋkrəmənt/  
    **English definition:** a small amount by which something increases  
    **中文释义：** 增量；递增量  
    **Example:** The software saves each small increment of your progress automatically.  
    **中文：** 软件会自动保存每一小步的进度增量。

20. **indicator** /ˈɪndɪkeɪtər/  
    **English definition:** a sign or measurement that shows the condition or level of something  
    **中文释义：** 指标；迹象；指示器  
    **Example:** Customer return rates can be an indicator of product quality.  
    **中文：** 顾客退货率可以作为产品质量的一项指标。

21. **iterative** /ˈɪtəreɪtɪv/  
    **English definition:** involving repeated cycles of testing and improvement  
    **中文释义：** 反复迭代的；循序改进的  
    **Example:** The designers used an iterative process, testing a new version after each change.  
    **中文：** 设计师采用迭代流程，每次修改后都会测试新版本。

22. **merit** /ˈmerɪt/  
    **English definition:** a good quality that deserves attention or praise; to deserve something  
    **中文释义：** 优点；价值；值得  
    **Example:** The proposal has merit, although its costs need closer review.  
    **中文：** 这份提案有可取之处，不过仍需进一步审查成本。

23. **notion** /ˈnoʊʃən/  
    **English definition:** an idea, belief, or understanding about something  
    **中文释义：** 观念；想法；看法  
    **Example:** The data challenges the notion that remote workers are less productive.  
    **中文：** 这些数据对“远程工作者效率较低”这一看法提出了质疑。

24. **paradox** /ˈpærədɑːks/  
    **English definition:** a statement or situation that seems contradictory but may contain truth  
    **中文释义：** 悖论；看似矛盾的情况  
    **Example:** It is a paradox that having more choices can sometimes make people less satisfied.  
    **中文：** 选择更多有时反而让人更不满意，这看似矛盾却确实存在。

25. **prevalence** /ˈprevələns/  
    **English definition:** the fact or degree of being common in a particular place or group  
    **中文释义：** 普遍程度；流行率  
    **Example:** The survey measured the prevalence of food allergies among schoolchildren.  
    **中文：** 这项调查统计了学龄儿童中食物过敏的普遍程度。
