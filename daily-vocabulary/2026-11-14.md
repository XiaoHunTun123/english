# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-11-14

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **bargaining** /ˈbɑːrɡənɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** discussion between people or groups to reach an agreement, especially about price, pay, or conditions  
   **中文释义：** 谈判；协商；议价  
   **Example:** The union began bargaining with management over wages and working hours.  
   **中文：** 工会开始与管理层就工资和工作时间进行谈判。

2. **brittle** /ˈbrɪtəl/  
   **English definition:** hard but likely to break or crack under pressure  
   **中文释义：** 易碎的；脆弱的  
   **Example:** The plastic becomes brittle after years of exposure to sunlight.  
   **中文：** 这种塑料经过多年日晒后会变脆。

3. **civilian** /səˈvɪljən/  
   **English definition:** a person who is not a member of the armed forces or police  
   **中文释义：** 平民；文职人员  
   **Example:** The training teaches civilian staff how to respond to emergencies.  
   **中文：** 这项培训教文职员工如何应对紧急情况。

4. **clientele** /ˌklaɪənˈtel/  
   **English definition:** the customers or clients who regularly use a business or service  
   **中文释义：** 顾客群；客户群  
   **Example:** The bookstore has built a loyal clientele among local teachers.  
   **中文：** 这家书店在当地教师中建立了稳定的客户群。

5. **commute** /kəˈmjuːt/  
   **English definition:** to travel regularly between home and work or school  
   **中文释义：** 通勤；往返于工作地点和住处  
   **Example:** He commutes by train and uses the journey to catch up on reading.  
   **中文：** 他乘火车通勤，并利用路上的时间补读书。

6. **contempt** /kənˈtempt/  
   **English definition:** a lack of respect; in law, disobedience to or disrespect for a court  
   **中文释义：** 轻蔑；（法律）藐视法庭  
   **Example:** She was found in contempt of court for repeatedly ignoring the order.  
   **中文：** 她因多次无视法庭命令而被裁定藐视法庭。

7. **contractor** /ˈkɑːntræktər/  
   **English definition:** a person or company hired to do specific work under an agreement  
   **中文释义：** 承包商；合同工  
   **Example:** The city hired a contractor to repair the damaged bridge.  
   **中文：** 市政府聘请了承包商修复受损的桥梁。

8. **corrupt** /kəˈrʌpt/  
   **English definition:** dishonest, especially through bribery; also, damaged or changed in a harmful way  
   **中文释义：** 腐败的；贪污的；损坏的  
   **Example:** The audit exposed a corrupt scheme involving public contracts.  
   **中文：** 审计揭露了一起涉及公共合同的腐败计划。

9. **custody** /ˈkʌstədi/  
   **English definition:** legal care or control of a person or property; detention by authorities  
   **中文释义：** 监护权；保管；拘留  
   **Example:** The court granted the parents joint custody of their child.  
   **中文：** 法院判决父母共同拥有孩子的监护权。

10. **defendant** /dɪˈfendənt/  
    **English definition:** a person or organization accused or sued in a court of law  
    **中文释义：** 被告  
    **Example:** The defendant denied the allegation and requested a jury trial.  
    **中文：** 被告否认了指控，并要求由陪审团审理。

11. **disclosure** /dɪsˈkloʊʒər/  
    **English definition:** the act of making information known, especially information that was private  
    **中文释义：** 披露；公开；揭露  
    **Example:** The agreement requires full disclosure of any additional fees.  
    **中文：** 协议要求完整披露任何额外费用。

12. **dismissal** /dɪsˈmɪsəl/  
    **English definition:** the act of removing someone from a job or rejecting a claim or case  
    **中文释义：** 解雇；驳回  
    **Example:** The employee challenged his dismissal, arguing that the process was unfair.  
    **中文：** 这名员工质疑解雇决定，认为处理程序不公平。

13. **disposal** /dɪˈspoʊzəl/  
    **English definition:** the act of getting rid of something or placing it somewhere  
    **中文释义：** 处理；处置；丢弃  
    **Example:** Batteries should be taken to a collection point for safe disposal.  
    **中文：** 电池应送到回收点进行安全处理。

14. **disregard** /ˌdɪsrɪˈɡɑːrd/  
    **English definition:** to pay no attention to something or treat it as unimportant  
    **中文释义：** 忽视；不理会  
    **Example:** Drivers who disregard the warning signs risk damaging their vehicles.  
    **中文：** 无视警示标志的司机可能会损坏车辆。

15. **electoral** /ɪˈlektərəl/  
    **English definition:** relating to elections or the process of voting  
    **中文释义：** 选举的；选民的  
    **Example:** The reform would change how electoral districts are drawn.  
    **中文：** 这项改革将改变选区的划分方式。

16. **embassy** /ˈembəsi/  
    **English definition:** the official office and residence of a country's representatives in another country  
    **中文释义：** 大使馆  
    **Example:** Travelers were advised to contact their embassy during the unrest.  
    **中文：** 局势动荡期间，旅行者被建议联系本国大使馆。

17. **enforcement** /ɪnˈfɔːrsmənt/  
    **English definition:** the act of making sure that a law, rule, or agreement is obeyed  
    **中文释义：** 执行；实施；强制执行  
    **Example:** Consistent enforcement of parking rules reduced congestion downtown.  
    **中文：** 持续严格地执行停车规定，减少了市中心的拥堵。

18. **entrepreneur** /ˌɑːntrəprəˈnɜːr/  
    **English definition:** a person who starts and runs a business, accepting the risks involved  
    **中文释义：** 企业家；创业者  
    **Example:** The entrepreneur turned a small repair shop into an online business.  
    **中文：** 这位创业者把一家小维修店发展成了线上业务。

19. **erosion** /ɪˈroʊʒən/  
    **English definition:** the gradual wearing away of land or material; a slow reduction of something  
    **中文释义：** 侵蚀；削弱；逐渐减少  
    **Example:** Coastal erosion has forced several families to move inland.  
    **中文：** 海岸侵蚀迫使几户人家搬往内陆。

20. **ethics** /ˈeθɪks/  
    **English definition:** moral principles that guide behavior, especially in a profession  
    **中文释义：** 道德规范；伦理  
    **Example:** Researchers must follow strict ethics when collecting personal data.  
    **中文：** 研究人员收集个人数据时必须遵守严格的伦理规范。

21. **fiscal** /ˈfɪskəl/  
    **English definition:** relating to government revenue, taxes, or public spending  
    **中文释义：** 财政的；国库的  
    **Example:** The proposal will be reviewed as part of next year's fiscal plan.  
    **中文：** 这项提案将作为明年财政计划的一部分接受审查。

22. **fraud** /frɔːd/  
    **English definition:** the crime of deceiving someone in order to gain money or another benefit  
    **中文释义：** 欺诈；诈骗  
    **Example:** The bank added extra checks to prevent identity fraud.  
    **中文：** 银行增加了额外核查，以防止身份诈骗。

23. **fundraising** /ˈfʌndˌreɪzɪŋ/  
    **English definition:** the activity of collecting money for an organization or cause  
    **中文释义：** 筹款；募捐  
    **Example:** The school organized a fundraising event for its library.  
    **中文：** 学校为图书馆举办了一场筹款活动。

24. **harassment** /həˈræsmənt/  
    **English definition:** repeated behavior that troubles, intimidates, or harms another person  
    **中文释义：** 骚扰；侵扰  
    **Example:** Employees can report workplace harassment through a confidential channel.  
    **中文：** 员工可以通过保密渠道举报职场骚扰。

25. **hazard** /ˈhæzərd/  
    **English definition:** something that may cause injury, damage, or danger  
    **中文释义：** 危险；危害；隐患  
    **Example:** Wet floors are a common hazard in busy kitchens.  
    **中文：** 湿滑的地面是繁忙厨房里常见的安全隐患。
