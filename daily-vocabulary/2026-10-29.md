# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-29

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **abnormal** /æbˈnɔːrməl/  
   **English definition:** different from what is usual or expected, sometimes in a way that may be concerning  
   **中文释义：** 异常的；反常的  
   **Example:** The sensor sends an alert when it detects abnormal heat levels.  
   **中文：** 传感器检测到异常高温时会发出警报。

2. **absence** /ˈæbsəns/  
   **English definition:** the state of not being present or of something not existing  
   **中文释义：** 缺席；缺乏；不存在  
   **Example:** In the absence of clear instructions, the staff followed the usual procedure.  
   **中文：** 在没有明确指示的情况下，员工沿用了常规流程。

3. **accessory** /əkˈsesəri/  
   **English definition:** an extra item that is useful or decorative, but not essential  
   **中文释义：** 配件；附件；装饰品  
   **Example:** The camera comes with a carrying case, but the tripod is sold as an accessory.  
   **中文：** 这款相机附带便携包，但三脚架需作为配件另行购买。

4. **accordingly** /əˈkɔːrdɪŋli/  
   **English definition:** in a way that is suitable for a particular situation; therefore  
   **中文释义：** 相应地；因此  
   **Example:** The forecast changed, so we adjusted our route accordingly.  
   **中文：** 天气预报有变，所以我们相应地调整了路线。

5. **accuse** /əˈkjuːz/  
   **English definition:** to say that someone has done something wrong or illegal  
   **中文释义：** 指责；控告  
   **Example:** The report accused the company of ignoring repeated safety warnings.  
   **中文：** 报告指责该公司无视了多次安全警告。

6. **align** /əˈlaɪn/  
   **English definition:** to arrange things in a straight line, or bring plans and goals into agreement  
   **中文释义：** 排成一线；使一致；使协调  
   **Example:** The managers met to align their priorities for the next quarter.  
   **中文：** 经理们开会协调下一季度的工作重点。

7. **annual** /ˈænjuəl/  
   **English definition:** happening once every year  
   **中文释义：** 每年的；一年一次的  
   **Example:** The organization publishes an annual review of its projects.  
   **中文：** 该组织每年都会发布一份项目年度回顾。

8. **anxiety** /æŋˈzaɪəti/  
   **English definition:** a feeling of worry or nervousness about what may happen  
   **中文释义：** 焦虑；担忧  
   **Example:** Breaking the task into smaller steps helped ease her anxiety.  
   **中文：** 把任务拆分成更小的步骤，缓解了她的焦虑。

9. **appropriate** /əˈproʊpriət/  
   **English definition:** suitable or correct for a particular purpose, person, or situation  
   **中文释义：** 恰当的；合适的  
   **Example:** Please wear appropriate footwear for the rocky trail.  
   **中文：** 走这条多岩石的步道，请穿合适的鞋。

10. **assess** /əˈses/  
    **English definition:** to examine something and judge its quality, importance, or value  
    **中文释义：** 评估；评价  
    **Example:** The team will assess the damage before deciding how to proceed.  
    **中文：** 团队会先评估损失，再决定如何继续。

11. **attitude** /ˈætɪtuːd/  
    **English definition:** the way a person thinks, feels, or behaves toward something  
    **中文释义：** 态度；看法  
    **Example:** A flexible attitude can make collaboration much easier.  
    **中文：** 灵活的态度能让合作顺利得多。

12. **authority** /əˈθɔːrəti/  
    **English definition:** the power or right to make decisions and give instructions  
    **中文释义：** 权力；职权  
    **Example:** Only the site supervisor has the authority to stop the work.  
    **中文：** 只有现场主管有权叫停这项工作。

13. **circumstance** /ˈsɜːrkəmstæns/  
    **English definition:** a fact or condition that affects a situation  
    **中文释义：** 情况；环境；情形  
    **Example:** Under normal circumstances, delivery takes two business days.  
    **中文：** 正常情况下，配送需要两个工作日。

14. **conflict** /ˈkɑːnflɪkt/  
    **English definition:** a serious disagreement or a situation in which needs or goals cannot easily fit together  
    **中文释义：** 冲突；矛盾；争执  
    **Example:** There is a conflict between the meeting time and her flight schedule.  
    **中文：** 会议时间与她的航班安排有冲突。

15. **construct** /kənˈstrʌkt/  
    **English definition:** to build something, or form it by putting parts together  
    **中文释义：** 建造；构建；构成  
    **Example:** Researchers constructed a model to explain how the system works.  
    **中文：** 研究人员构建了一个模型来解释该系统的运行方式。

16. **consume** /kənˈsuːm/  
    **English definition:** to use a resource, such as time or energy; to eat or drink something  
    **中文释义：** 消耗；耗费；食用  
    **Example:** The old machine consumes twice as much electricity as the new one.  
    **中文：** 这台旧机器的耗电量是新机器的两倍。

17. **convention** /kənˈvenʃən/  
    **English definition:** a widely accepted way of behaving or doing something  
    **中文释义：** 惯例；习俗；常规  
    **Example:** By convention, the date is written before the month in this format.  
    **中文：** 按照惯例，这种格式先写日期，再写月份。

18. **decline** /dɪˈklaɪn/  
    **English definition:** to become smaller, weaker, or less; to politely refuse something  
    **中文释义：** 下降；减少；谢绝  
    **Example:** Membership began to decline after the local office closed.  
    **中文：** 当地办事处关闭后，会员人数开始减少。

19. **dimension** /dɪˈmenʃən/  
    **English definition:** a measurable size of an object, or one aspect of a situation  
    **中文释义：** 尺寸；方面；维度  
    **Example:** Cost is only one dimension of the decision; reliability matters too.  
    **中文：** 成本只是决策的一个方面，可靠性也很重要。

20. **distribute** /dɪˈstrɪbjuːt/  
    **English definition:** to give or deliver things to a number of people or places  
    **中文释义：** 分发；分配；配送  
    **Example:** Volunteers distributed clean water to households affected by the flood.  
    **中文：** 志愿者向受洪水影响的住户分发了饮用水。

21. **exclude** /ɪkˈskluːd/  
    **English definition:** to deliberately leave someone or something out, or prevent them from taking part  
    **中文释义：** 排除；不包括；阻止参与  
    **Example:** The estimate excludes installation and maintenance costs.  
    **中文：** 这份估算不包括安装和维护费用。

22. **fundamental** /ˌfʌndəˈmentəl/  
    **English definition:** basic and essential; forming the foundation of something  
    **中文释义：** 基本的；根本的；基础的  
    **Example:** Trust is fundamental to a productive working relationship.  
    **中文：** 信任是建立高效工作关系的根本。

23. **implicate** /ˈɪmplɪkeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to show or suggest that someone is involved in a crime or problem  
    **中文释义：** 暗示或证明（某人）牵涉其中  
    **Example:** The newly discovered emails may implicate several senior officials.  
    **中文：** 新发现的邮件可能表明几名高级官员也牵涉其中。

24. **legislate** /ˈledʒɪsleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to make or pass laws  
    **中文释义：** 制定法律；立法  
    **Example:** The government plans to legislate stricter rules for data protection.  
    **中文：** 政府计划立法制定更严格的数据保护规则。

25. **neutral** /ˈnuːtrəl/  
    **English definition:** not supporting either side in a disagreement; not strongly marked by one quality  
    **中文释义：** 中立的；中性的  
    **Example:** The mediator remained neutral while both sides explained their concerns.  
    **中文：** 双方陈述各自的顾虑时，调解员始终保持中立。
