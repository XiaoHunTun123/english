# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-26

25 个偏实用、略有挑战度的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **accompany** /əˈkʌmpəni/  
   **English definition:** to go somewhere with someone, or to happen together with something  
   **中文释义：** 陪同；伴随  
   **Example:** A short explanation accompanied each chart in the report.  
   **中文：** 报告中的每张图表都附有简短说明。

2. **addiction** /əˈdɪkʃən/  
   **English definition:** a strong and harmful need to regularly do or use something  
   **中文释义：** 成瘾；瘾  
   **Example:** The documentary explores how phone addiction can affect sleep.  
   **中文：** 这部纪录片探讨了手机成瘾会如何影响睡眠。

3. **adolescent** /ˌædəˈlesənt/  
   **English definition:** a young person who is developing from a child into an adult  
   **中文释义：** 青少年；青春期的  
   **Example:** The program offers counseling for adolescents under stress.  
   **中文：** 该项目为承受压力的青少年提供心理咨询。

4. **aggression** /əˈɡreʃən/  
   **English definition:** angry or threatening behavior, or the act of attacking  
   **中文释义：** 挑衅；攻击性；侵略  
   **Example:** The coach asked the players to compete with confidence, not aggression.  
   **中文：** 教练要求队员自信地比赛，而不是带着攻击性。

5. **agriculture** /ˈæɡrɪˌkʌltʃər/  
   **English definition:** the practice and business of growing crops and raising animals  
   **中文释义：** 农业  
   **Example:** New irrigation methods have helped agriculture in the dry region.  
   **中文：** 新的灌溉方法帮助干旱地区发展农业。

6. **appreciate** /əˈpriːʃieɪt/  
   **English definition:** to recognize the value of something or feel grateful for it  
   **中文释义：** 欣赏；理解；感激  
   **Example:** I appreciate your patience while we resolve the issue.  
   **中文：** 在我们解决问题期间，感谢你的耐心。

7. **artificial** /ˌɑːrtɪˈfɪʃəl/  
   **English definition:** made by people rather than occurring naturally  
   **中文释义：** 人造的；人工的  
   **Example:** The garden uses artificial light to grow herbs indoors.  
   **中文：** 这座花园使用人造光在室内种植香草。

8. **assault** /əˈsɔːlt/  
   **English definition:** a physical attack, or a serious attempt to harm someone  
   **中文释义：** 袭击；攻击  
   **Example:** The suspect was charged with assault after the argument.  
   **中文：** 争执发生后，嫌疑人被控袭击罪。

9. **astonish** /əˈstɑːnɪʃ/  
   **English definition:** to surprise someone greatly  
   **中文释义：** 使十分惊讶；使震惊  
   **Example:** Her ability to recall details never fails to astonish me.  
   **中文：** 她回忆细节的能力总让我惊叹不已。

10. **awkward** /ˈɔːkwərd/  
    **English definition:** uncomfortable or embarrassing; difficult to use or handle  
    **中文释义：** 尴尬的；棘手的；笨拙的  
    **Example:** There was an awkward silence after the unexpected question.  
    **中文：** 那个出乎意料的问题之后，现场陷入了尴尬的沉默。

11. **bizarre** /bɪˈzɑːr/  
    **English definition:** very strange or unusual  
    **中文释义：** 古怪的；离奇的  
    **Example:** We heard a bizarre story about a piano found in the forest.  
    **中文：** 我们听说了一个离奇的故事：有人在森林里发现了一架钢琴。

12. **blunt** /blʌnt/  
    **English definition:** very direct, sometimes in a way that may seem rude; not sharp  
    **中文释义：** 直率得近乎生硬的；钝的  
    **Example:** She gave me a blunt but useful assessment of the proposal.  
    **中文：** 她对这份提案作了直截了当但很有用的评价。

13. **boast** /boʊst/  
    **English definition:** to speak too proudly about something you have or have done  
    **中文释义：** 吹嘘；自夸  
    **Example:** The restaurant boasts a terrace with views of the harbor.  
    **中文：** 这家餐厅以拥有可眺望港口的露台为特色。

14. **bracket** /ˈbrækɪt/  
    **English definition:** one of a pair of marks used to enclose words or numbers; a category or range  
    **中文释义：** 括号；档次；区间  
    **Example:** Put the extra details in brackets so the main sentence stays clear.  
    **中文：** 把补充细节放在括号里，这样主句会更清楚。

15. **browse** /braʊz/  
    **English definition:** to look through items or information casually, without a fixed plan  
    **中文释义：** 浏览；随意翻看  
    **Example:** I browsed the bookstore while I waited for my train.  
    **中文：** 等火车时，我随意逛了逛书店。

16. **campaign** /kæmˈpeɪn/  
    **English definition:** an organized series of actions intended to achieve a particular goal  
    **中文释义：** 专项行动；（竞选或宣传）活动  
    **Example:** The city launched a campaign to reduce food waste.  
    **中文：** 这座城市发起了一项减少食物浪费的行动。

17. **casualty** /ˈkæʒuəlti/  
    **English definition:** a person injured or killed in an accident, war, or disaster  
    **中文释义：** 伤亡人员；（事故或灾难中的）受害者  
    **Example:** Fortunately, the storm caused damage but no casualties.  
    **中文：** 幸运的是，风暴造成了损失，但没有人员伤亡。

18. **cater** /ˈkeɪtər/  
    **English definition:** to provide food for an event; to meet a particular need or preference  
    **中文释义：** 承办餐饮；满足（需求或喜好）  
    **Example:** The course caters to beginners as well as experienced users.  
    **中文：** 这门课程既适合初学者，也适合有经验的使用者。

19. **certificate** /sərˈtɪfɪkət/  
    **English definition:** an official document that proves a fact, achievement, or qualification  
    **中文释义：** 证书；证明  
    **Example:** You will receive a certificate after completing the training.  
    **中文：** 完成培训后，你将获得一份证书。

20. **chaos** /ˈkeɪɑːs/  
    **English definition:** a state of complete confusion and lack of order  
    **中文释义：** 混乱；无秩序状态  
    **Example:** A clear schedule helped prevent chaos on moving day.  
    **中文：** 一份清晰的日程安排避免了搬家当天陷入混乱。

21. **charter** /ˈtʃɑːrtər/  
    **English definition:** an official document that establishes an organization or its rights; to hire a vehicle for a special journey  
    **中文释义：** 宪章；特许状；包租  
    **Example:** The group charter sets out its purpose and decision-making rules.  
    **中文：** 该团体的章程阐明了宗旨和决策规则。

22. **circuit** /ˈsɜːrkɪt/  
    **English definition:** a complete route around a place; a closed path through which electricity flows  
    **中文释义：** 环行路线；电路  
    **Example:** We walked a five-kilometer circuit around the lake.  
    **中文：** 我们绕湖走了一圈，全程五公里。

23. **circulate** /ˈsɜːrkjəleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to move around or through a place, or to distribute something among people  
    **中文释义：** 循环；流通；传阅  
    **Example:** Please circulate the updated agenda before tomorrow's meeting.  
    **中文：** 请在明天开会前把更新后的议程传阅给大家。

24. **clarity** /ˈklærəti/  
    **English definition:** the quality of being clear and easy to understand  
    **中文释义：** 清晰；明晰  
    **Example:** Adding headings improved the clarity of the instructions.  
    **中文：** 添加标题后，说明内容变得更清晰了。

25. **coalition** /ˌkoʊəˈlɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a temporary alliance of groups or people working toward a shared goal  
    **中文释义：** 联盟；联合体  
    **Example:** Several local organizations formed a coalition to protect the wetlands.  
    **中文：** 几个当地组织组成联盟，共同保护湿地。
