# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-24

以下 25 个词汇兼顾实用性与拓展性，适合阅读、新闻和工作表达。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **accusation** /ˌækjəˈzeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a claim that someone has done something wrong or illegal  
   **中文释义：** 指责；指控  
   **Example:** The official rejected the accusation and asked for the evidence to be reviewed.  
   **中文：** 这名官员否认了指控，并要求重新审查证据。

2. **alongside** /əˌlɔːŋˈsaɪd/  
   **English definition:** next to someone or something; working together with  
   **中文释义：** 在……旁边；与……一起  
   **Example:** The new guide will be published alongside the updated application form.  
   **中文：** 新指南将与更新后的申请表一同发布。

3. **analyst** /ˈænəlɪst/  
   **English definition:** someone whose job is to examine information and explain what it means  
   **中文释义：** 分析师；分析人员  
   **Example:** An analyst compared the figures across several reporting periods.  
   **中文：** 一位分析师比较了几个报告期的数据。

4. **backdrop** /ˈbækdrɑːp/  
   **English definition:** the general situation or conditions in which something happens; a background scene  
   **中文释义：** 背景；背景环境  
   **Example:** The agreement was reached against a backdrop of rising costs.  
   **中文：** 这项协议是在成本不断上升的背景下达成的。

5. **beverage** /ˈbevərɪdʒ/  
   **English definition:** a drink, especially one other than water  
   **中文释义：** 饮料  
   **Example:** The venue offers hot beverages and light meals throughout the day.  
   **中文：** 场地全天供应热饮和简餐。

6. **bureau** /ˈbjʊroʊ/  
   **English definition:** a government department or an office that provides a particular service  
   **中文释义：** 局；处；办事机构  
   **Example:** The bureau released revised guidance for local authorities.  
   **中文：** 该局发布了面向地方政府的修订版指南。

7. **cautionary** /ˈkɔːʃəneri/  
   **English definition:** intended to warn people about a possible danger or mistake  
   **中文释义：** 警示性的；告诫性的  
   **Example:** The report offers a cautionary example of what can happen without regular backups.  
   **中文：** 这份报告以一个警示案例说明了不定期备份可能带来的后果。

8. **census** /ˈsensəs/  
   **English definition:** an official count of a population, usually including information about its members  
   **中文释义：** 人口普查；统计调查  
   **Example:** Census data helps planners estimate demand for schools and public transport.  
   **中文：** 人口普查数据有助于规划人员估算学校和公共交通的需求。

9. **citation** /saɪˈteɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a reference to a source of information; an official notice or award  
   **中文释义：** 引文；引用出处；正式表彰或传票  
   **Example:** Every claim in the paper should have a reliable citation.  
   **中文：** 论文中的每项论断都应附有可靠的引用出处。

10. **clause** /klɔːz/  
    **English definition:** a particular part of a legal document or agreement  
    **中文释义：** 条款；从句  
    **Example:** A confidentiality clause protects information shared during the review.  
    **中文：** 保密条款能保护审查期间共享的信息。

11. **closure** /ˈkloʊʒər/  
    **English definition:** the act of closing something; a feeling that an unresolved matter has been settled  
    **中文释义：** 关闭；结束；了结  
    **Example:** The investigation brought closure to a case that had remained open for years.  
    **中文：** 这次调查为一桩多年未结的案件画上了句号。

12. **cluster** /ˈklʌstər/  
    **English definition:** a group of similar things or people located close together  
    **中文释义：** 群；簇；聚集的一组  
    **Example:** A cluster of small firms has formed around the research center.  
    **中文：** 研究中心周边聚集了一批小型企业。

13. **collaborative** /kəˈlæbəreɪtɪv/  
    **English definition:** involving people or groups working together toward a shared result  
    **中文释义：** 合作的；协作的  
    **Example:** The course uses a collaborative approach in which participants solve problems in teams.  
    **中文：** 这门课程采用协作式教学，让学员以团队形式解决问题。

14. **commerce** /ˈkɑːmɜːrs/  
    **English definition:** the activity of buying and selling goods and services, especially on a large scale  
    **中文释义：** 商业；贸易  
    **Example:** Digital tools have changed how small businesses conduct commerce.  
    **中文：** 数字工具改变了小型企业开展商业活动的方式。

15. **compliance** /kəmˈplaɪəns/  
    **English definition:** the act of following a rule, law, standard, or request  
    **中文释义：** 遵守；合规  
    **Example:** The audit checks the company's compliance with privacy requirements.  
    **中文：** 这次审计检查公司是否遵守隐私要求。

16. **complication** /ˌkɑːmplɪˈkeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a problem or new difficulty that makes a situation more complicated  
    **中文释义：** 难题；复杂情况；并发症  
    **Example:** A delayed shipment created an unexpected complication for the launch.  
    **中文：** 延迟发货给产品发布带来了意料之外的难题。

17. **conceal** /kənˈsiːl/  
    **English definition:** to hide something or keep it from being known  
    **中文释义：** 隐藏；隐瞒  
    **Example:** The revised summary should explain the findings rather than conceal important limitations.  
    **中文：** 修订后的摘要应解释研究结果，而不是隐瞒重要局限。

18. **concrete** /ˈkɑːnkriːt/  
    **English definition:** specific and real rather than general or theoretical; also, a hard building material  
    **中文释义：** 具体的；明确的；混凝土  
    **Example:** The proposal includes concrete steps for reducing energy use.  
    **中文：** 这份提案列出了降低能耗的具体措施。

19. **conservation** /ˌkɑːnsərˈveɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the protection of natural resources, places, or wildlife from damage or loss  
    **中文释义：** 保护；保存；（自然资源或野生生物）保护  
    **Example:** The conservation program restored wetlands used by migrating birds.  
    **中文：** 这项保护计划修复了候鸟栖息的湿地。

20. **crackdown** /ˈkrækdaʊn/  
    **English definition:** strong official action taken to stop illegal or unwanted activity  
    **中文释义：** 严厉打击；强力整顿  
    **Example:** The city announced a crackdown on illegal dumping.  
    **中文：** 市政府宣布严厉打击非法倾倒垃圾的行为。

21. **credibility** /ˌkredəˈbɪləti/  
    **English definition:** the quality of being trusted and believed  
    **中文释义：** 可信度；公信力  
    **Example:** Transparent methods strengthened the credibility of the study.  
    **中文：** 透明的方法提高了这项研究的可信度。

22. **debris** /dəˈbriː/  
    **English definition:** scattered pieces of waste or broken material left after damage or an accident  
    **中文释义：** 碎片；残骸；瓦砾  
    **Example:** Crews cleared debris from the road after the storm.  
    **中文：** 暴风雨过后，工作人员清理了道路上的碎片。

23. **dampen** /ˈdæmpən/  
    **English definition:** to make something slightly wet; to reduce the strength or effect of something  
    **中文释义：** 弄湿；减弱；抑制  
    **Example:** Higher costs could dampen demand for nonessential products.  
    **中文：** 成本上升可能会抑制对非必需品的需求。

24. **defect** /ˈdiːfekt/  
    **English definition:** a fault or imperfection that makes something incomplete or less effective  
    **中文释义：** 缺陷；瑕疵；故障  
    **Example:** The inspection found a minor defect in the cable insulation.  
    **中文：** 检查发现电缆绝缘层有一处轻微缺陷。

25. **depict** /dɪˈpɪkt/  
    **English definition:** to represent someone or something in a picture, story, or description  
    **中文释义：** 描绘；描述；刻画  
    **Example:** The chart depicts changes in household energy use over ten years.  
    **中文：** 这张图表描绘了十年来家庭能源使用的变化。
