# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-06

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **adduce** /əˈduːs/  
   **English definition:** to provide evidence or an example to support an argument  
   **中文释义：** 提出证据；举出例证  
   **Example:** The report adduces several studies to support its conclusion.  
   **中文：** 这份报告列举了多项研究来支持其结论。

2. **adjunct** /ˈædʒʌŋkt/  
   **English definition:** something added to another thing as a useful but less important part  
   **中文释义：** 附件；辅助物；附属部分  
   **Example:** The online course is offered as an adjunct to the university’s regular program.  
   **中文：** 这门在线课程作为该大学常规项目的辅助课程提供。

3. **adumbration** /ˌædʌmˈbreɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a general outline or vague indication of something  
   **中文释义：** 概略；模糊的暗示  
   **Example:** The opening chapter provides an adumbration of the conflict that follows.  
   **中文：** 开篇一章概略暗示了后续发生的冲突。

4. **aesthete** /ˈiːsθiːt/  
   **English definition:** a person who has a strong interest in beauty and artistic experiences  
   **中文释义：** 唯美主义者；审美鉴赏家  
   **Example:** As an aesthete, she pays close attention to the design of everyday objects.  
   **中文：** 作为一名审美鉴赏家，她非常关注日常物品的设计。

5. **agape** /əˈɡeɪp/  
   **English definition:** with the mouth open, especially in surprise or amazement  
   **中文释义：** 目瞪口呆地；张大嘴的  
   **Example:** The audience sat agape as the performer completed the dangerous stunt.  
   **中文：** 表演者完成危险特技时，观众们目瞪口呆地坐在那里。

6. **ameliorative** /əˈmiːliəreɪtɪv/  
   **English definition:** intended to improve a bad or difficult situation  
   **中文释义：** 改善性的；缓解问题的  
   **Example:** The committee proposed several ameliorative measures for low-income families.  
   **中文：** 委员会为低收入家庭提出了几项改善性措施。

7. **aphelion** /æfˈiːliən/  
   **English definition:** the point in an orbit at which an object is farthest from the Sun  
   **中文释义：** 远日点  
   **Example:** The planet moves most slowly when it reaches aphelion.  
   **中文：** 行星到达远日点时运行速度最慢。

8. **apologist** /əˈpɑːlədʒɪst/  
   **English definition:** a person who publicly defends a particular belief, policy, or person  
   **中文释义：** 辩护者；拥护者  
   **Example:** He is a strong apologist for public investment in scientific research.  
   **中文：** 他是公共投资科学研究的坚定拥护者。

9. **appellation** /ˌæpəˈleɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a name or title used to identify a person, place, or thing  
   **中文释义：** 名称；称号；称呼  
   **Example:** The appellation “green city” has become central to the town’s identity.  
   **中文：** “绿色城市”这一称号已经成为该城镇身份的重要部分。

10. **appertain** /ˌæpərˈteɪn/  
    **English definition:** to relate or belong to something  
    **中文释义：** 与……有关；属于  
    **Example:** The rules appertaining to professional conduct are listed in the handbook.  
    **中文：** 与职业操守有关的规则列在手册中。

11. **apropos** /ˌæprəˈpoʊ/  
    **English definition:** relevant or appropriate to what is being discussed  
    **中文释义：** 恰当的；相关的  
    **Example:** Her remark was apropos because the team was discussing customer complaints.  
    **中文：** 她的话很恰当，因为团队当时正在讨论客户投诉。

12. **arbiter** /ˈɑːrbɪtər/  
    **English definition:** a person or organization with the power to make a judgment or settle a dispute  
    **中文释义：** 仲裁者；裁决者  
    **Example:** The court became the final arbiter of the constitutional dispute.  
    **中文：** 法院成为这场宪法争议的最终裁决者。

13. **bonhomie** /ˌbɑːnəˈmiː/  
    **English definition:** a friendly and cheerful manner  
    **中文释义：** 友善；和蔼；融洽气氛  
    **Example:** The host’s bonhomie helped bridge the differences between the guests.  
    **中文：** 主人的友善态度帮助弥合了客人之间的分歧。

14. **brouhaha** /ˈbruːhɑːhɑː/  
    **English definition:** a noisy and confused reaction to something, often involving public argument  
    **中文释义：** 喧闹；大惊小怪；轩然大波  
    **Example:** The minor scheduling error caused an unnecessary brouhaha online.  
    **中文：** 一个小小的日程错误在网上引发了不必要的轩然大波。

15. **celerity** /səˈlerəti/  
    **English definition:** great quickness or speed  
    **中文释义：** 迅速；敏捷  
    **Example:** The emergency team acted with remarkable celerity.  
    **中文：** 应急团队以惊人的速度采取了行动。

16. **codify** /ˈkɑːdɪfaɪ/  
    **English definition:** to arrange rules or principles into a systematic code  
    **中文释义：** 编纂成法典；系统整理规则  
    **Example:** The government plans to codify the regulations in a single document.  
    **中文：** 政府计划把这些规定编纂到一份文件中。

17. **condescend** /ˌkɑːndɪˈsend/  
    **English definition:** to behave as if you are superior to someone; to lower yourself to do something  
    **中文释义：** 居高临下；屈尊  
    **Example:** The manager should explain the process clearly instead of condescending to new employees.  
    **中文：** 经理应该清楚解释流程，而不是对新员工摆出居高临下的态度。

18. **confabulate** /kənˈfæbjəleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to talk casually; to invent details without intending to deceive, especially when recalling something  
    **中文释义：** 闲谈；无意编造细节  
    **Example:** The two researchers confabulated over coffee before the seminar began.  
    **中文：** 研讨会开始前，两位研究人员一边喝咖啡一边闲谈。

19. **confound** /kənˈfaʊnd/  
    **English definition:** to confuse someone or prove something unexpectedly wrong  
    **中文释义：** 使困惑；使出乎意料地失败  
    **Example:** The results confounded the researchers’ original expectations.  
    **中文：** 结果出乎研究人员的原本预期。

20. **contravene** /ˌkɑːntrəˈviːn/  
    **English definition:** to violate a law, rule, or agreement  
    **中文释义：** 违反；违背  
    **Example:** The construction project was halted because it contravened environmental regulations.  
    **中文：** 该建筑项目因违反环保法规而被叫停。

21. **cosset** /ˈkɑːsɪt/  
    **English definition:** to treat someone with excessive care and attention  
    **中文释义：** 溺爱；娇惯；过分呵护  
    **Example:** The organization should support new staff without cosseting them.  
    **中文：** 组织应该支持新员工，但不应过分娇惯他们。

22. **dudgeon** /ˈdʌdʒən/  
    **English definition:** a feeling of anger or offense, often shown through proud silence  
    **中文释义：** 愤怒；怨气；恼怒  
    **Example:** He left the meeting in high dudgeon after his proposal was dismissed.  
    **中文：** 他的提案被否决后，他满腔恼怒地离开了会议。

23. **epiphany** /ɪˈpɪfəni/  
    **English definition:** a sudden moment of understanding or insight  
    **中文释义：** 顿悟；突然的领悟  
    **Example:** She had an epiphany while reviewing the data and found the source of the error.  
    **中文：** 她检查数据时突然顿悟，找到了错误的来源。

24. **ersatz** /ˈersɑːts/  
    **English definition:** made as a substitute for something better or more valuable, usually of inferior quality  
    **中文释义：** 代用品的；劣质替代的  
    **Example:** The critics dismissed the product as an ersatz version of a well-known design.  
    **中文：** 评论家认为这款产品只是知名设计的劣质替代品。

25. **euphony** /ˈjuːfəni/  
    **English definition:** a pleasing combination of sounds  
    **中文释义：** 悦耳的声音；和谐音调  
    **Example:** The poem is admired for the euphony of its carefully chosen words.  
    **中文：** 这首诗因精心选词所形成的悦耳音调而受到赞赏。
