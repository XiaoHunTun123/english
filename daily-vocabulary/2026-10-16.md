# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-16

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **analytical** /ˌænəˈlɪtɪkəl/  
   **English definition:** using careful examination and logical thinking to understand something  
   **中文释义：** 分析的；善于分析的  
   **Example:** An analytical approach helped the team find the source of recurring delays.  
   **中文：** 分析性的方法帮助团队找到了反复延误的根源。

2. **aspire** /əˈspaɪr/  
   **English definition:** to hope or work toward achieving something important or challenging  
   **中文释义：** 渴望；立志；追求  
   **Example:** After working in the field, she aspired to lead her own research group.  
   **中文：** 在该领域工作之后，她立志领导自己的研究团队。

3. **capture** /ˈkæptʃər/  
   **English definition:** to record, represent, or preserve something, such as information or an image  
   **中文释义：** 记录；捕捉；呈现  
   **Example:** The minutes capture the main decisions but omit the debate.  
   **中文：** 会议记录记下了主要决定，但省略了讨论过程。

4. **caution** /ˈkɔːʃən/  
   **English definition:** to warn someone to take care or avoid a particular risk  
   **中文释义：** 提醒谨慎；警告  
   **Example:** The notice cautions visitors against using the trail after heavy rain.  
   **中文：** 公告提醒访客，大雨过后不要走这条步道。

5. **commemorate** /kəˈmeməreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to honor or remember an important person or event, often with a ceremony or object  
   **中文释义：** 纪念；庆祝（重要事件）  
   **Example:** The city planted trees to commemorate the neighborhood’s centennial.  
   **中文：** 为纪念这个街区建成一百周年，市里种下了树木。

6. **compel** /kəmˈpel/  
   **English definition:** to force or strongly persuade someone to do something  
   **中文释义：** 迫使；强烈促使  
   **Example:** The evidence compelled the board to reopen the inquiry.  
   **中文：** 这些证据促使董事会重新启动调查。

7. **distort** /dɪˈstɔːrt/  
   **English definition:** to change or present something in a way that makes it inaccurate or misleading  
   **中文释义：** 歪曲；扭曲；使失真  
   **Example:** Changing the graph’s scale could distort the comparison.  
   **中文：** 改变图表的刻度可能会使比较结果失真。

8. **embrace** /ɪmˈbreɪs/  
   **English definition:** to accept an idea or change willingly and with enthusiasm  
   **中文释义：** 欣然接受；拥抱（变化或理念）  
   **Example:** The library embraced a flexible booking system to serve evening readers.  
   **中文：** 图书馆采用了灵活的预约系统，以方便晚间来馆的读者。

9. **emerge** /ɪˈmɜːrdʒ/  
   **English definition:** to become visible, known, or understood after being hidden or unclear  
   **中文释义：** 显现；出现；显露出来  
   **Example:** A clearer pattern emerged after the researchers grouped responses by age.  
   **中文：** 研究人员按年龄对回复进行分组后，规律变得更加清晰。

10. **emphasize** /ˈemfəsaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to give special importance or attention to something  
   **中文释义：** 强调；着重指出  
   **Example:** The guide emphasizes checking sources before sharing claims.  
   **中文：** 这份指南强调，转发说法之前要先核查来源。

11. **enact** /ɪˈnækt/  
   **English definition:** to make a proposal or rule official, especially by passing it into law  
   **中文释义：** 颁布；制定并使（法律或规定）生效  
   **Example:** The council enacted new rules for short-term rentals.  
   **中文：** 市议会颁布了有关短期租赁的新规定。

12. **enrich** /ɪnˈrɪtʃ/  
   **English definition:** to improve the quality, value, or depth of something  
   **中文释义：** 丰富；充实；提升价值  
   **Example:** Local interviews enriched the documentary with firsthand accounts.  
   **中文：** 当地访谈为这部纪录片增添了亲历者的讲述。

13. **equate** /ɪˈkweɪt/  
   **English definition:** to consider two things equal or treat them as if they were the same  
   **中文释义：** 将……等同；认为……相同  
   **Example:** We shouldn’t equate a high view count with public trust.  
   **中文：** 我们不应把高浏览量等同于公众信任。

14. **excessive** /ɪkˈsesɪv/  
   **English definition:** greater than what is reasonable, necessary, or appropriate  
   **中文释义：** 过度的；过多的；过分的  
   **Example:** Excessive packaging increases both cost and waste.  
   **中文：** 过度包装会同时增加成本和浪费。

15. **filter** /ˈfɪltər/  
   **English definition:** to remove unwanted parts or select what is useful from a larger amount  
   **中文释义：** 过滤；筛选；滤除  
   **Example:** The app filters out duplicate alerts before sending a summary.  
   **中文：** 该应用会先滤除重复提醒，再发送摘要。

16. **grasp** /ɡræsp/  
   **English definition:** to understand something fully, or to take hold of it firmly  
   **中文释义：** 理解；领会；抓牢  
   **Example:** It took me a while to grasp why the figures changed after adjustment.  
   **中文：** 我花了一些时间才理解为什么调整后数字会发生变化。

17. **modest** /ˈmɑːdɪst/  
   **English definition:** not large, extreme, or expensive; moderate in size or amount  
   **中文释义：** 适度的；不大的；朴素的  
   **Example:** A modest investment in insulation cut heating costs.  
   **中文：** 适度增加隔热投入降低了供暖费用。

18. **omit** /oʊˈmɪt/  
   **English definition:** to leave something out, either deliberately or by accident  
   **中文释义：** 省略；遗漏；不包括  
   **Example:** The summary omits details that do not affect the recommendation.  
   **中文：** 摘要省略了不影响建议的细节。

19. **overlap** /ˌoʊvərˈlæp/  
   **English definition:** to partly cover the same area, include some of the same things, or occur at the same time  
   **中文释义：** 重叠；部分相同；时间上交叉  
   **Example:** The two workshops overlap by half an hour, so choose one.  
   **中文：** 两场工作坊有半小时的时间重叠，所以选一场参加即可。

20. **prevail** /prɪˈveɪl/  
   **English definition:** to be successful or prove stronger in the end; to exist widely or commonly  
   **中文释义：** 获胜；占上风；普遍存在  
   **Example:** Calm prevailed after the team clarified the schedule.  
   **中文：** 团队澄清日程后，大家的情绪平静了下来。

21. **reassure** /ˌriːəˈʃʊr/  
   **English definition:** to say or do something that reduces someone’s worry or doubt  
   **中文释义：** 使安心；打消疑虑；安慰  
   **Example:** The technician reassured residents that the water had been tested.  
   **中文：** 技术人员让居民放心，表示水已经检测过了。

22. **retention** /rɪˈtenʃən/  
   **English definition:** the ability to keep something, such as information, or the act of continuing to have it  
   **中文释义：** 保留；保持；记忆力；留存率  
   **Example:** Short, spaced reviews can improve vocabulary retention.  
   **中文：** 简短且间隔进行的复习可以提高词汇记忆效果。

23. **scheme** /skiːm/  
   **English definition:** an organized plan or system for achieving something  
   **中文释义：** 计划；方案；体系  
   **Example:** The city introduced a grant scheme for small businesses.  
   **中文：** 市里为小型企业推出了一项补助计划。

24. **sensible** /ˈsensəbəl/  
   **English definition:** showing good judgment and practical understanding of a situation  
   **中文释义：** 明智的；合理的；切合实际的  
   **Example:** It’s sensible to keep a copy of the receipt until the refund arrives.  
   **中文：** 退款到账前保留收据副本是明智的做法。

25. **virtue** /ˈvɜːrtʃuː/  
   **English definition:** a good quality or useful feature; also, morally good behavior  
   **中文释义：** 优点；美德；长处  
   **Example:** One virtue of the tool is that it works offline.  
   **中文：** 这个工具的一个优点是可以离线使用。
