# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-31

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **assertive** /əˈsɜːrtɪv/  
   **English definition:** confident in expressing your needs or opinions clearly, without being aggressive  
   **中文释义：** 坚定自信的；敢于表达的  
   **Example:** Being assertive means stating your needs clearly while respecting others.  
   **中文：** 坚定表达自己的需求，同时尊重他人，这就是自信而不咄咄逼人。

2. **corresponding** /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** matching or connected to something else  
   **中文释义：** 相应的；对应的  
   **Example:** Enter the code in the corresponding box on the form.  
   **中文：** 请在表格中相应的方框内填写代码。

3. **distinctive** /dɪˈstɪŋktɪv/  
   **English definition:** having a quality that makes someone or something easy to recognize  
   **中文释义：** 独特的；有辨识度的  
   **Example:** The building's curved roof gives it a distinctive silhouette.  
   **中文：** 这栋建筑的弧形屋顶赋予它鲜明的轮廓。

4. **dominant** /ˈdɑːmənənt/  
   **English definition:** more powerful, common, or influential than others  
   **中文释义：** 占主导地位的；强势的  
   **Example:** In this region, online sales have become the dominant channel.  
   **中文：** 在这个地区，线上销售已成为主要渠道。

5. **durable** /ˈdʊrəbəl/  
   **English definition:** able to last a long time without breaking or wearing out  
   **中文释义：** 耐用的；持久的  
   **Example:** They chose a durable fabric for the outdoor seats.  
   **中文：** 他们为户外座椅选用了耐用的布料。

6. **fierce** /fɪrs/  
   **English definition:** very strong, intense, or determined; sometimes aggressive  
   **中文释义：** 激烈的；强烈的；凶猛的  
   **Example:** The proposal faced fierce opposition from local residents.  
   **中文：** 这项提案遭到了当地居民的强烈反对。

7. **flourish** /ˈflɜːrɪʃ/  
   **English definition:** to grow or develop successfully  
   **中文释义：** 茁壮成长；兴旺；繁荣  
   **Example:** Independent shops can flourish when the neighborhood attracts steady foot traffic.  
   **中文：** 社区人流稳定时，独立商店也能蓬勃发展。

8. **hesitant** /ˈhezɪtənt/  
   **English definition:** slow to act or speak because you are uncertain or nervous  
   **中文释义：** 犹豫的；迟疑的  
   **Example:** She was hesitant to share the draft before checking the figures.  
   **中文：** 核对数据之前，她不太愿意分享草稿。

9. **intensive** /ɪnˈtensɪv/  
   **English definition:** involving a lot of activity, effort, or attention in a short time  
   **中文释义：** 集中的；强化的；密集的  
   **Example:** The program includes an intensive two-week language course.  
   **中文：** 该项目包含为期两周的强化语言课程。

10. **intermediate** /ˌɪntərˈmiːdiət/  
    **English definition:** between beginner and advanced in skill or difficulty  
    **中文释义：** 中级的；中间阶段的  
    **Example:** This tutorial is designed for intermediate users who know the basics.  
    **中文：** 这份教程面向已掌握基础知识的中级用户。

11. **isolate** /ˈaɪsəleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to separate someone or something from others; to identify one factor on its own  
    **中文释义：** 隔离；分离；单独找出  
    **Example:** The test helps isolate the cause of the noise.  
    **中文：** 这项测试有助于单独找出噪声的来源。

12. **likewise** /ˈlaɪkwaɪz/  
    **English definition:** in the same way; also  
    **中文释义：** 同样地；也  
    **Example:** The first team was praised for its careful work, and the second was likewise recognized.  
    **中文：** 第一组因工作细致受到表扬，第二组也同样得到了认可。

13. **mere** /mɪr/  
    **English definition:** used to emphasize that something is small, simple, or not important by itself  
    **中文释义：** 仅仅的；只不过  
    **Example:** A mere apology won't repair the damage, but it is a start.  
    **中文：** 仅仅道歉无法弥补损失，不过这至少是个开始。

14. **mutual** /ˈmjuːtʃuəl/  
    **English definition:** shared by two or more people or groups  
    **中文释义：** 相互的；共同的  
    **Example:** The two firms have a mutual interest in keeping the supplier reliable.  
    **中文：** 这两家公司有共同利益，都希望供应商保持可靠。

15. **neglect** /nɪˈɡlekt/  
    **English definition:** to fail to give proper care or attention to someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 忽视；疏于照料  
    **Example:** If you neglect regular backups, a single device failure can erase your work.  
    **中文：** 如果忽视定期备份，一次设备故障就可能让你的工作成果全部丢失。

16. **nevertheless** /ˌnevərðəˈles/  
    **English definition:** despite what has just been said or done  
    **中文释义：** 尽管如此；然而  
    **Example:** The deadline was tight; nevertheless, the team completed the review on time.  
    **中文：** 截止时间很紧；尽管如此，团队还是按时完成了审查。

17. **notable** /ˈnoʊtəbəl/  
    **English definition:** important or interesting enough to deserve attention  
    **中文释义：** 值得注意的；显著的  
    **Example:** One notable change is that applications can now be submitted online.  
    **中文：** 一个显著变化是，现在可以在线提交申请。

18. **optimistic** /ˌɑːptɪˈmɪstɪk/  
    **English definition:** expecting good things to happen or believing a situation will improve  
    **中文释义：** 乐观的；抱乐观看法的  
    **Example:** The early results are promising, so the researchers remain optimistic.  
    **中文：** 初步结果令人鼓舞，因此研究人员仍然保持乐观。

19. **overlook** /ˌoʊvərˈlʊk/  
    **English definition:** to fail to notice something; to have a view from above  
    **中文释义：** 忽略；未注意到；俯瞰  
    **Example:** We overlooked a small formatting error in the first version.  
    **中文：** 我们忽略了第一版中的一个小格式错误。

20. **preceding** /prɪˈsiːdɪŋ/  
    **English definition:** coming or happening before something else  
    **中文释义：** 在前的；前面的  
    **Example:** The information was missing from the preceding section.  
    **中文：** 前一节中缺少这项信息。

21. **prospective** /prəˈspektɪv/  
    **English definition:** expected or likely to become something in the future  
    **中文释义：** 预期的；未来的；潜在的  
    **Example:** Prospective tenants can book a viewing through the website.  
    **中文：** 有意租房的人可以通过网站预约看房。

22. **restrain** /rɪˈstreɪn/  
    **English definition:** to control or limit someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 抑制；限制；约束  
    **Example:** The new policy is intended to restrain unnecessary spending.  
    **中文：** 这项新政策旨在限制不必要的开支。

23. **scarce** /skers/  
    **English definition:** available only in small amounts; not easy to find  
    **中文释义：** 稀缺的；不足的  
    **Example:** Clean water became scarce during the prolonged drought.  
    **中文：** 长期干旱期间，清洁水变得十分稀缺。

24. **skeptical** /ˈskeptɪkəl/  
    **English definition:** having doubts about whether something is true, useful, or reliable  
    **中文释义：** 怀疑的；持怀疑态度的  
    **Example:** Investors remained skeptical until the company released audited figures.  
    **中文：** 在公司公布经审计的数据前，投资者仍持怀疑态度。

25. **vulnerable** /ˈvʌlnərəbəl/  
    **English definition:** easily harmed, attacked, or affected  
    **中文释义：** 脆弱的；易受伤害的；易受影响的  
    **Example:** Without two-factor authentication, the account is more vulnerable to theft.  
    **中文：** 如果没有双重验证，这个账户更容易被盗。
