# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-21

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **brusque** /brʊsk/  
   **English definition:** using very few words and sounding unfriendly or impatient  
   **中文释义：** 唐突的；冷淡简短的  
   **Example:** His brusque reply made it clear that he did not want to continue the conversation.  
   **中文：** 他冷淡而简短的回答表明，他不想继续谈话。

2. **debacle** /deɪˈbɑːkəl/  
   **English definition:** a sudden and complete failure, usually causing embarrassment  
   **中文释义：** 惨败；彻底失败  
   **Example:** The product launch became a public debacle after the website crashed.  
   **中文：** 网站崩溃后，这次产品发布变成了一场公开的惨败。

3. **demure** /dɪˈmjʊr/  
   **English definition:** quiet, modest, and behaving in a reserved way  
   **中文释义：** 娴静的；端庄含蓄的  
   **Example:** She gave a demure smile when the audience applauded her performance.  
   **中文：** 观众为她的表演鼓掌时，她露出了端庄含蓄的微笑。

4. **disconcerting** /ˌdɪskənˈsɜːrtɪŋ/  
   **English definition:** causing someone to feel confused, worried, or unsettled  
   **中文释义：** 令人不安的；使人困惑的  
   **Example:** It was disconcerting to find that several important records had disappeared.  
   **中文：** 发现几份重要记录不见了，令人感到不安。

5. **embolden** /ɪmˈboʊldən/  
   **English definition:** to make someone more confident or willing to take a risk  
   **中文释义：** 鼓舞；使更有胆量  
   **Example:** The early success emboldened the team to attempt a more ambitious project.  
   **中文：** 初步的成功鼓舞了团队，使他们敢于尝试更具雄心的项目。

6. **entreat** /ɪnˈtriːt/  
   **English definition:** to ask someone earnestly and repeatedly to do something  
   **中文释义：** 恳求；哀求  
   **Example:** The residents entreated the council to preserve the historic building.  
   **中文：** 居民们恳求委员会保留这座历史建筑。

7. **fractious** /ˈfrækʃəs/  
   **English definition:** easily irritated and often involved in arguments  
   **中文释义：** 易怒的；好争吵的  
   **Example:** The fractious committee struggled to agree on even minor details.  
   **中文：** 这个脾气急躁、争吵不断的委员会连小细节都难以达成一致。

8. **garner** /ˈɡɑːrnər/  
   **English definition:** to obtain or collect something, especially support or attention  
   **中文释义：** 获得；收集，尤指支持或关注  
   **Example:** The proposal garnered considerable support from local businesses.  
   **中文：** 这项提案获得了当地企业的大力支持。

9. **guile** /ɡaɪl/  
   **English definition:** clever but dishonest behavior used to deceive someone  
   **中文释义：** 欺诈；诡计  
   **Example:** The negotiator relied on patience rather than guile to reach an agreement.  
   **中文：** 这位谈判者依靠耐心而不是诡计来达成协议。

10. **heedless** /ˈhiːdləs/  
    **English definition:** not paying attention to advice, warnings, or possible consequences  
    **中文释义：** 不理会警告的；不顾后果的  
    **Example:** He was heedless of the warning signs and continued driving into the storm.  
    **中文：** 他无视警告标志，继续驾车驶入暴风雨中。

11. **inimical** /ɪˈnɪmɪkəl/  
    **English definition:** harmful or opposed to someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 有害的；敌对的  
    **Example:** Excessive bureaucracy is inimical to innovation.  
    **中文：** 过度的官僚主义不利于创新。

12. **itinerant** /aɪˈtɪnərənt/  
    **English definition:** traveling from place to place, especially for work  
    **中文释义：** 巡回的；流动的  
    **Example:** The itinerant musician performed in small towns along the coast.  
    **中文：** 这位巡回音乐家在沿海的小城镇演出。

13. **loquacious** /ləˈkweɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** talking a lot, especially about unimportant things  
    **中文释义：** 健谈的；话多的  
    **Example:** The normally quiet student became surprisingly loquacious during the interview.  
    **中文：** 那名平时沉默的学生在面试中意外地健谈。

14. **meritorious** /ˌmerɪˈtɔːriəs/  
    **English definition:** deserving praise, reward, or recognition  
    **中文释义：** 值得赞扬或奖励的；有功绩的  
    **Example:** The foundation gives grants to meritorious projects that benefit the community.  
    **中文：** 该基金会向有益于社区的优秀项目提供资助。

15. **overt** /oʊˈvɜːrt/  
    **English definition:** open, clear, and not secret or hidden  
    **中文释义：** 公开的；明显的  
    **Example:** The report found no overt attempt to influence the investigation.  
    **中文：** 报告没有发现任何公开影响调查的企图。

16. **pensive** /ˈpensɪv/  
    **English definition:** quietly thoughtful, often with a slightly sad expression  
    **中文释义：** 沉思的；忧郁的  
    **Example:** She grew pensive while looking through the letters from her childhood.  
    **中文：** 翻看童年时期的信件时，她陷入了沉思。

17. **poignant** /ˈpɔɪnjənt/  
    **English definition:** causing strong feelings of sadness, sympathy, or emotion  
    **中文释义：** 辛酸动人的；令人深受触动的  
    **Example:** The documentary offered a poignant account of families separated by war.  
    **中文：** 这部纪录片感人地讲述了被战争分离的家庭。

18. **pretentious** /prɪˈtenʃəs/  
    **English definition:** trying to appear more important, intelligent, or cultured than one really is  
    **中文释义：** 自命不凡的；矫揉造作的  
    **Example:** The review criticized the film for its pretentious dialogue and weak story.  
    **中文：** 评论批评这部电影的对白矫揉造作、故事薄弱。

19. **rebuke** /rɪˈbjuːk/  
    **English definition:** to criticize someone sharply for doing something wrong  
    **中文释义：** 严厉斥责；谴责  
    **Example:** The director rebuked the staff for ignoring the safety procedure.  
    **中文：** 主管严厉斥责员工忽视安全流程。

20. **reverent** /ˈrevərənt/  
    **English definition:** showing deep respect for someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 表示崇敬的；恭敬的  
    **Example:** Visitors spoke in reverent tones inside the memorial hall.  
    **中文：** 参观者在纪念馆内用恭敬的语气交谈。

21. **temperate** /ˈtempərət/  
    **English definition:** moderate and self-controlled, especially in behavior or opinions  
    **中文释义：** 温和克制的；适度的  
    **Example:** Even during the debate, she maintained a temperate tone.  
    **中文：** 即使在辩论中，她也保持了温和克制的语气。

22. **trenchant** /ˈtrentʃənt/  
    **English definition:** very strong, clear, and effective in criticizing something  
    **中文释义：** 尖锐有力的；犀利的  
    **Example:** The columnist offered a trenchant critique of the proposed reform.  
    **中文：** 这位专栏作家对拟议的改革提出了尖锐有力的批评。

23. **tumultuous** /tuːˈmʌltʃuəs/  
    **English definition:** full of noise, confusion, or sudden changes  
    **中文释义：** 动荡的；喧闹混乱的  
    **Example:** The country experienced a tumultuous decade of political change.  
    **中文：** 这个国家经历了政治变革动荡的十年。

24. **probity** /ˈproʊbəti/  
    **English definition:** complete honesty and strong moral principles  
    **中文释义：** 正直；廉洁  
    **Example:** Her probity made her a trusted advisor during the investigation.  
    **中文：** 她的正直使她成为调查期间值得信赖的顾问。

25. **recondite** /ˈrekəndaɪt/  
    **English definition:** difficult to understand because it concerns specialized or obscure knowledge  
    **中文释义：** 深奥的；鲜为人知的  
    **Example:** The lecture explored a recondite question in medieval philosophy.  
    **中文：** 这场讲座探讨了中世纪哲学中的一个深奥问题。
