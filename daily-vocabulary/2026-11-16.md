# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-11-16

25 个偏实用、兼具拓展性的英语词汇。每个词附音标、英文释义、中文释义及双语例句。

1. **aftermath** /ˈæftərmæθ/  
   **English definition:** the effects and events that follow an unpleasant or important event  
   **中文释义：** 后果；余波；事后局面  
   **Example:** The town is still repairing roads in the aftermath of the storm.  
   **中文：** 风暴过后，这座小镇仍在修复道路。

2. **amenity** /əˈmenəti/  
   **English definition:** a feature or service that makes a place more comfortable or convenient  
   **中文释义：** 便利设施；生活配套  
   **Example:** The apartment complex offers amenities such as a gym and a shared workspace.  
   **中文：** 这片公寓区提供健身房和共享工作空间等配套设施。

3. **antidote** /ˈæntɪdoʊt/  
   **English definition:** a substance that counteracts a poison; something that helps prevent or correct a harmful effect  
   **中文释义：** 解毒剂；对抗不良影响的方法  
   **Example:** Careful fact-checking is an effective antidote to online misinformation.  
   **中文：** 认真核实事实是应对网络错误信息的有效方法。

4. **arbitration** /ˌɑːrbɪˈtreɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a process in which an independent person makes a decision to settle a disagreement  
   **中文释义：** 仲裁  
   **Example:** The contract requires arbitration if the two companies cannot settle the dispute.  
   **中文：** 合同规定，如果两家公司无法解决争议，就必须进行仲裁。

5. **aspiration** /ˌæspəˈreɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a strong hope or ambition to achieve something  
   **中文释义：** 志向；抱负；愿望  
   **Example:** Her aspiration is to lead a research team working on clean energy.  
   **中文：** 她的志向是领导一支从事清洁能源研究的团队。

6. **bottleneck** /ˈbɑːtəlnek/  
   **English definition:** a point in a process where progress is slowed because capacity is limited  
   **中文释义：** 瓶颈；阻碍流程的环节  
   **Example:** Limited testing capacity became the bottleneck in the product launch.  
   **中文：** 测试能力有限成了产品上市流程中的瓶颈。

7. **brisk** /brɪsk/  
   **English definition:** quick and energetic; pleasantly cool when describing weather  
   **中文释义：** 轻快的；有活力的；（天气）凉爽的  
   **Example:** We took a brisk walk before the morning meeting.  
   **中文：** 早会前我们快步散了会儿步。

8. **collateral** /kəˈlætərəl/  
   **English definition:** property promised to a lender as security for a loan  
   **中文释义：** 抵押品；担保物  
   **Example:** The bank accepted the equipment as collateral for the business loan.  
   **中文：** 银行接受这批设备作为商业贷款的抵押品。

9. **compulsory** /kəmˈpʌlsəri/  
   **English definition:** required by a rule, law, or authority; not optional  
   **中文释义：** 强制的；必修的；必须参加的  
   **Example:** Safety training is compulsory for everyone working on the site.  
   **中文：** 所有在该工地工作的人都必须参加安全培训。

10. **confidential** /ˌkɑːnfɪˈdenʃəl/  
    **English definition:** intended to be kept private and shared only with authorized people  
    **中文释义：** 机密的；保密的  
    **Example:** Please send the confidential files through the secure portal.  
    **中文：** 请通过安全门户发送这些机密文件。

11. **confrontation** /ˌkɑːnfrənˈteɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a direct meeting or argument between people with opposing views  
   **中文释义：** 对峙；冲突  
    **Example:** The manager arranged a private meeting to prevent a public confrontation.  
    **中文：** 经理安排了一次私下会面，以避免公开冲突。

12. **consistency** /kənˈsɪstənsi/  
    **English definition:** the quality of staying the same in standard, behavior, or result  
    **中文释义：** 一致性；稳定性；连贯性  
    **Example:** Consistency in customer service helped the small firm earn repeat business.  
    **中文：** 稳定一致的客户服务帮助这家小公司赢得了回头客。

13. **constituency** /kənˈstɪtʃuənsi/  
    **English definition:** the group of voters represented by an elected official  
    **中文释义：** 选区；选民群体  
    **Example:** The representative held a meeting to hear concerns from her constituency.  
    **中文：** 这名代表举行会议，听取其选民群体的意见。

14. **consultation** /ˌkɑːnsəlˈteɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a meeting with an expert to receive advice, or a discussion to gather views  
    **中文释义：** 咨询；会诊；征求意见  
    **Example:** Patients can book a short consultation with a specialist online.  
    **中文：** 患者可以在线预约与专科医生进行简短咨询。

15. **conviction** /kənˈvɪkʃən/  
    **English definition:** a firmly held belief or opinion  
    **中文释义：** 坚定的信念；确信  
    **Example:** He explained his position with conviction but remained open to questions.  
    **中文：** 他坚定地阐述了自己的立场，同时也愿意接受提问。

16. **correspondence** /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndəns/  
    **English definition:** letters, emails, or other messages exchanged between people or organizations  
    **中文释义：** 通信；往来信件  
    **Example:** Keep all correspondence with the supplier in the project folder.  
    **中文：** 请把与供应商的所有往来邮件都保存在项目文件夹中。

17. **deductible** /dɪˈdʌktəbəl/  
    **English definition:** the amount an insured person must pay before insurance covers the remaining costs  
    **中文释义：** 免赔额；自付额  
    **Example:** This insurance plan has a lower monthly payment but a higher deductible.  
    **中文：** 这份保险计划的月缴费较低，但免赔额较高。

18. **demographic** /ˌdeməˈɡræfɪk/  
    **English definition:** relating to a group of people defined by characteristics such as age or location  
    **中文释义：** 人口统计的；特定人群的  
    **Example:** The survey included participants from several age demographics.  
    **中文：** 这项调查纳入了多个年龄群体的参与者。

19. **deterioration** /dɪˌtɪriəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the process of becoming worse in condition, quality, or value  
    **中文释义：** 恶化；退化  
    **Example:** Regular inspections can reveal early deterioration in the roof.  
    **中文：** 定期检查可以及早发现屋顶的老化情况。

20. **diagnosis** /ˌdaɪəɡˈnoʊsɪs/  
    **English definition:** the identification of an illness or problem by examining its signs  
    **中文释义：** 诊断；问题判断  
    **Example:** The technician's diagnosis revealed a fault in the cooling system.  
    **中文：** 技术人员的诊断发现冷却系统存在故障。

21. **discourse** /ˈdɪskɔːrs/  
    **English definition:** formal discussion or writing about a particular subject  
    **中文释义：** 论述；正式讨论；话语  
    **Example:** The book examines how public discourse about climate has changed.  
    **中文：** 这本书探讨了公众关于气候问题的讨论方式如何发生变化。

22. **earnings** /ˈɜːrnɪŋz/  
    **English definition:** money received from work, business, or investment  
    **中文释义：** 收入；所得  
    **Example:** Her monthly earnings vary because she works on short-term contracts.  
    **中文：** 由于从事短期合同工作，她每月的收入有所波动。

23. **ecosystem** /ˈiːkoʊsɪstəm/  
    **English definition:** a community of living things and the environment they interact with  
    **中文释义：** 生态系统；相互关联的体系  
    **Example:** Wetlands support a diverse ecosystem and help absorb floodwater.  
    **中文：** 湿地支撑着多样的生态系统，也有助于吸收洪水。

24. **emission** /ɪˈmɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the release of gas, heat, light, or other substances into the environment  
    **中文释义：** 排放；散发物  
    **Example:** The factory installed new filters to reduce harmful emissions.  
    **中文：** 工厂安装了新过滤器，以减少有害排放。

25. **enrollment** /ɪnˈroʊlmənt/  
    **English definition:** the process of registering for a course or service, or the number of people registered  
    **中文释义：** 注册；入学；登记人数  
    **Example:** Online enrollment for the evening course closes on Friday.  
    **中文：** 晚间课程的在线报名将于周五截止。
