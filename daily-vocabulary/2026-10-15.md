# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-15

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **acknowledge** /əkˈnɑːlɪdʒ/  
   **English definition:** to accept that something is true or exists, or to show that you have received or noticed something  
   **中文释义：** 承认；确认收到；注意到  
   **Example:** The firm acknowledged the delay and explained how it would prevent a repeat.  
   **中文：** 公司承认了延误，并说明将如何避免再次发生。

2. **acquire** /əˈkwaɪər/  
   **English definition:** to get or obtain something, often through effort, learning, or experience  
   **中文释义：** 获得；习得；取得  
   **Example:** She acquired practical skills through the apprenticeship.  
   **中文：** 她通过学徒培训掌握了实用技能。

3. **adapt** /əˈdæpt/  
   **English definition:** to change something or yourself to suit new conditions or needs  
   **中文释义：** 适应；调整；改编  
   **Example:** The team adapted the guide for readers with limited internet access.  
   **中文：** 团队调整了指南，以方便网络条件有限的读者使用。

4. **advocate** /ˈædvəkeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to publicly support or recommend an idea, action, or policy  
   **中文释义：** 提倡；拥护；主张  
   **Example:** Several researchers advocate a simpler method for collecting the data.  
   **中文：** 几位研究人员主张采用更简单的数据收集方法。

5. **approximate** /əˈprɑːksəmət/  
   **English definition:** close to the actual amount or value, but not completely exact  
   **中文释义：** 大约的；近似的  
   **Example:** The approximate cost includes delivery but not installation.  
   **中文：** 估算费用包含配送，但不包含安装。

6. **arise** /əˈraɪz/  
   **English definition:** to happen or become noticeable, especially when a situation or problem develops  
   **中文释义：** 出现；发生；产生  
   **Example:** If any technical issues arise, contact the support team.  
   **中文：** 如果出现任何技术问题，请联系支持团队。

7. **aspect** /ˈæspekt/  
   **English definition:** one particular part or feature of a situation, idea, or problem  
   **中文释义：** 方面；层面；特征  
   **Example:** Cost is only one aspect of the decision.  
   **中文：** 成本只是这项决定需要考虑的一个方面。

8. **assign** /əˈsaɪn/  
   **English definition:** to give someone a task or responsibility, or allocate something for a purpose  
   **中文释义：** 分配；指派；指定  
   **Example:** The supervisor assigned each group a different case study.  
   **中文：** 主管给每个小组分配了不同的案例研究。

9. **assure** /əˈʃʊr/  
   **English definition:** to tell someone confidently that something is true, often to remove doubt or worry  
   **中文释义：** 向……保证；使确信；确保  
   **Example:** I can assure you that the files are backed up every night.  
   **中文：** 我可以向你保证，这些文件每晚都会备份。

10. **boundary** /ˈbaʊndəri/  
   **English definition:** a real or imagined line that marks a limit or separates things  
   **中文释义：** 边界；界限；范围  
   **Example:** Setting clear boundaries helps teams manage after-hours messages.  
   **中文：** 设定明确的界限有助于团队管理下班后的消息沟通。

11. **characterize** /ˈkærəktəraɪz/  
   **English definition:** to describe the distinctive qualities or typical features of someone or something  
   **中文释义：** 描述……的特征；以……为特点  
   **Example:** Researchers characterize the region as highly vulnerable to drought.  
   **中文：** 研究人员将该地区描述为极易受干旱影响的区域。

12. **classify** /ˈklæsəfaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to arrange people or things into groups according to shared features  
   **中文释义：** 分类；归类  
   **Example:** The system classifies requests by urgency.  
   **中文：** 该系统根据紧急程度对请求进行分类。

13. **collapse** /kəˈlæps/  
   **English definition:** to fall down or fail suddenly; to cause something to fall or fail  
   **中文释义：** 倒塌；崩溃；突然失败  
   **Example:** The negotiations collapsed after both sides rejected the revised terms.  
   **中文：** 双方都拒绝了修订后的条款，谈判因此破裂。

14. **compromise** /ˈkɑːmprəmaɪz/  
   **English definition:** an agreement in which each side accepts less than it wanted; to reach such an agreement  
   **中文释义：** 妥协；折中方案；达成折中  
   **Example:** They reached a compromise that gave both teams more time.  
   **中文：** 双方达成了折中方案，让两个团队都获得了更多时间。

15. **consent** /kənˈsent/  
   **English definition:** permission or agreement given freely and with an understanding of what is involved  
   **中文释义：** 同意；许可；知情同意  
   **Example:** Participants gave written consent before the interview.  
   **中文：** 参与者在访谈前提供了书面同意。

16. **consult** /kənˈsʌlt/  
   **English definition:** to ask someone for information or advice, or discuss something with them  
   **中文释义：** 咨询；请教；商议  
   **Example:** Before changing the schedule, consult the site coordinator.  
   **中文：** 更改日程前，请先咨询现场协调员。

17. **cooperate** /koʊˈɑːpəreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to work or act together willingly toward a shared goal  
   **中文释义：** 合作；协作；配合  
   **Example:** The departments cooperated to resolve the billing issue.  
   **中文：** 各部门协作解决了账单问题。

18. **cope** /koʊp/  
   **English definition:** to manage a difficult situation or deal successfully with a problem  
   **中文释义：** 应付；处理；克服困难  
   **Example:** The backup process helps small teams cope with sudden spikes in demand.  
   **中文：** 备份流程帮助小团队应对需求突然激增的情况。

19. **distinct** /dɪˈstɪŋkt/  
   **English definition:** clearly different or easy to recognize as separate  
   **中文释义：** 明显不同的；清晰可辨的  
   **Example:** The latest version has a distinct icon to prevent confusion.  
   **中文：** 最新版本有一个明显不同的图标，以避免混淆。

20. **duration** /duˈreɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** the length of time that something continues or lasts  
   **中文释义：** 持续时间；期间  
   **Example:** The permit is valid for the duration of the project.  
   **中文：** 许可证在整个项目期间有效。

21. **efficient** /ɪˈfɪʃənt/  
   **English definition:** working well without wasting time, energy, or other resources  
   **中文释义：** 高效的；效率高的；节约资源的  
   **Example:** A more efficient route cut delivery times by 20 percent.  
   **中文：** 更高效的路线将配送时间缩短了百分之二十。

22. **engage** /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒ/  
   **English definition:** to involve someone in an activity or attract and hold their attention  
   **中文释义：** 使参与；吸引；使投入  
   **Example:** The workshop uses short exercises to engage new employees.  
   **中文：** 这场培训通过简短练习让新员工参与其中。

23. **insist** /ɪnˈsɪst/  
   **English definition:** to demand firmly that something happen or state firmly that something is true  
   **中文释义：** 坚持要求；坚称  
   **Example:** The auditor insisted on seeing the original receipts.  
   **中文：** 审计员坚持要查看原始收据。

24. **interact** /ˌɪntərˈækt/  
   **English definition:** to communicate or work with someone, or affect one another  
   **中文释义：** 互动；交流；相互作用  
   **Example:** The two systems interact through a secure interface.  
   **中文：** 这两个系统通过安全接口进行交互。

25. **rational** /ˈræʃənəl/  
   **English definition:** based on reason and clear thinking rather than emotion or guesswork  
   **中文释义：** 理性的；合理的；基于逻辑的  
   **Example:** We need a rational basis for comparing these proposals.  
   **中文：** 我们需要一个理性的依据来比较这些提案。
