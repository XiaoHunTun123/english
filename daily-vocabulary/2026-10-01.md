# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-01

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abecedarian** /ˌeɪbɪsiˈderiən/  
   **English definition:** relating to the alphabet or to the early stages of learning something  
   **中文释义：** 字母表的；初学的；基础入门的  
   **Example:** The course offers an abecedarian introduction to data analysis.  
   **中文：** 这门课程提供数据分析的基础入门介绍。

2. **accoutrement** /əˈkuːtərmənt/  
   **English definition:** an item of clothing or equipment needed for a particular activity  
   **中文释义：** 装备；服饰；配件  
   **Example:** The hikers checked every accoutrement before setting out.  
   **中文：** 徒步者出发前检查了每一件装备。

3. **acerbity** /əˈsɜːrbəti/  
   **English definition:** sharpness or bitterness in speech, manner, or taste  
   **中文释义：** 尖刻；辛辣；苦涩  
   **Example:** The reviewer’s acerbity made the otherwise useful criticism difficult to accept.  
   **中文：** 评论者尖刻的语气让原本有用的批评变得难以接受。

4. **agglomerate** /əˈɡlɑːməreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to gather or form into a closely packed mass or group  
   **中文释义：** 聚集；凝聚成团  
   **Example:** Small technology firms began to agglomerate around the new research center.  
   **中文：** 小型科技公司开始聚集在新的研究中心周围。

5. **alchemy** /ˈælkəmi/  
   **English definition:** a process that seems to transform ordinary things into something valuable or special  
   **中文释义：** 炼金术；神奇的转化过程  
   **Example:** The chef’s alchemy turned inexpensive ingredients into an impressive meal.  
   **中文：** 厨师的神奇技艺把普通食材变成了一顿令人印象深刻的饭菜。

6. **argot** /ˈɑːrɡoʊ/  
   **English definition:** specialized language used by a particular group or profession  
   **中文释义：** 行话；黑话；专门用语  
   **Example:** The article explains the argot used by professional game designers.  
   **中文：** 这篇文章解释了专业游戏设计师使用的行话。

7. **beleaguer** /bɪˈliːɡər/  
   **English definition:** to cause someone or something persistent difficulty or trouble  
   **中文释义：** 困扰；使陷入困境  
   **Example:** The beleaguered department faced another round of budget cuts.  
   **中文：** 饱受困扰的部门又面临新一轮预算削减。

8. **bloviate** /ˈbloʊvieɪt/  
   **English definition:** to speak at length in a pompous or empty way  
   **中文释义：** 夸夸其谈；空洞地长篇大论  
   **Example:** The speaker continued to bloviate instead of answering the audience’s question.  
   **中文：** 演讲者没有回答观众的问题，而是继续夸夸其谈。

9. **canard** /kəˈnɑːrd/  
   **English definition:** a false story or idea that is repeated as if it were true  
   **中文释义：** 谣言；谬传  
   **Example:** The claim that the treatment is risk-free is an old canard.  
   **中文：** “这种疗法毫无风险”的说法是一个老掉牙的谬传。

10. **circumspection** /ˌsɜːrkəmsˈpekʃən/  
    **English definition:** careful thought and caution before taking action  
    **中文释义：** 谨慎；慎重  
    **Example:** The ambassador handled the sensitive question with considerable circumspection.  
    **中文：** 大使十分谨慎地处理了这个敏感问题。

11. **connive** /kəˈnaɪv/  
    **English definition:** to secretly allow or cooperate in something wrong  
    **中文释义：** 共谋；默许不当行为  
    **Example:** Several employees were accused of conniving with suppliers to inflate invoices.  
    **中文：** 几名员工被指控与供应商勾结，抬高发票金额。

12. **contumely** /ˈkɑːntjuːməli/  
    **English definition:** insulting or humiliating language or treatment  
    **中文释义：** 侮辱；傲慢无礼的言辞  
    **Example:** The witness endured public contumely after questioning the official account.  
    **中文：** 这名证人质疑官方说法后，遭受了公开侮辱。

13. **disabuse** /ˌdɪsəˈbjuːz/  
    **English definition:** to persuade someone to stop believing something that is untrue  
    **中文释义：** 纠正错误观念；使醒悟  
    **Example:** The guide tried to disabuse visitors of the idea that the ruins were recently built.  
    **中文：** 导游试图纠正游客认为这些遗址是最近建成的错误观念。

14. **enjoin** /ɪnˈdʒɔɪn/  
    **English definition:** to order or strongly encourage someone to do something  
    **中文释义：** 命令；强烈要求；劝诫  
    **Example:** The court enjoined the company to stop using the disputed trademark.  
    **中文：** 法院命令该公司停止使用有争议的商标。

15. **enmity** /ˈenməti/  
    **English definition:** a strong feeling of hostility or hatred  
    **中文释义：** 敌意；仇恨  
    **Example:** Years of political enmity made cooperation between the parties difficult.  
    **中文：** 多年的政治敌意使双方合作十分困难。

16. **epistolary** /ɪˈpɪstələri/  
    **English definition:** relating to letters or written correspondence  
    **中文释义：** 书信的；书信体的  
    **Example:** The novel takes an epistolary form, unfolding through emails and handwritten notes.  
    **中文：** 这部小说采用书信体，通过电子邮件和手写便笺展开故事。

17. **feckless** /ˈfekləs/  
    **English definition:** lacking energy, effectiveness, or a sense of responsibility  
    **中文释义：** 无能的；软弱无力的；不负责任的  
    **Example:** The feckless response from management left employees unsure what to do.  
    **中文：** 管理层无力的回应让员工不知所措。

18. **gambit** /ˈɡæmbɪt/  
    **English definition:** an action or remark used strategically to gain an advantage  
    **中文释义：** 策略；策略性举动  
    **Example:** Offering a small concession was a calculated gambit to restart negotiations.  
    **中文：** 提出小幅让步是重新启动谈判的一步策略。

19. **gravitas** /ˈɡrævɪtɑːs/  
    **English definition:** seriousness, dignity, and importance in a person or situation  
    **中文释义：** 严肃；庄重；分量  
    **Example:** Her calm delivery gave the announcement the gravitas it required.  
    **中文：** 她沉着的表达赋予了这则公告应有的庄重分量。

20. **interstice** /ɪnˈtɜːrstɪs/  
    **English definition:** a small space or gap between things  
    **中文释义：** 缝隙；间隙  
    **Example:** Dust had gathered in the narrow interstices between the floorboards.  
    **中文：** 灰尘聚集在地板木板之间狭窄的缝隙里。

21. **languor** /ˈlæŋɡər/  
    **English definition:** a pleasant or unpleasant feeling of tiredness and lack of energy  
    **中文释义：** 慵懒；倦怠；无精打采  
    **Example:** The intense heat brought a feeling of languor to the entire afternoon.  
    **中文：** 酷热让整个下午都弥漫着慵懒倦怠的感觉。

22. **panegyric** /ˌpænɪˈdʒɪrɪk/  
    **English definition:** a public speech or piece of writing that praises someone highly  
    **中文释义：** 颂词；赞文  
    **Example:** The review was less an analysis than a panegyric to the director’s talent.  
    **中文：** 这篇评论与其说是分析，不如说是对导演才华的颂扬。

23. **perspicuity** /ˌpɜːrspɪˈkjuːəti/  
    **English definition:** clarity of expression or ease of understanding  
    **中文释义：** 清晰；明晰易懂  
    **Example:** The manual is valued for its perspicuity and practical examples.  
    **中文：** 这本手册因表达清晰且实例实用而受到重视。

24. **prevaricate** /prɪˈværɪkeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to avoid giving a direct answer by speaking vaguely or misleadingly  
    **中文释义：** 含糊其辞；支吾其词  
    **Example:** The official continued to prevaricate when asked who had approved the contract.  
    **中文：** 当被问及谁批准了合同时，这名官员仍继续含糊其辞。

25. **vituperate** /vɪˈtuːpəreɪt/  
    **English definition:** to criticize or insult someone angrily and abusively  
    **中文释义：** 痛骂；严厉斥责  
    **Example:** The columnist chose to analyze the decision rather than vituperate against its supporters.  
    **中文：** 这位专栏作家选择分析这项决定，而不是痛骂其支持者。
