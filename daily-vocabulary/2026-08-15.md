# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-08-15

本日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。

1. **affable** /ˈæfəbəl/ — friendly and easy to talk to；和蔼可亲的  
   *The manager is affable and approachable.*  
   这位经理和蔼可亲，很容易接近。

2. **anomalous** /əˈnɑːmələs/ — unusual or inconsistent with what is expected；异常的  
   *The researchers noticed an anomalous result.*  
   研究人员注意到一个异常结果。

3. **appraise** /əˈpreɪz/ — to assess the value or quality of something；评估；鉴定  
   *An expert appraised the antique painting.*  
   专家鉴定了这幅古画。

4. **austere** /ɔːˈstɪr/ — simple, strict, and without luxury；朴素的；严苛的  
   *The room had an austere design.*  
   这个房间的设计十分简朴。

5. **candid** /ˈkændɪd/ — honest and direct；坦率的；直言不讳的  
   *She gave a candid account of the incident.*  
   她坦率地讲述了这起事件。

6. **complacent** /kəmˈpleɪsənt/ — too satisfied and not alert to danger；自满的；掉以轻心的  
   *Success made the team complacent.*  
   成功让团队变得自满。

7. **deference** /ˈdefərəns/ — respectful submission to another person；敬意；顺从  
   *He showed deference to his senior colleagues.*  
   他对资深同事表示了敬意。

8. **delineate** /dɪˈlɪnieɪt/ — to describe or mark something precisely；描述；划定  
   *The report delineates the responsibilities of each department.*  
   报告明确划分了各部门的职责。

9. **depreciate** /dɪˈpriːʃieɪt/ — to lose value over time；贬值  
   *Cars tend to depreciate quickly.*  
   汽车往往会快速贬值。

10. **disparate** /ˈdɪspərət/ — fundamentally different；迥异的；不同类的  
    *The study combines data from disparate sources.*  
    这项研究整合了来自不同来源的数据。

11. **eclectic** /ɪˈklektɪk/ — drawing ideas from many different sources；兼收并蓄的  
    *She has an eclectic taste in music.*  
    她的音乐品味广泛而多元。

12. **eloquent** /ˈeləkwənt/ — fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing；雄辩的；有说服力的  
    *He gave an eloquent speech about justice.*  
    他发表了一场关于正义的精彩演讲。

13. **enigmatic** /ˌenɪɡˈmætɪk/ — mysterious and difficult to understand；神秘莫测的  
    *The artist was known for his enigmatic smile.*  
    这位艺术家以神秘的微笑而闻名。

14. **equivocal** /ɪˈkwɪvəkəl/ — deliberately unclear or open to several meanings；模棱两可的  
    *His equivocal answer avoided the real issue.*  
    他模棱两可的回答回避了真正的问题。

15. **fervent** /ˈfɜːrvənt/ — showing intense passion or enthusiasm；热烈的；充满热情的  
    *She is a fervent supporter of animal welfare.*  
    她热烈支持动物福利事业。

16. **fray** /freɪ/ — to become worn or damaged at the edges；磨损；磨破  
    *The fabric began to fray along the edge.*  
    这块布的边缘开始磨损。

17. **galvanize** /ˈɡælvənaɪz/ — to stimulate people into taking action；激励；促使行动  
    *The speech galvanized public support.*  
    这场演讲激发了公众的支持。

18. **heed** /hiːd/ — to pay careful attention to advice or warnings；注意；听从  
    *You should heed the doctor’s advice.*  
    你应该听从医生的建议。

19. **judicious** /dʒuˈdɪʃəs/ — showing good judgment；明智的；审慎的  
    *A judicious investment can reduce risk.*  
    明智的投资可以降低风险。

20. **lucid** /ˈluːsɪd/ — clear and easy to understand；清晰易懂的  
    *The professor gave a lucid explanation.*  
    教授给出了清晰易懂的解释。

21. **ostensible** /ɑːˈstensəbəl/ — appearing to be true, but possibly not；表面上的；貌似的  
    *The ostensible reason was lack of funding.*  
    表面上的理由是资金不足。

22. **salient** /ˈseɪliənt/ — most noticeable or important；显著的；突出的  
    *The report highlights the salient facts.*  
    报告突出了最重要的事实。

23. **tenacious** /təˈneɪʃəs/ — persistent and unwilling to give up；坚韧的；顽强的  
    *Her tenacious efforts eventually paid off.*  
    她坚持不懈的努力最终得到了回报。

24. **ubiquitous** /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/ — present or found everywhere；无处不在的  
    *Smartphones are ubiquitous in modern life.*  
    智能手机在现代生活中无处不在。

25. **vindicate** /ˈvɪndɪkeɪt/ — to prove that someone was right or not guilty；证明正确；洗清罪名  
    *The new evidence vindicated the accused.*  
    新证据证明被告是清白的。
