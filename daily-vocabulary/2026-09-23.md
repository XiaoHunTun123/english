# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-23

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abscond** /əbˈskɑːnd/  
   **English definition:** to leave secretly and quickly, especially to avoid arrest or responsibility  
   **中文释义：** 潜逃；偷偷离开  
   **Example:** The suspect absconded before investigators could question him.  
   **中文：** 调查人员还没来得及询问，嫌疑人就潜逃了。

2. **acrimony** /ˈækrɪmoʊni/  
   **English definition:** bitter anger or harshness in speech or behavior  
   **中文释义：** 刻薄；怨恨；尖刻的言辞  
   **Example:** The negotiations ended in acrimony after both sides rejected the compromise.  
   **中文：** 双方都拒绝妥协后，谈判在怨恨和争吵中结束。

3. **animus** /ˈænɪməs/  
   **English definition:** a strong feeling of hostility or dislike  
   **中文释义：** 敌意；仇恨  
   **Example:** The article criticized the proposal without any personal animus toward its author.  
   **中文：** 这篇文章批评了该提案，但并不针对作者个人怀有敌意。

4. **approbation** /ˌæprəˈbeɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** approval or praise, especially from an official or respected person  
   **中文释义：** 认可；赞许  
   **Example:** The scientist received international approbation for her research.  
   **中文：** 这位科学家的研究得到了国际认可。

5. **attrition** /əˈtrɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a gradual reduction in strength or numbers caused by continued pressure or loss  
   **中文释义：** 消耗；人员逐渐减少  
   **Example:** The company reduced its workforce through natural attrition rather than mass layoffs.  
   **中文：** 公司通过自然减员而非大规模裁员来减少员工数量。

6. **bereft** /bɪˈreft/  
   **English definition:** lacking something or someone important, especially because of loss  
   **中文释义：** 丧失重要事物的；失去亲人的  
   **Example:** After the closure of the factory, the town was bereft of its main source of employment.  
   **中文：** 工厂关闭后，这座城镇失去了主要就业来源。

7. **clemency** /ˈklemənsi/  
   **English definition:** mercy or leniency shown toward someone who has committed an offense  
   **中文释义：** 宽大；仁慈；宽恕  
   **Example:** The prisoner appealed to the governor for clemency.  
   **中文：** 这名囚犯请求州长予以宽大处理。

8. **condone** /kənˈdoʊn/  
   **English definition:** to accept or forgive behavior that is considered wrong  
   **中文释义：** 宽恕；纵容；默许  
   **Example:** The organization does not condone discrimination in any form.  
   **中文：** 该组织不容忍任何形式的歧视。

9. **deplore** /dɪˈplɔːr/  
   **English definition:** to strongly disapprove of something and feel that it is regrettable  
   **中文释义：** 强烈反对；痛惜；谴责  
   **Example:** Community leaders deplored the destruction of the historic site.  
   **中文：** 社区领导人强烈谴责对历史遗址的破坏。

10. **derelict** /ˈderəlɪkt/  
    **English definition:** abandoned and in very poor condition; failing to do a duty  
    **中文释义：** 荒废的；失职的  
    **Example:** The city plans to convert the derelict warehouse into affordable housing.  
    **中文：** 该市计划把这座荒废的仓库改造成保障性住房。

11. **dissident** /ˈdɪsɪdənt/  
    **English definition:** a person who publicly disagrees with an official policy or system  
    **中文释义：** 持不同政见者；异议人士  
    **Example:** The dissident journalist continued to publish articles despite official pressure.  
    **中文：** 尽管受到官方压力，这名异议记者仍继续发表文章。

12. **exigency** /ˈeksɪdʒənsi/  
    **English definition:** an urgent need or situation that requires immediate action  
    **中文释义：** 紧急情况；紧迫需要  
    **Example:** In response to the exigency, the hospital opened an additional treatment area.  
    **中文：** 为应对紧急情况，医院增设了一个治疗区域。

13. **flippant** /ˈflɪpənt/  
    **English definition:** not showing a serious or respectful attitude toward something important  
    **中文释义：** 轻率的；不严肃的  
    **Example:** His flippant answer was inappropriate given the seriousness of the issue.  
    **中文：** 考虑到问题的严重性，他轻率的回答并不合适。

14. **forestall** /fɔːrˈstɔːl/  
    **English definition:** to prevent something from happening by acting before it does  
    **中文释义：** 预先阻止；抢先采取行动  
    **Example:** The company issued a clarification to forestall further rumors.  
    **中文：** 公司发布澄清声明，以预先阻止谣言进一步传播。

15. **garish** /ˈɡerɪʃ/  
    **English definition:** excessively bright, colorful, or showy in a way that is unattractive  
    **中文释义：** 过分鲜艳的；俗艳的  
    **Example:** The shop’s garish signs were visible from several blocks away.  
    **中文：** 这家商店俗艳的招牌从几个街区外都能看见。

16. **inculcate** /ˈɪŋkʌlkeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to gradually teach an idea or principle through repeated instruction  
    **中文释义：** 反复灌输；谆谆教导  
    **Example:** The program aims to inculcate respect for different cultures.  
    **中文：** 该项目旨在逐步培养对不同文化的尊重。

17. **inscrutable** /ɪnˈskruːtəbəl/  
    **English definition:** impossible to understand or interpret  
    **中文释义：** 高深莫测的；难以理解的  
    **Example:** The manager’s inscrutable expression gave no clue about her decision.  
    **中文：** 经理高深莫测的表情让人无法猜出她的决定。

18. **jeopardize** /ˈdʒepərdaɪz/  
    **English definition:** to put something valuable or important at risk  
    **中文释义：** 危及；使遭受风险  
    **Example:** Sharing confidential data could jeopardize the entire investigation.  
    **中文：** 共享机密数据可能危及整个调查。

19. **limpid** /ˈlɪmpɪd/  
    **English definition:** clear and transparent; expressed in a simple and easily understood way  
    **中文释义：** 清澈的；清晰易懂的  
    **Example:** The author explains the complex theory in limpid prose.  
    **中文：** 作者用清晰易懂的文字解释了这一复杂理论。

20. **palpable** /ˈpælpəbəl/  
    **English definition:** so clear or strong that it can almost be felt  
    **中文释义：** 明显的；强烈到几乎可以感受到的  
    **Example:** There was a palpable sense of relief when the missing child was found.  
    **中文：** 找到失踪儿童时，现场弥漫着明显的如释重负感。

21. **pejorative** /pɪˈdʒɔːrətɪv/  
    **English definition:** expressing disapproval or contempt  
    **中文释义：** 贬义的；表示轻蔑的  
    **Example:** The term is often used in a pejorative sense to dismiss opposing views.  
    **中文：** 这个词常被用于贬义，以轻易否定相反观点。

22. **pernicious** /pərˈnɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** having a harmful effect, especially in a gradual or less obvious way  
   **中文释义：** 有害的；有潜移默化危害的  
   **Example:** The report warns of the pernicious effects of misinformation on public trust.  
   **中文：** 报告警告说，错误信息会对公众信任造成潜移默化的危害。

23. **placid** /ˈplæsɪd/  
    **English definition:** calm and peaceful, with little movement or excitement  
    **中文释义：** 平静的；安宁的  
    **Example:** The lake looked placid before the sudden afternoon storm.  
    **中文：** 午后的暴风雨突然来临前，湖面看起来十分平静。

24. **promulgate** /ˈprɑːməlɡeɪt/  
    **English definition:** to officially announce or spread an idea, rule, or belief  
    **中文释义：** 正式公布；传播  
    **Example:** The agency will promulgate new safety standards next month.  
    **中文：** 该机构将在下个月正式公布新的安全标准。

25. **sycophant** /ˈsɪkəfænt/  
    **English definition:** a person who flatters someone powerful in order to gain an advantage  
    **中文释义：** 谄媚者；马屁精  
    **Example:** The executive surrounded himself with sycophants who never questioned his decisions.  
    **中文：** 这名高管身边围着一群从不质疑其决定的谄媚者。
