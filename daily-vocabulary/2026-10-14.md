# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-14

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **advantageous** /ˌædvənˈteɪdʒəs/  
   **English definition:** helpful or beneficial in a particular situation  
   **中文释义：** 有利的；有益的  
   **Example:** Booking early can be advantageous during the holiday season.  
   **中文：** 在假期期间，提前预订可能更有利。

2. **assemble** /əˈsembəl/  
   **English definition:** to bring people or parts together to form a group or complete object  
   **中文释义：** 集合；组装；装配  
   **Example:** The technicians assembled the prototype in under an hour.  
   **中文：** 技术人员不到一小时就组装好了原型。

3. **compile** /kəmˈpaɪl/  
   **English definition:** to collect information or items and arrange them into a list, report, or other whole  
   **中文释义：** 汇编；编制；整理  
   **Example:** Please compile the survey responses before Friday.  
   **中文：** 请在周五前整理好调查问卷的回复。

4. **consequence** /ˈkɑːnsəkwens/  
   **English definition:** a result or effect of an action or situation, especially one that matters  
   **中文释义：** 后果；结果；影响  
   **Example:** One consequence of the redesign was a shorter checkout process.  
   **中文：** 重新设计带来的一个结果是结账流程缩短了。

5. **constitute** /ˈkɑːnstətuːt/  
   **English definition:** to form or make up something; to be considered as something  
   **中文释义：** 构成；组成；算作  
   **Example:** Together, these steps constitute a complete review process.  
   **中文：** 这些步骤共同构成了一套完整的审查流程。

6. **deduct** /dɪˈdʌkt/  
   **English definition:** to subtract an amount from a total or take it away from a payment  
   **中文释义：** 扣除；减去  
   **Example:** The final bill deducts the deposit you paid last month.  
   **中文：** 最终账单会扣除你上个月支付的押金。

7. **differentiate** /ˌdɪfəˈrenʃieɪt/  
   **English definition:** to recognize or show the difference between people or things  
   **中文释义：** 区分；辨别；使有差别  
   **Example:** The label helps customers differentiate the two product versions.  
   **中文：** 这个标签有助于顾客区分两个产品版本。

8. **diverse** /daɪˈvɜːrs/  
   **English definition:** including many different types, people, or ideas  
   **中文释义：** 多种多样的；不同的；多元的  
   **Example:** The program draws participants from diverse professional backgrounds.  
   **中文：** 该项目吸引了来自不同职业背景的参与者。

9. **expose** /ɪkˈspoʊz/  
   **English definition:** to reveal something hidden or make someone or something unprotected  
   **中文释义：** 揭露；暴露；使面临  
   **Example:** The audit exposed gaps in how the team stored customer records.  
   **中文：** 审计揭示了团队存储客户记录时存在的漏洞。

10. **extend** /ɪkˈstend/  
   **English definition:** to make something longer or larger, or offer it to someone  
   **中文释义：** 延长；扩大；给予  
   **Example:** The library extended its opening hours during exam week.  
   **中文：** 考试周期间，图书馆延长了开放时间。

11. **influence** /ˈɪnfluəns/  
   **English definition:** to affect how someone or something develops, behaves, or is decided  
   **中文释义：** 影响；对……产生作用  
   **Example:** Early feedback influenced the final design.  
   **中文：** 早期反馈影响了最终设计。

12. **initiate** /ɪˈnɪʃieɪt/  
   **English definition:** to cause a process or activity to begin  
   **中文释义：** 发起；启动；开始  
   **Example:** The company initiated a review after the service outage.  
   **中文：** 服务中断后，公司启动了一项审查。

13. **inquire** /ɪnˈkwaɪər/  
   **English definition:** to ask for information, often in a formal or careful way  
   **中文释义：** 询问；打听；查询  
   **Example:** Several residents inquired about the construction schedule.  
   **中文：** 几位居民询问了施工安排。

14. **oppose** /əˈpoʊz/  
   **English definition:** to disagree with, resist, or try to prevent a plan or action  
   **中文释义：** 反对；抵制  
   **Example:** Some residents oppose the plan because it would remove public parking.  
   **中文：** 一些居民反对这项计划，因为它会取消公共停车位。

15. **predict** /prɪˈdɪkt/  
   **English definition:** to say what is likely to happen in the future, based on information or experience  
   **中文释义：** 预测；预言  
   **Example:** The model predicts higher demand in the winter months.  
   **中文：** 该模型预测冬季月份的需求会更高。

16. **purchase** /ˈpɜːrtʃəs/  
   **English definition:** to buy something; a formal word for buying  
   **中文释义：** 购买；采购  
   **Example:** Customers can purchase replacement parts through the website.  
   **中文：** 顾客可以通过网站购买替换零件。

17. **recognize** /ˈrekəɡnaɪz/  
   **English definition:** to identify someone or something from previous experience, or acknowledge a fact  
   **中文释义：** 认出；识别；承认  
   **Example:** The software can recognize duplicate entries automatically.  
   **中文：** 该软件能够自动识别重复条目。

18. **regard** /rɪˈɡɑːrd/  
   **English definition:** to think of or consider someone or something in a particular way  
   **中文释义：** 将……视为；认为；看待  
   **Example:** Many readers regard the essay as her clearest work.  
   **中文：** 许多读者认为这篇文章是她最清晰易懂的作品。

19. **relate** /rɪˈleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to be connected to something, or to understand another person’s experience  
   **中文释义：** 与……有关；联系；理解并感同身受  
   **Example:** The findings relate directly to the question we are trying to answer.  
   **中文：** 这些发现与我们试图回答的问题直接相关。

20. **substantial** /səbˈstænʃəl/  
   **English definition:** large in amount, size, or importance  
   **中文释义：** 大量的；重大的；实质性的  
   **Example:** The repairs require a substantial investment.  
   **中文：** 这项维修需要大量投入。

21. **suppress** /səˈpres/  
   **English definition:** to prevent an activity, sound, or feeling from being expressed or noticed  
   **中文释义：** 抑制；压制；阻止显现  
   **Example:** The new filter suppresses background noise during calls.  
   **中文：** 新的滤波器可以抑制通话中的背景噪声。

22. **thorough** /ˈθɜːroʊ/  
   **English definition:** careful and complete, with attention to every important detail  
   **中文释义：** 彻底的；全面的；细致的  
   **Example:** A thorough review caught two errors before publication.  
   **中文：** 一次全面审查在发布前发现了两个错误。

23. **undertake** /ˌʌndərˈteɪk/  
   **English definition:** to agree or commit to do a task, project, or responsibility  
   **中文释义：** 承担；着手进行；承诺做  
   **Example:** The agency undertook a survey of rural households.  
   **中文：** 该机构着手对农村家庭进行调查。

24. **wholesale** /ˈhoʊlseɪl/  
   **English definition:** relating to the sale of goods in large quantities, usually to businesses rather than consumers  
   **中文释义：** 批发的；批发地；批发业务  
   **Example:** The retailer buys seasonal goods wholesale and sells them through local stores.  
   **中文：** 这家零售商批量采购季节性商品，再通过当地门店销售。

25. **unify** /ˈjuːnɪfaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to bring separate people, systems, or parts together into one whole  
   **中文释义：** 统一；使联合；使成为一体  
   **Example:** The update will unify the two account systems.  
   **中文：** 这次更新将统一两个账户系统。
