# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-20

以下 25 个词汇偏进阶但实用，适合阅读、工作和表达中拓展使用。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **bulk** /bʌlk/  
   **English definition:** the main or largest part of something; a large quantity of goods  
   **中文释义：** 主体；大部分；大批量  
   **Example:** The bulk of the evidence supports the original explanation.  
   **中文：** 大部分证据都支持最初的解释。

2. **cease** /siːs/  
   **English definition:** to stop doing something or to come to an end  
   **中文释义：** 停止；终止  
   **Example:** The company agreed to cease operations at the site by June.  
   **中文：** 公司同意在六月前停止该地点的运营。

3. **comprehensive** /ˌkɑːmprɪˈhensɪv/  
   **English definition:** complete and including nearly everything that is relevant  
   **中文释义：** 全面的；综合的  
   **Example:** The report provides a comprehensive review of current safety procedures.  
   **中文：** 这份报告全面审查了现行安全流程。

4. **component** /kəmˈpoʊnənt/  
   **English definition:** one of the parts that combine to form a larger whole  
   **中文释义：** 组成部分；部件  
   **Example:** Trust is an essential component of an effective partnership.  
   **中文：** 信任是有效合作关系中不可或缺的组成部分。

5. **conclude** /kənˈkluːd/  
   **English definition:** to decide something after considering the evidence; to bring something to an end  
   **中文释义：** 得出结论；结束  
   **Example:** We concluded that the apparent decline was caused by a reporting change.  
   **中文：** 我们得出结论，表面上的下降是由报告方式变更造成的。

6. **conduct** /ˈkɑːndʌkt/  
   **English definition:** to organize and carry out an activity, study, or survey  
   **中文释义：** 进行；实施；开展  
   **Example:** The team conducted interviews with users in several regions.  
   **中文：** 团队采访了多个地区的用户。

7. **convey** /kənˈveɪ/  
   **English definition:** to communicate an idea, feeling, or message  
   **中文释义：** 传达；表达；传递  
   **Example:** A short example can convey the idea more clearly than a long explanation.  
   **中文：** 一个简短的例子有时比冗长的解释更能清楚地传达这个想法。

8. **crucial** /ˈkruːʃəl/  
   **English definition:** extremely important to the success or outcome of something  
   **中文释义：** 至关重要的；决定性的  
   **Example:** Clear communication is crucial when responsibilities are shared.  
   **中文：** 在共同承担职责时，清晰沟通至关重要。

9. **detect** /dɪˈtekt/  
   **English definition:** to discover or notice something, especially something that is difficult to see  
   **中文释义：** 察觉；发现；检测  
   **Example:** The new system can detect unusual activity within seconds.  
   **中文：** 新系统能在几秒内检测到异常活动。

10. **dispose** /dɪˈspoʊz/  
    **English definition:** to get rid of something; in “dispose of,” to deal with or remove something  
    **中文释义：** 处理；处置；丢弃  
    **Example:** Please dispose of confidential documents in the secure bins.  
    **中文：** 请将机密文件投入安全回收箱处理。

11. **dominate** /ˈdɑːməneɪt/  
    **English definition:** to control or have a stronger influence than others  
    **中文释义：** 支配；主导；占据优势  
    **Example:** Two large firms dominate the local market.  
    **中文：** 两家大型企业主导着当地市场。

12. **evolve** /ɪˈvɑːlv/  
    **English definition:** to develop gradually, often into a more advanced or different form  
    **中文释义：** 逐渐发展；演变  
    **Example:** The project evolved from a small pilot into a region-wide service.  
    **中文：** 这个项目从小规模试点逐步发展成覆盖全地区的服务。

13. **finite** /ˈfaɪnaɪt/  
    **English definition:** limited in size, amount, or duration  
    **中文释义：** 有限的；有限度的  
    **Example:** We have a finite amount of time, so let's focus on the key issues.  
    **中文：** 我们的时间有限，因此把精力放在关键问题上吧。

14. **framework** /ˈfreɪmwɜːrk/  
    **English definition:** a basic structure or set of principles used to organize or guide something  
    **中文释义：** 框架；体系；准则  
    **Example:** The policy creates a framework for evaluating new proposals.  
    **中文：** 这项政策建立了一套评估新提案的框架。

15. **hypothesis** /haɪˈpɑːθəsɪs/  
    **English definition:** an idea or explanation that can be tested through study or experimentation  
    **中文释义：** 假设；假说  
    **Example:** The researchers tested the hypothesis using data from three years.  
    **中文：** 研究人员用三年的数据检验了这一假设。

16. **identical** /aɪˈdentɪkəl/  
    **English definition:** exactly the same in every detail  
    **中文释义：** 完全相同的；一模一样的  
    **Example:** The two versions look identical, but their underlying settings differ.  
    **中文：** 这两个版本看起来完全相同，但底层设置并不一样。

17. **impose** /ɪmˈpoʊz/  
    **English definition:** to officially force a rule, tax, or restriction on someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 强加；实行；征收  
    **Example:** The city imposed a temporary limit on water use.  
    **中文：** 市政府对用水实行了临时限制。

18. **nonetheless** /ˌnʌnðəˈles/  
    **English definition:** despite what has just been said; nevertheless  
    **中文释义：** 尽管如此；然而  
    **Example:** The first attempt was unsuccessful; nonetheless, the team continued refining the design.  
    **中文：** 第一次尝试没有成功，尽管如此，团队仍继续改进设计。

19. **ongoing** /ˈɑːnˌɡoʊɪŋ/  
    **English definition:** continuing to happen or develop  
    **中文释义：** 持续进行的；正在发展的  
    **Example:** The findings are part of an ongoing investigation.  
    **中文：** 这些发现属于一项仍在进行的调查。

20. **parameter** /pəˈræmɪtər/  
    **English definition:** a limit or condition that controls how something can be done; a measurable factor in a system  
    **中文释义：** 参数；限定条件；范围  
    **Example:** We need to define the project parameters before estimating the cost.  
    **中文：** 估算成本前，我们需要先明确项目的范围和条件。

21. **phenomenon** /fəˈnɑːməˌnɑːn/  
    **English definition:** an event, fact, or situation that can be observed, especially one that is unusual or difficult to explain  
    **中文释义：** 现象；（非凡或异常的）事物  
    **Example:** The researchers are studying this phenomenon across different climates.  
    **中文：** 研究人员正在不同气候条件下研究这一现象。

22. **predominant** /prɪˈdɑːmɪnənt/  
    **English definition:** more common, powerful, or noticeable than other things of the same kind  
    **中文释义：** 占主导地位的；最普遍的  
    **Example:** Online sales became the predominant source of new customers.  
    **中文：** 线上销售成为新客户的主要来源。

23. **regime** /reɪˈʒiːm/  
    **English definition:** a system or method of government, management, or regulation  
    **中文释义：** 政体；管理制度；监管体系  
    **Example:** The agreement established a stricter regime for monitoring emissions.  
    **中文：** 这项协议建立了更严格的排放监测制度。

24. **simulate** /ˈsɪmjuleɪt/  
    **English definition:** to imitate the conditions or behavior of a real process, often for testing or training  
    **中文释义：** 模拟；仿真  
    **Example:** The software simulates heavy traffic so engineers can test the design.  
    **中文：** 这款软件能模拟繁忙路况，供工程师测试设计方案。

25. **whereas** /werˈæz/  
    **English definition:** used to compare two facts or situations that are different  
    **中文释义：** 而；然而；鉴于（用于对比）  
    **Example:** The older model is inexpensive, whereas the newer one uses less energy.  
    **中文：** 旧款价格便宜，而新款能耗更低。
