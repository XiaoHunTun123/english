# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-27

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **adumbrate** /ˈædʌmˌbreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to give only a general or unclear description of something  
   **中文释义：** 概略说明；隐约勾勒  
   **Example:** The report adumbrates a possible solution but provides few practical details.  
   **中文：** 报告概略提出了一个可能的解决方案，但很少提供实际细节。

2. **adventitious** /ˌædvenˈtɪʃəs/  
   **English definition:** happening by chance or arising from an external source  
   **中文释义：** 偶然发生的；外来的  
   **Example:** The researchers treated the unexpected result as an adventitious finding.  
   **中文：** 研究人员把这个意外结果视为偶然发现。

3. **aegis** /ˈiːdʒɪs/  
   **English definition:** protection, support, or sponsorship provided by a person or organization  
   **中文释义：** 保护；支持；庇护  
   **Example:** The project was completed under the aegis of an international education foundation.  
   **中文：** 该项目在一个国际教育基金会的支持下完成。

4. **archetypal** /ˌɑːrkɪˈtaɪpəl/  
   **English definition:** representing the most typical or perfect example of something  
   **中文释义：** 原型的；典型的  
   **Example:** The film presents an archetypal conflict between personal ambition and family duty.  
   **中文：** 这部电影展现了个人野心与家庭责任之间的典型冲突。

5. **atavistic** /ˌætəˈvɪstɪk/  
   **English definition:** showing a return to a very old or primitive behavior or belief  
   **中文释义：** 返祖的；退化到原始状态的  
   **Example:** The violent ritual was criticized as an atavistic practice with no place in modern society.  
   **中文：** 这种暴力仪式被批评为现代社会不应存在的原始习俗。

6. **avow** /əˈvaʊ/  
   **English definition:** to state openly and confidently that something is true  
   **中文释义：** 公开承认；坦白宣称  
   **Example:** The minister avowed that the policy would be revised after public consultation.  
   **中文：** 部长公开表示，政策将在征求公众意见后进行修订。

7. **bifurcate** /ˈbaɪfərkeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to divide into two separate parts or branches  
   **中文释义：** 分成两部分；分叉  
   **Example:** The trail bifurcates near the lake, with one route leading to the village.  
   **中文：** 小路在湖边分成两条，其中一条通往村庄。

8. **circuitous** /sɜːrˈkjuːɪtəs/  
   **English definition:** longer and less direct than necessary  
   **中文释义：** 迂回的；绕道的  
   **Example:** The application process was unnecessarily circuitous and confused many users.  
   **中文：** 申请流程不必要地迂回，让许多用户感到困惑。

9. **congruent** /ˈkɑːŋɡruənt/  
   **English definition:** matching or agreeing with something else  
   **中文释义：** 一致的；相符的  
   **Example:** The company’s actions were not congruent with its public commitment to sustainability.  
   **中文：** 公司的行为与其公开承诺的可持续发展理念并不一致。

10. **decadent** /ˈdekədənt/  
    **English definition:** characterized by excessive luxury or by a decline in moral or cultural standards  
    **中文释义：** 腐朽的；奢靡的；堕落的  
    **Example:** Critics viewed the extravagant celebration as a symbol of a decadent political culture.  
    **中文：** 批评者认为，这场奢华的庆典象征着一种腐朽的政治文化。

11. **evanescent** /ˌevəˈnesənt/  
    **English definition:** lasting for only a very short time and quickly disappearing  
    **中文释义：** 短暂易逝的；转瞬即逝的  
    **Example:** The artist tried to capture the evanescent colors of the sunset.  
    **中文：** 这位艺术家试图捕捉日落时转瞬即逝的色彩。

12. **exigent** /ˈeksɪdʒənt/  
    **English definition:** requiring immediate attention or action because of urgency  
    **中文释义：** 紧急的；迫切需要处理的  
    **Example:** The shelter was created in response to an exigent need for temporary housing.  
    **中文：** 这个避难所是为了应对临时住房的迫切需求而设立的。

13. **felicitous** /fəˈlɪsɪtəs/  
    **English definition:** well chosen, suitable, or pleasingly expressed  
    **中文释义：** 恰当的；措辞得体的；幸运的  
    **Example:** Her felicitous choice of words helped calm the disagreement.  
    **中文：** 她措辞得体，有助于缓和分歧。

14. **hagiography** /ˌhæɡiˈɑːɡrəfi/  
    **English definition:** a biography that presents someone as perfect or saintly and ignores faults  
    **中文释义：** 圣徒传；过分美化的传记  
    **Example:** The documentary avoids hagiography by discussing the leader’s failures as well as achievements.  
    **中文：** 这部纪录片既讨论这位领导人的成就，也讨论其失败之处，避免了过度美化。

15. **hegemony** /hɪˈdʒeməni/  
    **English definition:** dominant influence or control by one group, country, or ideology  
    **中文释义：** 霸权；主导地位  
    **Example:** The company’s technological hegemony has been challenged by several innovative rivals.  
    **中文：** 这家公司的技术霸权受到了几家创新型竞争对手的挑战。

16. **insouciant** /ɪnˈsuːsiənt/  
    **English definition:** relaxed and unconcerned, especially in a way that suggests confidence  
    **中文释义：** 无忧无虑的；漫不经心的  
    **Example:** He adopted an insouciant manner even when the negotiations became tense.  
    **中文：** 即使谈判变得紧张，他仍保持着漫不经心的姿态。

17. **interregnum** /ˌɪntərˈreɡnəm/  
    **English definition:** a period when normal government or leadership is suspended  
    **中文释义：** 政权空档期；过渡时期  
    **Example:** During the interregnum, a temporary council managed the city’s essential services.  
    **中文：** 在过渡时期，一个临时委员会负责管理城市的基本服务。

18. **liminal** /ˈlɪmɪnəl/  
    **English definition:** relating to a transitional stage between two conditions or places  
    **中文释义：** 处于过渡阶段的；临界的  
    **Example:** Graduates often experience a liminal period between university and full-time work.  
    **中文：** 毕业生常常会经历一段从大学到全职工作的过渡期。

19. **lugubrious** /ləˈɡuːbriəs/  
    **English definition:** excessively mournful or gloomy  
    **中文释义：** 忧郁悲哀的；阴沉的  
    **Example:** The film’s lugubrious tone made even ordinary scenes feel tragic.  
    **中文：** 这部电影忧郁的基调让普通场景也显得悲剧性十足。

20. **machination** /ˌmækɪˈneɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** a secret and complicated plan, especially one intended to gain an advantage  
    **中文释义：** 阴谋；幕后策划  
    **Example:** The investigation exposed a machination to manipulate the bidding process.  
    **中文：** 调查揭露了一场操纵竞标流程的阴谋。

21. **mendacity** /menˈdæsəti/  
    **English definition:** dishonesty or a tendency to lie  
    **中文释义：** 虚假；撒谎；不诚实  
    **Example:** The witness’s mendacity became clear when the documents were examined.  
    **中文：** 审查文件后，证人的不诚实变得十分明显。

22. **obsequious** /əbˈsiːkwiəs/  
    **English definition:** too eager to please someone powerful by behaving in a servile way  
    **中文释义：** 谄媚的；卑躬屈膝的  
    **Example:** The manager disliked the consultant’s obsequious praise and preferred direct feedback.  
    **中文：** 经理不喜欢顾问卑躬屈膝的吹捧，更希望听到直接的反馈。

23. **paucity** /ˈpɔːsəti/  
    **English definition:** a very small amount or number of something  
    **中文释义：** 缺乏；少量  
    **Example:** The paucity of reliable data made it difficult to evaluate the program.  
    **中文：** 可靠数据的缺乏使评估该项目变得困难。

24. **peroration** /ˌperəˈreɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** the final part of a speech, often used to make a strong emotional appeal  
    **中文释义：** 演讲结尾；结语  
    **Example:** In his peroration, the speaker urged the audience to act immediately.  
    **中文：** 演讲者在结语中敦促听众立即采取行动。

25. **quiescent** /kwiˈesənt/  
    **English definition:** temporarily inactive, quiet, or not developing  
    **中文释义：** 暂时静止的；不活跃的  
    **Example:** The volcano has remained quiescent for more than a century.  
    **中文：** 这座火山已经沉寂了一个多世纪。
