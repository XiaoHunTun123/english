# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-13

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abnegate** /ˈæbnɪɡeɪt/ — give up or deny oneself something；放弃某物或克制自己的欲望；放弃；克制
   - **Example:** The leader chose to abnegate personal ambition for the sake of the team.
   - **中文：** 这位领导为了团队选择放弃个人抱负。

2. **acclimate** /ˈækləmeɪt/ — adjust to a new environment or situation；适应新的环境或情况；适应
   - **Example:** It took the new employees several weeks to acclimate to the company culture.
   - **中文：** 新员工花了几周时间才适应公司文化。

3. **accolade** /ˈækəleɪd/ — an award or expression of praise；奖项或赞扬的表达；荣誉；赞誉
   - **Example:** The scientist received international accolades for her research.
   - **中文：** 这位科学家因其研究获得了国际赞誉。

4. **admonitory** /ədˈmɑːnətɔːri/ — expressing a warning or mild criticism；表达警告或轻微批评的；警诫的
   - **Example:** The director gave the team an admonitory reminder about data security.
   - **中文：** 主任以警诫的口吻提醒团队注意数据安全。

5. **amenable** /əˈmiːnəbəl/ — willing to accept suggestions or likely to be influenced；愿意接受建议或容易受影响的；顺从的；乐于接受的
   - **Example:** The client was amenable to revising the delivery schedule.
   - **中文：** 客户愿意接受修改交付时间表的建议。

6. **antipathy** /ænˈtɪpəθi/ — a strong feeling of dislike；强烈的不喜欢；反感；厌恶
   - **Example:** His antipathy toward bureaucracy was clear from his repeated complaints.
   - **中文：** 他对官僚作风的反感从反复的抱怨中显而易见。

7. **archetype** /ˈɑːrkɪtaɪp/ — a typical example or original model；典型例子或原始模型；原型；典范
   - **Example:** The character is an archetype of the ambitious outsider.
   - **中文：** 这个人物是雄心勃勃的局外人的典型原型。

8. **ascetic** /əˈsetɪk/ — avoiding physical comforts for spiritual or self-disciplined reasons；为了精神追求或自律而避免物质享受的；苦行的；禁欲的
   - **Example:** The monk followed an ascetic lifestyle with very few possessions.
   - **中文：** 这位僧人过着物品极少的苦行生活。

9. **auspice** /ˈɔːspɪs/ — support, protection, or sponsorship；支持、保护或赞助；支持；赞助
   - **Example:** The exhibition was held under the auspices of the city museum.
   - **中文：** 这次展览是在市博物馆的支持下举办的。

10. **aversion** /əˈvɜːrʒən/ — a strong dislike or desire to avoid something；强烈的不喜欢或回避某事的愿望；厌恶
    - **Example:** She has a strong aversion to making decisions without sufficient evidence.
    - **中文：** 她非常反感在证据不足的情况下作决定。

11. **cacophony** /kəˈkɑːfəni/ — a harsh mixture of unpleasant sounds；刺耳且令人不快的声音混合；刺耳声
    - **Example:** A cacophony of alarms interrupted the morning meeting.
    - **中文：** 一阵刺耳的警报声打断了早晨的会议。

12. **composure** /kəmˈpoʊʒər/ — calm control of one’s feelings and behavior；对自己的情绪和行为保持冷静的状态；镇定
    - **Example:** She maintained her composure while answering difficult questions.
    - **中文：** 回答棘手问题时，她保持了镇定。

13. **contrite** /kənˈtraɪt/ — genuinely sorry and remorseful for wrongdoing；对错误行为真诚抱歉并感到悔恨的；悔悟的
    - **Example:** The contrite manager apologized directly to the affected customers.
    - **中文：** 那位悔悟的经理直接向受影响的顾客道了歉。

14. **derogatory** /dɪˈrɑːɡətɔːri/ — expressing disrespect or a low opinion；表达不尊重或轻蔑看法的；贬低的
    - **Example:** The moderator removed several derogatory comments from the forum.
    - **中文：** 版主删除了论坛中的几条贬低性评论。

15. **dissonance** /ˈdɪsənəns/ — a lack of harmony or a conflict between ideas；缺乏和谐或观点之间的冲突；不和谐；矛盾
    - **Example:** There was a noticeable dissonance between the company’s promises and its actions.
    - **中文：** 公司的承诺与行动之间存在明显矛盾。

16. **eclipse** /ɪˈklɪps/ — surpass someone or something in importance or attention；在重要性或关注度上超过某人或某事；使黯然失色
    - **Example:** The young performer’s success soon eclipsed that of her mentor.
    - **中文：** 这位年轻表演者的成功很快使她导师的成就黯然失色。

17. **equivocate** /ɪˈkwɪvəkeɪt/ — use ambiguous language to avoid giving a clear answer；使用含糊语言以避免明确回答；含糊其辞
    - **Example:** The spokesperson continued to equivocate when asked about the missing funds.
    - **中文：** 当被问及失踪资金时，发言人一直含糊其辞。

18. **lament** /ləˈment/ — express sorrow, regret, or disappointment；表达悲伤、遗憾或失望；哀叹；惋惜
    - **Example:** Residents lamented the loss of the historic theater.
    - **中文：** 居民们对那座历史剧院的消失深感惋惜。

19. **magnitude** /ˈmæɡnɪtuːd/ — the great size, scale, or importance of something；某事物的巨大规模、程度或重要性；量级；规模
    - **Example:** The engineers underestimated the magnitude of the repair work.
    - **中文：** 工程师低估了维修工作的规模。

20. **obfuscate** /ˈɑːbfəskeɪt/ — make something unclear or difficult to understand；使某事不清楚或难以理解；混淆
    - **Example:** The report uses technical language to obfuscate a simple financial problem.
    - **中文：** 这份报告用技术术语混淆了一个简单的财务问题。

21. **prodigious** /prəˈdɪdʒəs/ — very large, impressive, or extraordinary；非常巨大、令人印象深刻或非凡的；巨大的；惊人的
    - **Example:** The team made a prodigious effort to complete the bridge on time.
    - **中文：** 团队付出了惊人的努力，按时完成了桥梁建设。

22. **prosaic** /proʊˈzeɪɪk/ — ordinary, dull, or lacking imagination；普通、乏味或缺乏想象力的；平淡的
    - **Example:** The documentary transformed a prosaic subject into an engaging story.
    - **中文：** 这部纪录片把一个平淡的主题变成了引人入胜的故事。

23. **rancor** /ˈræŋkər/ — persistent bitterness or resentment；持续的痛苦怨恨或憎恶；怨恨
    - **Example:** The two sides reached an agreement without rancor.
    - **中文：** 双方没有带着怨恨，达成了协议。

24. **redoubtable** /rɪˈdaʊtəbəl/ — formidable, respected, and difficult to defeat；强大、受尊敬且难以击败的；令人敬畏的
    - **Example:** She is a redoubtable opponent with decades of experience.
    - **中文：** 她是一位令人敬畏的对手，拥有数十年的经验。

25. **remiss** /rɪˈmɪs/ — careless or negligent in fulfilling a duty；履行职责时粗心或疏忽的；失职的
    - **Example:** We would be remiss if we failed to thank the volunteers.
    - **中文：** 如果我们不感谢志愿者，那就是我们的失职。
