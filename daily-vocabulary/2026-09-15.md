# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-15

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **allege** /əˈledʒ/ — claim that someone has done something wrong, without yet proving it；声称某人做了错事但尚未证明；指控；声称
   - **Example:** The report alleges that the data were deliberately concealed.
   - **中文：** 报告指控这些数据曾被故意隐瞒。

2. **allude** /əˈluːd/ — refer to something indirectly or without stating it clearly；间接提及某事而不明确说出；暗指
   - **Example:** The speaker alluded to recent disagreements without naming anyone.
   - **中文：** 演讲者没有点名，只是暗指了最近的分歧。

3. **artifice** /ˈɑːrtɪfɪs/ — a clever device or strategy, especially one used to deceive；聪明的手段或策略，尤其是用于欺骗的；计谋；诡计
   - **Example:** The advertisement relies on artifice rather than solid evidence.
   - **中文：** 这则广告依靠的是花招，而不是可靠证据。

4. **axiom** /ˈæksiəm/ — a statement accepted as true and used as a basis for reasoning；被认为正确并作为推理基础的陈述；公理；基本原则
   - **Example:** The team works from the axiom that clear goals improve performance.
   - **中文：** 这个团队以“明确的目标能提高表现”这一基本原则开展工作。

5. **banal** /bəˈnɑːl/ — so ordinary or overused that it lacks originality；普通或使用过度而缺乏新意的；陈腐的
   - **Example:** The article offered banal advice that readers had heard many times before.
   - **中文：** 这篇文章提供的是读者早已听过多次的陈腐建议。

6. **brazen** /ˈbreɪzən/ — bold and shameless, often in a way that is offensive；大胆而不知羞耻的，通常令人反感；厚颜无耻的
   - **Example:** The brazen attempt to alter the records was quickly discovered.
   - **中文：** 这次厚颜无耻地篡改记录的企图很快就被发现了。

7. **credible** /ˈkredəbəl/ — believable and worthy of trust；可信且值得信赖的；可信的
   - **Example:** Investigators found no credible explanation for the discrepancy.
   - **中文：** 调查人员没有找到能够解释这一不一致的可信说法。

8. **gainsay** /ˌɡeɪnˈseɪ/ — deny, contradict, or dispute something；否认、反驳或质疑某事；否认；反驳
   - **Example:** No one can gainsay the value of regular practice.
   - **中文：** 没有人能够否认定期练习的价值。

9. **irresolute** /ɪˈrezəluːt/ — unable to make a firm decision；无法作出坚定决定的；优柔寡断的
   - **Example:** His irresolute response left the team unsure about the next step.
   - **中文：** 他优柔寡断的回应让团队不确定下一步该怎么做。

10. **jargon** /ˈdʒɑːrɡən/ — specialized language used by a particular profession or group；某一职业或群体使用的专业语言；行话
    - **Example:** The manual avoids technical jargon so that beginners can follow it.
    - **中文：** 这本手册避免使用技术行话，以便初学者理解。

11. **jettison** /ˈdʒetɪsən/ — abandon or discard something that is no longer useful；放弃或丢弃不再有用的事物；抛弃
    - **Example:** The team decided to jettison the original plan and start again.
    - **中文：** 团队决定抛弃原计划，重新开始。

12. **meager** /ˈmiːɡər/ — small in amount or lacking in quality；数量少或质量不足的；微薄的；贫乏的
    - **Example:** The charity survived on meager donations during its first year.
    - **中文：** 这家慈善机构第一年靠微薄的捐款维持运转。

13. **obdurate** /ˈɑːbdərət/ — stubbornly refusing to change one’s opinion or course；固执地拒绝改变观点或做法的；顽固的
    - **Example:** The obdurate official rejected every proposed compromise.
    - **中文：** 那位顽固的官员拒绝了所有提出的妥协方案。

14. **perplex** /pərˈpleks/ — confuse someone and make a problem difficult to understand；使某人困惑，使问题难以理解；使困惑
    - **Example:** The conflicting instructions perplexed the new employees.
    - **中文：** 相互矛盾的指示让新员工感到困惑。

15. **repercussion** /ˌriːpərˈkʌʃən/ — an unintended consequence of an action or event；某项行动或事件产生的意外后果；反响；后果
    - **Example:** The decision could have serious repercussions for small businesses.
    - **中文：** 这个决定可能会给小企业带来严重后果。

16. **rhetorical** /rɪˈtɔːrɪkəl/ — relating to persuasive language or a question asked for effect；与说服性语言或为增强效果而提出的问题有关的；修辞的；反问的
    - **Example:** “Who would accept such a deal?” was a rhetorical question.
    - **中文：** “谁会接受这样的交易？”是一个反问句。

17. **staunch** /stɔːntʃ/ — strongly loyal, committed, or supportive；忠诚、坚定或支持态度强烈的；坚定的
    - **Example:** She has remained a staunch supporter of public education.
    - **中文：** 她一直是公共教育的坚定支持者。

18. **surfeit** /ˈsɜːrfɪt/ — an excessive amount of something；过量的某种事物；过剩；过量
    - **Example:** A surfeit of information can make it harder to identify the key facts.
    - **中文：** 信息过量会让人更难找出关键事实。

19. **taciturn** /ˈtæsɪtɜːrn/ — tending to say very little；倾向于很少说话的；沉默寡言的
    - **Example:** The taciturn engineer communicated mainly through concise written notes.
    - **中文：** 那位沉默寡言的工程师主要通过简短的书面笔记交流。

20. **temerity** /təˈmerəti/ — excessive confidence that may seem rude or reckless；过度自信，以至于显得无礼或鲁莽；鲁莽；冒失
    - **Example:** He had the temerity to criticize the plan without reading it.
    - **中文：** 他没读计划，就冒失地批评了它。

21. **thwart** /θwɔːrt/ — prevent someone from achieving a purpose；阻止某人实现目标；阻挠
    - **Example:** Strong winds thwarted the crew’s attempt to reach the island.
    - **中文：** 强风阻挠了船员抵达岛屿的尝试。

22. **underpin** /ˌʌndərˈpɪn/ — support, strengthen, or form the basis of something；支持、加强或构成某事物的基础；支撑
    - **Example:** Reliable statistics underpin the report’s main conclusions.
    - **中文：** 可靠的统计数据支撑着报告的主要结论。

23. **vicarious** /vaɪˈkeriəs/ — experienced indirectly through another person；通过他人间接体验的；间接感受到的
    - **Example:** She gained vicarious enjoyment from watching her children perform.
    - **中文：** 观看孩子们表演时，她间接感受到了快乐。

24. **wield** /wiːld/ — use power, influence, or a tool effectively；有效运用权力、影响力或工具；运用
    - **Example:** The editor wields considerable influence over the publication’s style.
    - **中文：** 这位编辑对刊物的风格有相当大的影响力。

25. **transcendent** /trænˈsendənt/ — surpassing ordinary limits or experience；超越普通界限或经验的；卓越的；超凡的
    - **Example:** The performance created a transcendent moment for the entire audience.
    - **中文：** 这场演出为全体观众创造了一个超凡的时刻。
