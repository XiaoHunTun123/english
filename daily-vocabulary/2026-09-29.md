# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-29

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **apothegm** /ˈæpəθem/  
   **English definition:** a short, clever, and memorable saying that expresses a general truth  
   **中文释义：** 格言；警句  
   **Example:** The essay opens with an apothegm about the value of patience.  
   **中文：** 这篇文章以一句关于耐心价值的格言开头。

2. **apotheosis** /əˌpɑːθiˈoʊsɪs/  
   **English definition:** the highest point or perfect example of something  
   **中文释义：** 完美典范；顶峰  
   **Example:** Many fans regard the final performance as the apotheosis of her career.  
   **中文：** 许多粉丝认为最后一场演出是她职业生涯的巅峰。

3. **autarky** /ˈɔːtɑːrki/  
   **English definition:** economic independence based on producing everything a country needs itself  
   **中文释义：** 经济自给自足；经济自立  
   **Example:** The policy of autarky proved unrealistic for a country dependent on imported energy.  
   **中文：** 对一个依赖能源进口的国家而言，经济自给自足的政策并不现实。

4. **bilious** /ˈbɪliəs/  
   **English definition:** bad-tempered or showing an unpleasantly angry mood  
   **中文释义：** 暴躁的；脾气坏的  
   **Example:** The bilious customer complained about every detail of the service.  
   **中文：** 那位脾气暴躁的顾客对服务的每个细节都表示不满。

5. **blithe** /blaɪð/  
   **English definition:** carefree and cheerful, sometimes without considering possible problems  
   **中文释义：** 无忧无虑的；轻率乐观的  
   **Example:** His blithe confidence disappeared when the first serious problem emerged.  
   **中文：** 第一个严重问题出现时，他轻率的自信消失了。

6. **broach** /broʊtʃ/  
   **English definition:** to introduce a difficult or sensitive subject for discussion  
   **中文释义：** 提出敏感话题；开始讨论  
   **Example:** She waited until the meeting was nearly over to broach the issue of budget cuts.  
   **中文：** 她等到会议快结束时才提出削减预算的问题。

7. **chary** /ˈtʃeri/  
   **English definition:** cautious and unwilling to take risks or trust something completely  
   **中文释义：** 谨慎的；小心提防的  
   **Example:** Investors remain chary of companies that make vague promises about future profits.  
   **中文：** 对于那些含糊承诺未来利润的公司，投资者仍然十分谨慎。

8. **complaisant** /kəmˈpleɪzənt/  
   **English definition:** willing to please others and accept what they want  
   **中文释义：** 迎合的；顺从的  
   **Example:** The complaisant assistant rarely challenged the director’s assumptions.  
   **中文：** 那位一味顺从的助理很少质疑主管的假设。

9. **comport** /kəmˈpɔːrt/  
   **English definition:** to behave in a particular way or be consistent with something  
   **中文释义：** 表现；举止；与……相一致  
   **Example:** Her actions did not comport with the professional standards of the organization.  
   **中文：** 她的行为与该组织的职业标准不一致。

10. **contumacious** /ˌkɑːntəˈmeɪʃəs/  
    **English definition:** stubbornly refusing to obey authority or a rule  
    **中文释义：** 顽固抗命的；拒不服从的  
    **Example:** The judge imposed a fine on the contumacious witness.  
    **中文：** 法官对拒不服从的证人处以罚款。

11. **cupidity** /kjuːˈpɪdəti/  
    **English definition:** an excessive desire for wealth or possessions  
    **中文释义：** 贪婪；贪财  
    **Example:** The scandal revealed how cupidity had corrupted the procurement process.  
    **中文：** 这起丑闻揭示了贪婪如何腐蚀采购流程。

12. **dilettante** /ˌdɪləˈtɑːnt/  
    **English definition:** a person who takes an interest in an activity without serious commitment or expertise  
    **中文释义：** 业余爱好者；浅尝辄止的人  
    **Example:** He is more than a dilettante and has studied classical music for decades.  
    **中文：** 他不只是浅尝辄止的爱好者，已经研究古典音乐几十年了。

13. **discomfit** /dɪsˈkʌmfɪt/  
    **English definition:** to make someone feel embarrassed, confused, or uncomfortable  
    **中文释义：** 使尴尬；使不安；使困惑  
    **Example:** The unexpected question discomfited the spokesperson during the interview.  
    **中文：** 这个意外问题让发言人在采访中感到尴尬不安。

14. **divest** /daɪˈvest/  
    **English definition:** to remove something from someone or to sell an investment or business  
    **中文释义：** 剥夺；出售资产；撤资  
    **Example:** The company plans to divest its least profitable division.  
    **中文：** 公司计划出售利润最低的部门。

15. **ebullience** /ɪˈbʊliəns/  
    **English definition:** a quality of being cheerful, energetic, and enthusiastic  
    **中文释义：** 热情洋溢；活力  
    **Example:** Her ebullience made even routine team meetings feel lively.  
    **中文：** 她的热情让即使是例行团队会议也显得充满活力。

16. **emollient** /ɪˈmɑːliənt/  
    **English definition:** soothing or softening, especially for the skin or for tense feelings  
    **中文释义：** 柔软滋润的；起缓和作用的  
    **Example:** The mediator used an emollient tone to ease tensions between the two sides.  
    **中文：** 调解员用缓和的语气来减轻双方的紧张情绪。

17. **excoriation** /ˌekskɔːriˈeɪʃən/  
    **English definition:** severe criticism or the removal of the surface of something  
    **中文释义：** 严厉批评；剥落  
    **Example:** The policy received widespread excoriation from consumer groups.  
    **中文：** 这项政策遭到了消费者团体的广泛严厉批评。

18. **factional** /ˈfækʃənəl/  
    **English definition:** relating to disagreements between smaller groups within a larger group  
    **中文释义：** 派系的；党派纷争的  
    **Example:** Factional disputes prevented the committee from reaching a decision.  
    **中文：** 派系争端使委员会无法作出决定。

19. **fecund** /ˈfekənd/  
    **English definition:** producing many ideas, results, or offspring; highly fertile  
    **中文释义：** 多产的；富有创造力的；肥沃的  
    **Example:** The workshop was a fecund environment for developing new research ideas.  
    **中文：** 这次研讨会为提出新的研究思路提供了富有成果的环境。

20. **flout** /flaʊt/  
    **English definition:** to openly ignore or disobey a rule, law, or convention  
    **中文释义：** 公然蔑视；违反  
    **Example:** Businesses that flout environmental regulations may face heavy penalties.  
    **中文：** 公然违反环保法规的企业可能面临重罚。

21. **gauche** /ɡoʊʃ/  
    **English definition:** socially awkward and lacking confidence or good manners  
    **中文释义：** 不善交际的；笨拙失礼的  
    **Example:** His gauche attempt at humor made the formal dinner uncomfortable.  
    **中文：** 他笨拙的玩笑让这场正式晚宴变得尴尬。

22. **iconoclast** /aɪˈkɑːnəklæst/  
    **English definition:** a person who challenges widely accepted beliefs or traditions  
    **中文释义：** 打破传统者；反传统者  
    **Example:** The iconoclast researcher questioned assumptions that had guided the field for decades.  
    **中文：** 这位反传统的研究者质疑了指导该领域几十年的假设。

23. **imbroglio** /ɪmˈbroʊlioʊ/  
    **English definition:** a confusing and complicated disagreement or situation  
    **中文释义：** 纠纷；错综复杂的局面  
    **Example:** The contract dispute developed into a legal imbroglio involving several countries.  
    **中文：** 这场合同纠纷演变成了涉及多个国家的复杂法律争端。

24. **imperious** /ɪmˈpɪriəs/  
    **English definition:** arrogant and expecting to be obeyed  
    **中文释义：** 傲慢专横的；盛气凌人的  
    **Example:** The executive’s imperious manner discouraged employees from offering honest feedback.  
    **中文：** 这名高管傲慢专横的态度让员工不敢提供真实反馈。

25. **impute** /ɪmˈpjuːt/  
    **English definition:** to attribute a fault, responsibility, or quality to someone or something  
    **中文释义：** 归咎于；把……归因于  
    **Example:** The report imputed the failure to poor planning rather than individual negligence.  
    **中文：** 报告把失败归因于计划不周，而不是个人疏忽。
