# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-07-27 (Additional Set)

本日追加词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。本文件因当天试推送已存在而单独保存，未覆盖原文件。

1. **nuance** /ˈnuːɑːns/ — a subtle difference in meaning or feeling；细微差别  
   *The translation misses an important nuance.*  
   这个翻译遗漏了一个重要的细微差别。

2. **scrutinize** /ˈskruːtənaɪz/ — to examine something very carefully；仔细审查  
   *The committee scrutinized every detail of the proposal.*  
   委员会仔细审查了提案的每个细节。

3. **corroborate** /kəˈrɑːbəreɪt/ — to confirm a statement with additional evidence；证实  
   *The witness's account was corroborated by video footage.*  
   目击者的说法得到了视频证据的证实。

4. **mitigate** /ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/ — to make something less severe or harmful；缓解；减轻  
   *These measures may mitigate the environmental impact.*  
   这些措施可能会减轻对环境的影响。

5. **foster** /ˈfɔːstər/ — to encourage the development of something；促进；培养  
   *The program fosters creativity among students.*  
   这个项目培养学生的创造力。

6. **hamper** /ˈhæmpər/ — to make it difficult for something to happen；妨碍  
   *Bad weather hampered the rescue effort.*  
   恶劣天气妨碍了救援行动。

7. **invoke** /ɪnˈvoʊk/ — to refer to a rule, law, or authority；援引；调用  
   *The lawyer invoked the constitutional right to privacy.*  
   律师援引了宪法赋予的隐私权。

8. **infer** /ɪnˈfɜːr/ — to reach a conclusion from evidence；推断  
   *We can infer his intentions from his actions.*  
   我们可以从他的行为推断他的意图。

9. **indispensable** /ˌɪndɪˈspensəbəl/ — absolutely necessary；不可或缺的  
   *Trust is indispensable in a healthy relationship.*  
   信任是健康关系中不可或缺的因素。

10. **negligible** /ˈneɡlɪdʒəbəl/ — too small to be important；微不足道的；可忽略的  
    *The difference in cost is negligible.*  
    成本差异微乎其微。

11. **versatile** /ˈvɜːrsətəl/ — able to be used in many different ways；多用途的；多才多艺的  
    *This versatile tool is useful for many tasks.*  
    这个多用途工具适合完成许多任务。

12. **arbitrary** /ˈɑːrbətreri/ — based on personal choice rather than a clear reason；任意的；武断的  
    *The rule seems arbitrary and unfair.*  
    这条规定似乎既武断又不公平。

13. **inherent** /ɪnˈhɪrənt/ — existing as a natural or essential part；固有的；内在的  
    *Every investment carries inherent risks.*  
    每项投资都带有固有风险。

14. **explicit** /ɪkˈsplɪsɪt/ — stated clearly and directly；明确的；直白的  
    *The instructions provide explicit guidance.*  
    说明提供了明确的指导。

15. **implicit** /ɪmˈplɪsɪt/ — suggested without being directly stated；含蓄的；暗含的  
    *There was an implicit warning in his message.*  
    他的信息中含有一个暗示性的警告。

16. **contentious** /kənˈtenʃəs/ — likely to cause disagreement；有争议的  
    *The proposal remains a contentious issue.*  
    这项提案仍然是一个有争议的问题。

17. **compelling** /kəmˈpelɪŋ/ — convincing or extremely persuasive；有说服力的；令人信服的  
    *She presented a compelling argument.*  
    她提出了一个令人信服的论点。

18. **consecutive** /kənˈsekjətɪv/ — following one after another without interruption；连续的  
    *He worked for ten consecutive days.*  
    他连续工作了十天。

19. **compatible** /kəmˈpætəbəl/ — able to exist or work well together；兼容的；相容的  
    *This software is compatible with most devices.*  
    这款软件兼容大多数设备。

20. **prevalent** /ˈprevələnt/ — common or widespread；普遍的；盛行的  
    *The belief is prevalent in many cultures.*  
    这种观念在许多文化中都很普遍。

21. **rectify** /ˈrektɪfaɪ/ — to correct a problem or mistake；纠正；改正  
    *We need to rectify the error immediately.*  
    我们需要立即纠正这个错误。

22. **supplement** /ˈsʌplɪment/ — something added to improve or complete something；补充；补充物  
    *The guide supplements the information in the textbook.*  
    这份指南补充了教材中的信息。

23. **anticipate** /ænˈtɪsəpeɪt/ — to expect or predict something；预期；预料  
    *We anticipate a sharp increase in demand.*  
    我们预计需求会大幅增加。

24. **expedite** /ˈekspədaɪt/ — to make a process happen more quickly；加快；促进  
    *The new system will expedite the approval process.*  
    新系统将加快审批流程。

25. **profound** /prəˈfaʊnd/ — very deep, serious, or meaningful；深刻的；意义深远的  
    *The experience had a profound effect on her.*  
    这段经历对她产生了深远的影响。
