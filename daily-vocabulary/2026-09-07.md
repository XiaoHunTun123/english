# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-07

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **allegory** /ˈæləɡɔːri/ — a story with a hidden moral or political meaning；含有隐含道德或政治意义的故事；寓言
   - **Example:** The novel is an allegory about the dangers of unchecked power.
   - **中文：** 这部小说是一则关于不受约束的权力所带来危险的寓言。

2. **anachronistic** /əˌnækrəˈnɪstɪk/ — belonging to a different period from the one being discussed；属于与所讨论时期不同的时代的；不合时代的
   - **Example:** The film’s anachronistic dialogue made the historical setting less convincing.
   - **中文：** 电影中不合时代的对白削弱了历史背景的可信度。

3. **astute** /əˈstuːt/ — able to understand situations and make good judgments；善于理解情况并作出正确判断的；精明的
   - **Example:** Her astute questions revealed the hidden assumption in the plan.
   - **中文：** 她精明的问题揭示了计划中隐藏的假设。

4. **atrophy** /ˈætrəfi/ — gradually become weaker or smaller through disuse；因长期不用而逐渐变弱或缩小；萎缩
   - **Example:** Without regular practice, language skills can atrophy quickly.
   - **中文：** 如果不经常练习，语言能力可能会迅速退化。

5. **boisterous** /ˈbɔɪstərəs/ — noisy, energetic, and cheerful；喧闹、精力充沛且愉快的；喧嚣的
   - **Example:** The boisterous crowd continued celebrating long after the game ended.
   - **中文：** 比赛结束很久后，喧闹的人群仍在庆祝。

6. **coerce** /koʊˈɜːrs/ — force someone to do something through threats or pressure；通过威胁或压力迫使某人做某事；胁迫
   - **Example:** The witness claimed that he had been coerced into signing the statement.
   - **中文：** 证人声称自己是被胁迫签署那份声明的。

7. **convergence** /kənˈvɜːrdʒəns/ — the process of coming together or becoming similar；聚集或变得相似的过程；汇聚；趋同
   - **Example:** The convergence of several technologies created a new type of device.
   - **中文：** 多项技术的融合创造出了一种新型设备。

8. **curtail** /kɜːrˈteɪl/ — reduce or shorten something；减少或缩短某事物；削减
   - **Example:** The company had to curtail spending during the economic downturn.
   - **中文：** 经济低迷期间，公司不得不削减开支。

9. **devoid** /dɪˈvɔɪd/ — completely lacking something；完全缺乏某物的；没有……的
   - **Example:** His explanation was devoid of any useful evidence.
   - **中文：** 他的解释完全没有任何有用的证据。

10. **dissipate** /ˈdɪsɪpeɪt/ — gradually disappear or waste something carelessly；逐渐消失或不慎浪费某物；消散；挥霍
    - **Example:** The morning mist dissipated as the sun rose.
    - **中文：** 太阳升起后，晨雾逐渐消散了。

11. **empathy** /ˈempəθi/ — the ability to understand another person’s feelings；理解他人感受的能力；同理心
    - **Example:** A good counselor listens with patience and empathy.
    - **中文：** 优秀的咨询师会带着耐心和同理心倾听。

12. **hinder** /ˈhɪndər/ — make it difficult for something to happen or progress；使某事难以发生或进展；阻碍
    - **Example:** Poor communication can hinder cooperation between departments.
    - **中文：** 沟通不畅会阻碍部门之间的合作。

13. **inhibit** /ɪnˈhɪbɪt/ — restrain or prevent an action or process；抑制或阻止某个行动或过程；抑制
    - **Example:** Fear of making mistakes may inhibit creative thinking.
    - **中文：** 害怕犯错可能会抑制创造性思维。

14. **mundane** /mʌnˈdeɪn/ — ordinary, routine, and not exciting；普通、日常且不令人兴奋的；平凡的
    - **Example:** Automation has freed staff from many mundane tasks.
    - **中文：** 自动化使员工摆脱了许多平凡重复的任务。

15. **obstinate** /ˈɑːbstɪnət/ — stubbornly refusing to change one’s opinion or behavior；固执地拒绝改变观点或行为的；顽固的
    - **Example:** The obstinate negotiator rejected every reasonable compromise.
    - **中文：** 那位顽固的谈判者拒绝了每一项合理的妥协方案。

16. **permeate** /ˈpɜːrmieɪt/ — spread throughout every part of something；遍布某事物的各个部分；渗透
    - **Example:** A spirit of caution permeated the entire organization.
    - **中文：** 整个组织都弥漫着谨慎的氛围。

17. **stagnant** /ˈstæɡnənt/ — not moving, changing, or developing；不流动、不变化或不发展的；停滞的
    - **Example:** The industry became stagnant after years without innovation.
    - **中文：** 多年没有创新后，这个行业陷入了停滞。

18. **discrepancy** /dɪˈskrepənsi/ — a difference between things that should match；本应一致的事物之间的差异；不一致
    - **Example:** The auditor noticed a discrepancy between the records and the actual payments.
    - **中文：** 审计员发现记录与实际付款之间存在不一致。

19. **dispel** /dɪˈspel/ — make a doubt, fear, or belief disappear；使疑虑、恐惧或某种看法消失；驱散
    - **Example:** The detailed report helped dispel rumors about the project.
    - **中文：** 这份详细报告帮助驱散了有关该项目的谣言。

20. **embargo** /ɪmˈbɑːrɡoʊ/ — an official ban on trade or other activity；对贸易或其他活动的官方禁令；禁运
    - **Example:** The country imposed an embargo on sensitive military equipment.
    - **中文：** 该国对敏感军事设备实施了禁运。

21. **proponent** /prəˈpoʊnənt/ — a person who supports an idea or proposal；支持某一想法或提议的人；支持者
    - **Example:** She is a strong proponent of flexible working arrangements.
    - **中文：** 她是灵活工作安排的坚定支持者。

22. **rebut** /rɪˈbʌt/ — prove that a statement or argument is wrong；证明某个陈述或论点错误；反驳
    - **Example:** The lawyer rebutted the accusation with independent evidence.
    - **中文：** 律师用独立证据反驳了这项指控。

23. **relinquish** /rɪˈlɪŋkwɪʃ/ — give up something, especially unwillingly；放弃某物，尤其是被迫放弃；放弃；让出
    - **Example:** The founder refused to relinquish control of the company.
    - **中文：** 创始人拒绝放弃对公司的控制权。

24. **residual** /rɪˈzɪdʒuəl/ — remaining after the main part has been removed；主要部分被去除后剩余的；残余的
    - **Example:** The treatment eliminated the infection but left some residual pain.
    - **中文：** 治疗消除了感染，但留下了一些残余疼痛。

25. **revere** /rɪˈvɪr/ — feel deep respect and admiration for someone；对某人怀有深深的尊敬和钦佩；崇敬
    - **Example:** The community continues to revere the scientist for her contributions.
    - **中文：** 社区仍然因为这位科学家的贡献而崇敬她。
