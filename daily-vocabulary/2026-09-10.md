# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-10

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abominable** /əˈbɑːmɪnəbəl/ — extremely unpleasant or morally unacceptable；极其令人不快或道德上不可接受的；糟糕的；可憎的
   - **Example:** The tenants complained about the abominable living conditions.
   - **中文：** 租户抱怨糟糕的居住条件。

2. **apocryphal** /əˈpɑːkrɪfəl/ — probably not true, although widely repeated；可能不真实但被广泛传播的；真伪存疑的
   - **Example:** The apocryphal story has been repeated for generations.
   - **中文：** 这个真伪存疑的故事已经流传了好几代。

3. **apposite** /ˈæpəzɪt/ — especially appropriate or relevant；特别恰当或相关的；切合的
   - **Example:** She made an apposite comparison during the discussion.
   - **中文：** 她在讨论中作了一个十分贴切的比较。

4. **augur** /ˈɔːɡər/ — be a sign that something will happen；预示某事将要发生；预兆
   - **Example:** Strong early sales augur well for the product’s future.
   - **中文：** 强劲的初期销量预示着这款产品前景良好。

5. **bucolic** /bjuːˈkɑːlɪk/ — relating to the pleasant aspects of the countryside；与乡村宁静美好的一面有关的；田园的
   - **Example:** They spent the weekend in a bucolic village surrounded by hills.
   - **中文：** 他们在一个群山环绕的田园村庄度过了周末。

6. **cloying** /ˈklɔɪɪŋ/ — excessively sweet, sentimental, or flattering；过分甜腻、多愁善感或奉承的；甜腻的
   - **Example:** The advertisement’s cloying optimism made it difficult to take seriously.
   - **中文：** 广告中过分甜腻的乐观语气让人难以认真看待。

7. **conscientious** /ˌkɑːnʃiˈenʃəs/ — careful, responsible, and thorough；认真负责且周到的；尽责的
   - **Example:** She is a conscientious employee who checks every detail.
   - **中文：** 她是一名尽责的员工，会检查每一个细节。

8. **corollary** /ˈkɔːrəleri/ — a direct and natural consequence of something；某事物直接而自然的结果；推论；必然结果
   - **Example:** A corollary of remote work is the need for clearer online communication.
   - **中文：** 远程工作的一个必然结果是需要更清晰的线上沟通。

9. **credulous** /ˈkredʒələs/ — too ready to believe something without enough evidence；没有足够证据就轻易相信的；轻信的
   - **Example:** Credulous readers may accept the fabricated story as fact.
   - **中文：** 轻信的读者可能会把编造的故事当成事实。

10. **defunct** /dɪˈfʌŋkt/ — no longer existing, operating, or used；不再存在、运行或使用的；已废止的
    - **Example:** The museum now occupies the site of a defunct railway station.
    - **中文：** 这座博物馆如今坐落在一座已废弃火车站的旧址上。

11. **demur** /dɪˈmɜːr/ — raise doubts or objections；提出疑问或反对意见；表示异议；犹豫
    - **Example:** The committee did not demur when the revised plan was presented.
    - **中文：** 修订后的计划提交时，委员会没有提出异议。

12. **exacting** /ɪɡˈzæktɪŋ/ — requiring great effort, care, or precision；需要极大努力、细心或精确度的；要求苛刻的
    - **Example:** The job is exacting, but it offers valuable professional experience.
    - **中文：** 这份工作要求很高，但能提供宝贵的职业经验。

13. **exhort** /ɪɡˈzɔːrt/ — strongly encourage or urge someone to do something；强烈鼓励或敦促某人做某事；劝诫
    - **Example:** The coach exhorted the players to remain focused until the final whistle.
    - **中文：** 教练敦促队员们一直保持专注，直到终场哨响。

14. **flummox** /ˈflʌməks/ — confuse or perplex someone；使某人困惑或不知所措；难住
    - **Example:** The unexpected change in the data flummoxed the research team.
    - **中文：** 数据的意外变化让研究团队不知所措。

15. **entrenched** /ɪnˈtrentʃt/ — firmly established and difficult to change；根深蒂固且难以改变的；牢固确立的
    - **Example:** The reform challenged several entrenched habits in the organization.
    - **中文：** 这项改革挑战了组织中一些根深蒂固的习惯。

16. **erode** /ɪˈroʊd/ — gradually wear away or weaken something；逐渐磨损或削弱某物；侵蚀
    - **Example:** Repeated delays can erode public confidence in the service.
    - **中文：** 反复延误会逐渐削弱公众对这项服务的信心。

17. **erroneous** /ɪˈroʊniəs/ — incorrect or based on a mistake；错误的或基于错误的；不正确的
    - **Example:** The report was withdrawn after several erroneous figures were discovered.
    - **中文：** 发现几处错误数据后，这份报告被撤回了。

18. **foment** /foʊˈment/ — encourage trouble, conflict, or rebellion；煽动麻烦、冲突或反抗；煽动
    - **Example:** The article was accused of fomenting distrust between the communities.
    - **中文：** 这篇文章被指责煽动了社区之间的不信任。

19. **inane** /ɪˈneɪn/ — silly, empty, or lacking meaning；愚蠢、空洞或缺乏意义的；空泛的
    - **Example:** The discussion was filled with inane comments and repeated complaints.
    - **中文：** 讨论中充斥着空洞的评论和反复的抱怨。

20. **incidental** /ˌɪnsɪˈdentəl/ — minor or occurring as a side effect；次要的或作为附带结果发生的；附带的
    - **Example:** The cost of printing is incidental compared with the cost of research.
    - **中文：** 与研究成本相比，印刷费用只是次要的。

21. **intangible** /ɪnˈtændʒəbəl/ — unable to be touched or difficult to define precisely；无法触摸或难以准确定义的；无形的
    - **Example:** Trust is an intangible asset that can determine a company’s success.
    - **中文：** 信任是一种无形资产，可能决定公司的成败。

22. **maladroit** /ˌmæləˈdrɔɪt/ — clumsy or ineffective in movement or action；动作笨拙或行动不得当的；笨拙的
    - **Example:** His maladroit response created more confusion than clarity.
    - **中文：** 他不得当的回应造成了更多混乱，而不是带来清晰说明。

23. **mercurial** /mɜːrˈkjʊəriəl/ — often changing mood, behavior, or opinion；情绪、行为或观点经常变化的；多变的
    - **Example:** The director’s mercurial decisions made long-term planning difficult.
    - **中文：** 导演多变的决定让长期规划变得困难。

24. **misnomer** /ˌmɪsˈnoʊmər/ — a name or description that is inaccurate；不准确的名称或描述；名不副实的称呼
    - **Example:** Calling the shortcut “easy” is something of a misnomer.
    - **中文：** 把这条捷径称为“简单路线”其实有些名不副实。

25. **panacea** /ˌpænəˈsiːə/ — a solution believed to solve every problem；被认为能解决所有问题的办法；万灵药
    - **Example:** Technology is useful, but it is not a panacea for social inequality.
    - **中文：** 技术很有用，但并不是解决社会不平等的万灵药。
