# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-12

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **complacency** /kəmˈpleɪsənsi/ — excessive satisfaction that prevents improvement or awareness of danger；阻碍改进或使人忽视危险的过度满足；自满
   - **Example:** Early success led to complacency, and competitors soon caught up.
   - **中文：** 早期的成功导致了自满，竞争对手很快就赶了上来。

2. **concession** /kənˈseʃən/ — something granted or admitted, often during a negotiation；常在谈判中给予或承认的事物；让步；承认
   - **Example:** The union made a concession to help finalize the agreement.
   - **中文：** 工会作出了一项让步，以帮助最终达成协议。

3. **conclusive** /kənˈkluːsɪv/ — proving something so clearly that there is no doubt；清楚证明某事而不留疑问的；决定性的
   - **Example:** The test did not provide conclusive evidence of a link between the two events.
   - **中文：** 这项测试没有提供两起事件之间存在联系的决定性证据。

4. **contention** /kənˈtenʃən/ — a strongly held opinion or a disagreement；坚定持有的观点或争议；主张；争论
   - **Example:** His central contention is that the policy benefits large firms disproportionately.
   - **中文：** 他的核心主张是，这项政策让大公司获得了不成比例的利益。

5. **converge** /kənˈvɜːrdʒ/ — move toward the same point or become increasingly similar；向同一点移动或变得越来越相似；汇聚；趋同
   - **Example:** The two research teams began to converge on the same explanation.
   - **中文：** 两个研究团队开始逐渐得出相同的解释。

6. **deterrent** /dɪˈtɜːrənt/ — something that discourages an action by making it seem difficult or risky；通过使某种行为显得困难或有风险而加以阻止的事物；威慑因素
   - **Example:** Heavy fines can serve as a deterrent to illegal dumping.
   - **中文：** 高额罚款可以对非法倾倒垃圾起到威慑作用。

7. **disclose** /dɪˈskloʊz/ — make information known, especially information previously kept secret；公开某些信息，尤其是此前保密的信息；披露
   - **Example:** The company must disclose any potential conflicts of interest.
   - **中文：** 公司必须披露任何潜在的利益冲突。

8. **docile** /ˈdoʊsəl/ — quiet and easy to control or teach；安静且容易控制或教导的；温顺的
   - **Example:** The normally docile dog became nervous in the crowded station.
   - **中文：** 平时温顺的狗在拥挤的车站里变得紧张起来。

9. **eccentric** /ɪkˈsentrɪk/ — unconventional and slightly strange in behavior or appearance；在行为或外表上不合常规且略显古怪的；古怪的
   - **Example:** The eccentric inventor filled his workshop with unusual machines.
   - **中文：** 那位古怪的发明家在工作室里摆满了不寻常的机器。

10. **expound** /ɪkˈspaʊnd/ — explain an idea or theory in detail；详细解释某个想法或理论；阐述
    - **Example:** The lecturer spent an hour expounding the principles of ethical design.
    - **中文：** 讲师花了一个小时阐述道德设计的原则。

11. **fathom** /ˈfæðəm/ — understand something after careful thought；经过仔细思考后理解某事；彻底理解
    - **Example:** I cannot fathom why the warning was ignored.
    - **中文：** 我无法理解为什么那个警告被忽视了。

12. **germane** /dʒɜːrˈmeɪn/ — directly relevant to the subject being discussed；与所讨论主题直接相关的；相关的
    - **Example:** Please keep your questions germane to the topic of the hearing.
    - **中文：** 请确保你的问题与听证会主题直接相关。

13. **gratify** /ˈɡrætɪfaɪ/ — please or satisfy someone；使某人高兴或满足；满足
    - **Example:** It gratified her to see the students apply what they had learned.
    - **中文：** 看到学生运用所学知识，她感到很满足。

14. **hindsight** /ˈhaɪndsaɪt/ — understanding of an event only after it has happened；事件发生后才获得的理解；事后之见
    - **Example:** With hindsight, we should have checked the supplier more carefully.
    - **中文：** 事后看来，我们本应该更仔细地核查供应商。

15. **insightful** /ˈɪnsaɪtfəl/ — showing a deep and accurate understanding；表现出深刻而准确理解的；有洞察力的
    - **Example:** Her insightful comments changed the direction of the discussion.
    - **中文：** 她富有洞察力的评论改变了讨论的方向。

16. **intuitive** /ɪnˈtuːɪtɪv/ — understood or known instinctively without conscious reasoning；无需有意识推理而凭直觉理解或知晓的；直觉的
    - **Example:** The new interface is intuitive enough for first-time users.
    - **中文：** 新界面对首次使用者来说足够直观。

17. **irascible** /ɪˈræsəbəl/ — easily made angry；容易生气的；易怒的
    - **Example:** The irascible supervisor apologized after realizing he had overreacted.
    - **中文：** 那位易怒的主管意识到自己反应过度后道了歉。

18. **legitimate** /lɪˈdʒɪtɪmət/ — lawful, valid, or reasonable；合法、有效或合理的；正当的
    - **Example:** The residents have a legitimate concern about the proposed construction.
    - **中文：** 居民对拟议中的建设有合理的担忧。

19. **malicious** /məˈlɪʃəs/ — intending to cause harm or upset；意图造成伤害或烦扰的；恶意的
    - **Example:** The website was targeted by a malicious attack.
    - **中文：** 这个网站遭到了恶意攻击。

20. **monolithic** /ˌmɑːnəˈlɪθɪk/ — very large, uniform, and difficult to change；庞大、单一且难以改变的；铁板一块的
    - **Example:** The monolithic organization struggled to respond to local needs.
    - **中文：** 这个庞大的单一组织难以回应当地需求。

21. **nonplussed** /ˌnɑːnˈplʌst/ — so surprised or confused that one does not know how to react；因惊讶或困惑而不知如何反应的；不知所措的
    - **Example:** She was nonplussed by the sudden change in the schedule.
    - **中文：** 日程突然改变让她不知所措。

22. **notorious** /noʊˈtɔːriəs/ — famous for something bad or unpleasant；因坏事或令人不快之事而出名的；臭名昭著的
    - **Example:** The intersection is notorious for its frequent traffic accidents.
    - **中文：** 这个十字路口因交通事故频发而臭名昭著。

23. **onerous** /ˈoʊnərəs/ — involving a heavy burden or demanding great effort；负担沉重或需要极大努力的；繁重的
    - **Example:** The new reporting requirements placed an onerous burden on small businesses.
    - **中文：** 新的报告要求给小企业带来了沉重负担。

24. **perpetuate** /pərˈpetʃueɪt/ — make something continue indefinitely；使某事无限期持续；延续
    - **Example:** The reforms aim to reduce inequality rather than perpetuate it.
    - **中文：** 这些改革旨在减少不平等，而不是让它延续下去。

25. **precedent** /ˈpresɪdənt/ — an earlier case that guides future decisions；为未来决定提供参考的先前案例；先例
    - **Example:** The ruling could set an important precedent for digital privacy cases.
    - **中文：** 这项裁决可能为数字隐私案件树立重要先例。
