# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-02

本日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。

1. **abject** /ˈæbdʒekt/ — extremely bad or hopeless；糟糕透顶的；卑微的  
   *The project ended in abject failure.*  
   这个项目以惨败告终。

2. **acrimonious** /ˌækrɪˈmoʊniəs/ — bitter and angry in tone；尖刻的；激烈的  
   *The meeting ended in an acrimonious dispute.*  
   会议以一场激烈的争执收场。

3. **alacrity** /əˈlækrəti/ — cheerful readiness and eagerness；欣然；敏捷  
   *She accepted the invitation with alacrity.*  
   她欣然接受了邀请。

4. **amorphous** /əˈmɔːrfəs/ — without a clear shape or structure；无定形的；模糊的  
   *The plan remained amorphous after several discussions.*  
   多次讨论后，这个计划仍然没有明确框架。

5. **assuage** /əˈsweɪdʒ/ — to make an unpleasant feeling less intense；缓和；减轻  
   *The apology did little to assuage her concerns.*  
   这份道歉几乎没有缓解她的担忧。

6. **bellwether** /ˈbelˌweðər/ — a person or thing that leads or indicates trends；风向标；先导  
   *The company is a bellwether for the technology industry.*  
   这家公司是科技行业的风向标。

7. **cogitate** /ˈkɑːdʒɪteɪt/ — to think carefully about something；认真思考  
   *He took a week to cogitate on the proposal.*  
   他花了一周认真思考这份提案。

8. **conflagration** /ˌkɑːnfləˈɡreɪʃən/ — a large destructive fire；大火；灾难性火灾  
   *The forest conflagration spread rapidly in the dry weather.*  
   干燥天气下，森林大火迅速蔓延。

9. **debilitate** /dɪˈbɪlɪteɪt/ — to make someone or something weak；使衰弱；削弱  
   *The illness debilitated him for several months.*  
   这场疾病使他虚弱了好几个月。

10. **decry** /dɪˈkraɪ/ — to publicly criticize something；谴责；公开批评  
    *Activists decried the lack of environmental protection.*  
    活动人士公开谴责缺乏环境保护。

11. **didactic** /daɪˈdæktɪk/ — intended to teach, sometimes too obviously；说教的；教诲性的  
    *The novel is entertaining without becoming didactic.*  
    这部小说很有趣，并没有变得过于说教。

12. **disparage** /dɪˈspærɪdʒ/ — to speak of someone or something as unimportant；贬低；贬损  
    *You should not disparage work you have not studied.*  
    你不应贬低自己不了解的工作。

13. **effusive** /ɪˈfjuːsɪv/ — expressing feelings openly and enthusiastically；热情洋溢的  
    *The host gave us an effusive welcome.*  
    主人热情洋溢地欢迎了我们。

14. **enervate** /ˈenərveɪt/ — to weaken or drain energy；使衰弱；使精疲力竭  
    *Long hours in the heat can enervate the body.*  
    长时间处于高温中会使身体精疲力竭。

15. **esoteric** /ˌesəˈterɪk/ — understood by only a small specialized group；深奥的；小众的  
    *The lecture dealt with an esoteric branch of mathematics.*  
    这场讲座讨论了数学中一个深奥的分支。

16. **flounder** /ˈflaʊndər/ — to struggle to make progress；挣扎；举步维艰  
    *The new business floundered without a clear strategy.*  
    没有明确策略，这家新企业举步维艰。

17. **garrulous** /ˈɡærələs/ — talking a lot, especially about unimportant things；喋喋不休的  
    *The garrulous passenger kept everyone awake.*  
    那位喋喋不休的乘客让大家都无法入睡。

18. **impervious** /ɪmˈpɜːrviəs/ — not affected or influenced by something；不受影响的；不透水的  
    *She seemed impervious to criticism.*  
    她似乎不受批评的影响。

19. **indolent** /ˈɪndələnt/ — lazy and avoiding activity；懒惰的；懒散的  
    *An indolent approach will not solve the problem.*  
    懒散的态度无法解决问题。

20. **intransigent** /ɪnˈtrænsɪdʒənt/ — unwilling to change one’s position；固执的；不妥协的  
    *The two sides remained intransigent during the talks.*  
    谈判期间双方都不愿妥协。

21. **languid** /ˈlæŋɡwɪd/ — lacking energy or urgency；慵懒的；无精打采的  
    *The afternoon passed in a languid rhythm.*  
    午后在慵懒的节奏中度过。

22. **mendacious** /menˈdeɪʃəs/ — dishonest or lying；虚假的；撒谎的  
    *The report was dismissed as mendacious propaganda.*  
    这份报告被斥为虚假的宣传。

23. **opulent** /ˈɑːpjələnt/ — richly luxurious；奢华的；富丽堂皇的  
    *The hotel lobby was decorated in an opulent style.*  
    酒店大堂装修得富丽堂皇。

24. **quell** /kwel/ — to put an end to something, especially unrest；平息；镇压  
    *The leader tried to quell the rumors.*  
    领导者试图平息谣言。

25. **quixotic** /kwɪkˈsɑːtɪk/ — idealistic but unrealistic；不切实际的；堂吉诃德式的  
    *His quixotic plan was admirable but impractical.*  
    他的理想主义计划令人钦佩，但并不切实际。
