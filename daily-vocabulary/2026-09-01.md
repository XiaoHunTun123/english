# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-01

本日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体约处于英语内容覆盖率 75%–90% 的范围。

1. **aberration** /ˌæbəˈreɪʃən/ — a departure from what is normal；偏差；异常现象  
   *The error was an aberration rather than a regular problem.*  
   这个错误是偶然偏差，并非经常出现的问题。

2. **acquiesce** /ˌækwiˈes/ — to accept something reluctantly；默许；勉强同意  
   *She eventually acquiesced to their demands.*  
   她最终默许了他们的要求。

3. **adroit** /əˈdrɔɪt/ — skillful and clever；熟练的；机巧的  
   *He gave an adroit response to the criticism.*  
   他巧妙地回应了批评。

4. **antithesis** /ænˈtɪθəsɪs/ — the exact opposite of something；对立面；反面  
   *Her calm manner was the antithesis of his impatience.*  
   她的冷静与他的急躁截然相反。

5. **apathetic** /ˌæpəˈθetɪk/ — showing little interest or emotion；冷漠的；无动于衷的  
   *Voters became apathetic after repeated disappointments.*  
   反复失望后，选民变得无动于衷。

6. **ardent** /ˈɑːrdənt/ — showing passionate enthusiasm；热情的；热烈的  
   *He is an ardent supporter of public education.*  
   他热烈支持公共教育。

7. **assiduous** /əˈsɪdʒuəs/ — showing persistent hard work；勤勉的；坚持不懈的  
   *Her assiduous preparation led to excellent results.*  
   她勤奋的准备带来了出色的结果。

8. **belie** /bɪˈlaɪ/ — to give a false impression of something；掩饰；与……不符  
   *His relaxed appearance belied his anxiety.*  
   他轻松的外表掩饰了内心的焦虑。

9. **censure** /ˈsenʃər/ — strong official criticism；谴责；严厉批评  
   *The committee censured the official for misconduct.*  
   委员会谴责了这名官员的不当行为。

10. **conjecture** /kənˈdʒektʃər/ — an opinion based on incomplete evidence；推测；猜想  
    *The cause of the failure remains a matter of conjecture.*  
    失败的原因仍然只是推测。

11. **culpable** /ˈkʌlpəbəl/ — deserving blame for a fault；应受责备的；有过错的  
    *The investigation found the company culpable for the breach.*  
    调查认定该公司应对这次违规负责。

12. **dearth** /dɜːrθ/ — a lack or scarcity of something；缺乏；短缺  
    *The region is facing a dearth of skilled workers.*  
    该地区正面临技术工人短缺。

13. **deferential** /ˌdefəˈrenʃəl/ — showing respectful submission；恭敬的；顺从的  
    *The intern was deferential toward the senior researchers.*  
    实习生对资深研究人员十分恭敬。

14. **dichotomy** /daɪˈkɑːtəmi/ — a division into two contrasting groups；二分法；对立  
    *The debate presents a false dichotomy between growth and sustainability.*  
    这场辩论把增长与可持续性错误地对立起来。

15. **diffident** /ˈdɪfɪdənt/ — shy because of a lack of confidence；缺乏自信的；羞怯的  
    *His diffident manner hid considerable expertise.*  
    他缺乏自信的举止掩盖了丰富的专业知识。

16. **egregious** /ɪˈɡriːdʒəs/ — outstandingly bad or shocking；极其恶劣的  
    *The report exposed egregious violations of safety rules.*  
    报告揭露了极其恶劣的安全违规行为。

17. **exonerate** /ɪɡˈzɑːnəreɪt/ — officially clear someone of blame；为……洗清罪名  
    *The new evidence exonerated the accused.*  
    新证据洗清了被告的罪名。

18. **impetuous** /ɪmˈpetʃuəs/ — acting suddenly without careful thought；冲动的；鲁莽的  
    *His impetuous decision created unnecessary risks.*  
    他冲动的决定带来了不必要的风险。

19. **incisive** /ɪnˈsaɪsɪv/ — clear, direct, and intelligent；深刻的；敏锐的  
    *The journalist asked several incisive questions.*  
    记者提出了几个尖锐而深刻的问题。

20. **magnanimous** /mæɡˈnænɪməs/ — generous and forgiving toward a rival；宽宏大量的  
    *She was magnanimous in victory.*  
    她在胜利后表现得十分宽宏大量。

21. **perfunctory** /pərˈfʌŋktəri/ — done with little care or enthusiasm；敷衍的；草率的  
    *He offered a perfunctory apology and left.*  
    他敷衍地道了歉，然后离开了。

22. **retrospective** /ˌretrəˈspektɪv/ — looking back on past events；回顾性的；追溯的  
    *The museum is holding a retrospective exhibition of her work.*  
    博物馆正在举办她的作品回顾展。

23. **surreptitious** /ˌsɜːrəpˈtɪʃəs/ — kept secret and done stealthily；偷偷摸摸的；秘密的  
    *He took a surreptitious glance at his phone.*  
    他偷偷瞥了一眼手机。

24. **tenuous** /ˈtenjuəs/ — weak, slight, or not convincing；脆弱的；牵强的  
    *The connection between the two events is tenuous.*  
    这两件事之间的联系很牵强。

25. **unprecedented** /ʌnˈpresɪdentɪd/ — never having happened before；前所未有的  
    *The city experienced unprecedented growth.*  
    这座城市经历了前所未有的增长。
