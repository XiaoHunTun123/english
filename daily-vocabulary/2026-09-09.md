# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-09-09

今日词汇定位：偏生僻但实用，整体适合英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的学习阶段。每个词提供音标、英释、中释及双语例句。

1. **abeyance** /əˈbeɪəns/ — a temporary state in which something is not active or decided；某事暂时停止或尚未决定的状态；搁置
   - **Example:** The proposal has been held in abeyance until more data are available.
   - **中文：** 在获得更多数据之前，这项提议一直处于搁置状态。

2. **acquiescent** /ˌækwiˈesənt/ — willing to accept something without protest；愿意不加反对地接受某事的；默许的
   - **Example:** The usually outspoken team became acquiescent after the safety concerns were explained.
   - **中文：** 解释安全问题后，平时直言不讳的团队默许了这一安排。

3. **beguile** /bɪˈɡaɪl/ — charm or entertain someone, sometimes deceptively；迷住或取悦某人，有时带有欺骗性；吸引；诱骗
   - **Example:** The storyteller beguiled the children with an imaginative tale.
   - **中文：** 讲故事的人用富有想象力的故事吸引了孩子们。

4. **commensurate** /kəˈmenʃərət/ — corresponding in size, degree, or importance；在大小、程度或重要性上相称的；相当的
   - **Example:** The salary should be commensurate with the responsibilities of the role.
   - **中文：** 薪资应与这个职位承担的责任相称。

5. **compendium** /kəmˈpendiəm/ — a concise collection of information on a subject；某一主题的信息汇编或简明集成；概要；大全
   - **Example:** The handbook is a useful compendium of local regulations.
   - **中文：** 这本手册是当地法规的一份实用汇编。

6. **conducive** /kənˈduːsɪv/ — helping to produce a particular result；有助于产生某种结果的；有利于
   - **Example:** A quiet environment is conducive to focused work.
   - **中文：** 安静的环境有利于专注工作。

7. **encroach** /ɪnˈkroʊtʃ/ — gradually intrude on someone’s space, rights, or time；逐渐侵占某人的空间、权利或时间；侵占
   - **Example:** Constant notifications began to encroach on her personal time.
   - **中文：** 持续不断的通知开始侵占她的私人时间。

8. **equanimity** /ˌiːkwəˈnɪməti/ — calmness and emotional balance in a difficult situation；在困难处境中的镇定和情绪平衡；平静
   - **Example:** He handled the unexpected criticism with remarkable equanimity.
   - **中文：** 面对意外的批评，他表现出了非凡的镇定。

9. **expedient** /ɪkˈspiːdiənt/ — convenient and practical, though not always morally ideal；方便实用但不一定符合道德理想的；权宜的
   - **Example:** Delaying the launch may be expedient, but it will not solve the underlying problem.
   - **中文：** 推迟发布也许是权宜之计，但无法解决根本问题。

10. **fickle** /ˈfɪkəl/ — changing opinions, loyalties, or feelings frequently；观点、忠诚或感情经常变化的；反复无常的
    - **Example:** Consumer preferences can be fickle in a rapidly changing market.
    - **中文：** 在快速变化的市场中，消费者偏好可能反复无常。

11. **idiosyncratic** /ˌɪdiəsɪŋˈkrætɪk/ — peculiar to a particular person or group；某个人或群体特有的；独特的
    - **Example:** Her idiosyncratic method of organizing notes works surprisingly well.
    - **中文：** 她独特的笔记整理方法出人意料地有效。

12. **indict** /ɪnˈdaɪt/ — formally accuse someone of a serious crime；正式指控某人犯有严重罪行；控告
    - **Example:** The grand jury voted to indict the former executive.
    - **中文：** 大陪审团投票决定控告那名前高管。

13. **ineffable** /ɪnˈefəbəl/ — too great or beautiful to be expressed in words；美好或强烈到难以用语言表达的；难言的
    - **Example:** The view from the summit gave them an ineffable sense of freedom.
    - **中文：** 山顶的景色给了他们一种难以言表的自由感。

14. **innovative** /ˈɪnəveɪtɪv/ — introducing new ideas, methods, or technology；引入新思想、方法或技术的；创新的
    - **Example:** The company is known for its innovative approach to urban transport.
    - **中文：** 这家公司以其创新的城市交通方案而闻名。

15. **insipid** /ɪnˈsɪpɪd/ — lacking flavor, energy, or interest；缺乏味道、活力或趣味的；乏味的
    - **Example:** The article was accurate but insipid, offering no fresh perspective.
    - **中文：** 这篇文章虽然准确，却很乏味，没有提供新颖的视角。

16. **intrepid** /ɪnˈtrepɪd/ — fearless and adventurous；无所畏惧且喜欢冒险的；勇敢无畏的
    - **Example:** The intrepid journalist continued reporting from the remote region.
    - **中文：** 那位勇敢无畏的记者继续在偏远地区进行报道。

17. **irrefutable** /ˌɪrɪˈfjuːtəbəl/ — impossible to deny or disprove；不可能否认或反驳的；无可辩驳的
    - **Example:** The video provided irrefutable evidence of the incident.
    - **中文：** 这段视频为事件提供了无可辩驳的证据。

18. **leeway** /ˈliːweɪ/ — freedom to act or make decisions within limits；在一定范围内行动或作决定的自由；回旋余地
    - **Example:** The contract gives the designer some leeway to adjust the schedule.
    - **中文：** 合同给设计师留出了一些调整时间表的余地。

19. **novice** /ˈnɑːvɪs/ — a person who is new to a skill or activity；刚接触某项技能或活动的人；新手
    - **Example:** Even a novice can use the software after a short tutorial.
    - **中文：** 即使是新手，经过简短教程后也能使用这款软件。

20. **oblivious** /əˈblɪviəs/ — unaware of what is happening around one；没有意识到周围正在发生之事的；未察觉的
    - **Example:** He was oblivious to the growing tension in the room.
    - **中文：** 他没有察觉到房间里日益紧张的气氛。

21. **ominous** /ˈɑːmɪnəs/ — suggesting that something bad may happen；预示坏事可能发生的；不祥的
    - **Example:** Dark clouds gathered, creating an ominous atmosphere before the storm.
    - **中文：** 暴风雨前乌云聚集，营造出一种不祥的气氛。

22. **opportunistic** /ˌɑːpərtuːˈnɪstɪk/ — taking advantage of circumstances for personal gain；利用形势谋取个人利益的；机会主义的
    - **Example:** The critic dismissed the announcement as an opportunistic publicity stunt.
    - **中文：** 评论家认为这项公告只是一次机会主义的宣传噱头。

23. **ostentatious** /ˌɑːstenˈteɪʃəs/ — designed to attract admiration by showing wealth or importance；通过炫耀财富或地位来吸引赞赏的；炫耀的
    - **Example:** The lobby was filled with ostentatious decorations.
    - **中文：** 大厅里摆满了炫耀性的装饰。

24. **pertinent** /ˈpɜːrtɪnənt/ — directly relevant to the subject being discussed；与所讨论主题直接相关的；相关的
    - **Example:** Please include only the pertinent facts in the executive summary.
    - **中文：** 请在执行摘要中只列出相关事实。

25. **preclude** /prɪˈkluːd/ — prevent something from happening or make it impossible；阻止某事发生或使其不可能；排除；阻止
    - **Example:** A lack of funding may preclude further research.
    - **中文：** 资金不足可能会阻碍进一步研究。
