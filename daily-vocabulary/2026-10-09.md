# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-09

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **abjuration** /ˌæbdʒəˈreɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** a formal or emphatic rejection of a belief, claim, or former position  
   **中文释义：** 正式放弃；断然否认  
   **Example:** The politician’s public abjuration of the policy surprised even her closest allies.  
   **中文：** 这位政治人物公开放弃该政策，连她最亲密的盟友都感到意外。

2. **acclivity** /əˈklɪvəti/  
   **English definition:** an upward slope, especially one that is steep  
   **中文释义：** 上坡；向上的斜坡  
   **Example:** The cyclists slowed as they approached the steep acclivity outside the village.  
   **中文：** 骑行者接近村外的陡坡时放慢了速度。

3. **affectation** /ˌæfekˈteɪʃən/  
   **English definition:** behavior or speech that is deliberately adopted to create a particular impression  
   **中文释义：** 矫揉造作；故作姿态  
   **Example:** Her precise diction felt natural rather than an affectation.  
   **中文：** 她咬字清晰，听起来自然而非故作姿态。

4. **allusion** /əˈluːʒən/  
   **English definition:** an indirect reference to a person, event, work, or idea  
   **中文释义：** 暗指；典故；间接提及  
   **Example:** The speech contains an allusion to a famous passage from Shakespeare.  
   **中文：** 这篇演讲暗指了莎士比亚的一段著名文字。

5. **amity** /ˈæməti/  
   **English definition:** a friendly relationship characterized by goodwill and peace  
   **中文释义：** 友好关系；和睦  
   **Example:** The agreement helped restore amity between the neighboring communities.  
   **中文：** 这项协议帮助邻近社区恢复了友好关系。

6. **antedate** /ˌæntiˈdeɪt/  
   **English definition:** to exist or happen before a particular date, event, or development  
   **中文释义：** 早于；先于……存在或发生  
   **Example:** The tradition antedates the founding of the city by several centuries.  
   **中文：** 这项传统比该城市的建立早了几个世纪。

7. **aporia** /əˈpɔːriə/  
   **English definition:** a state of puzzlement or doubt caused by an unresolved question or contradiction  
   **中文释义：** 困惑；因矛盾或难题而陷入的疑惑  
   **Example:** The final chapter leaves readers in aporia rather than offering an easy answer.  
   **中文：** 最后一章没有给出简单答案，而是让读者陷入思考与困惑。

8. **assay** /əˈseɪ/  
   **English definition:** to examine or test a substance to determine its ingredients or quality  
   **中文释义：** 化验；分析；测定成分或质量  
   **Example:** Technicians assayed the water samples for traces of heavy metals.  
   **中文：** 技术人员化验了水样，以检测其中的重金属痕迹。

9. **asseverate** /əˈsevəreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to state or declare something firmly and confidently  
   **中文释义：** 坚称；郑重断言  
   **Example:** The company continued to asseverate that customer data had not been compromised.  
   **中文：** 公司继续坚称客户数据没有遭到泄露。

10. **autonomy** /ɔːˈtɑːnəmi/  
    **English definition:** the ability or right to make decisions independently  
    **中文释义：** 自治；自主权  
    **Example:** The new policy gives regional offices greater autonomy over hiring.  
    **中文：** 新政策赋予地区办公室更大的招聘自主权。

11. **beholden** /bɪˈhoʊldən/  
    **English definition:** owing gratitude or an obligation to someone for help or support  
    **中文释义：** 对……负有人情；受……恩惠而有义务  
    **Example:** The board wants its members to remain independent and not beholden to major donors.  
    **中文：** 董事会希望成员保持独立，不受大额捐赠者的人情牵制。

12. **beneficent** /bəˈnefɪsənt/  
    **English definition:** doing or producing good; actively kind and helpful  
    **中文释义：** 行善的；有益的；慈善的  
    **Example:** The beneficent fund provides emergency grants to families after a disaster.  
    **中文：** 这个慈善基金会在灾后向家庭提供紧急补助。

13. **burgeon** /ˈbɜːrdʒən/  
    **English definition:** to grow or develop rapidly  
    **中文释义：** 迅速发展；激增  
    **Example:** Interest in community gardens began to burgeon during the pandemic.  
    **中文：** 疫情期间，人们对社区花园的兴趣开始迅速增长。

14. **cadence** /ˈkeɪdəns/  
    **English definition:** a regular rhythm or pattern in movement, sound, or speech  
    **中文释义：** 节奏；韵律；抑扬顿挫  
    **Example:** The presenter varied her cadence to keep the audience engaged.  
    **中文：** 主讲人变换语速和语调，以保持听众的注意力。

15. **chide** /tʃaɪd/  
    **English definition:** to criticize or scold someone mildly for doing something wrong  
    **中文释义：** 温和责备；嗔怪  
    **Example:** The coach chided the players for arriving late but praised their effort afterward.  
    **中文：** 教练责备队员迟到，但之后也称赞了他们的努力。

16. **contingent** /kənˈtɪndʒənt/  
    **English definition:** dependent on a particular condition or event  
    **中文释义：** 取决于……的；有条件的  
    **Example:** The funding is contingent on the project meeting its safety targets.  
    **中文：** 这笔资金能否拨付，取决于项目是否达到安全目标。

17. **depose** /dɪˈpoʊz/  
    **English definition:** to remove someone from a position of power; to give sworn evidence  
    **中文释义：** 废黜；罢免；宣誓作证  
    **Example:** Investigators asked the former director to depose about the contract negotiations.  
    **中文：** 调查人员要求前主管就合同谈判宣誓作证。

18. **dither** /ˈdɪðər/  
    **English definition:** to hesitate or act indecisively, especially when a decision is needed  
    **中文释义：** 犹豫不决；踌躇  
    **Example:** Rather than dither over minor details, the team agreed on a clear deadline.  
    **中文：** 团队没有在小细节上犹豫不决，而是确定了明确期限。

19. **elide** /ɪˈlaɪd/  
    **English definition:** to omit or leave out a sound, word, or section  
    **中文释义：** 省略；删去  
    **Example:** The summary elides background details that are not needed to understand the result.  
    **中文：** 这份摘要省略了理解结果所不需要的背景细节。

20. **foible** /ˈfɔɪbəl/  
    **English definition:** a small weakness or unusual habit that is generally harmless  
    **中文释义：** 小缺点；无伤大雅的怪癖  
    **Example:** His habit of labeling every drawer is a harmless foible.  
    **中文：** 他给每个抽屉都贴标签的习惯只是个无伤大雅的小怪癖。

21. **fortitude** /ˈfɔːrtɪtuːd/  
    **English definition:** courage and strength of mind when facing pain, difficulty, or adversity  
    **中文释义：** 坚韧；刚毅；承受逆境的勇气  
    **Example:** She showed remarkable fortitude while rebuilding her business after the fire.  
    **中文：** 火灾后重建生意时，她展现出非凡的坚韧。

22. **hermetic** /hərˈmetɪk/  
    **English definition:** sealed so that nothing can enter or escape; isolated from outside influence  
    **中文释义：** 密封的；与外界隔绝的  
    **Example:** The archive is kept in a hermetic chamber to protect fragile documents.  
    **中文：** 档案被保存在密封室中，以保护脆弱的文件。

23. **interlocutor** /ˌɪntərˈlɑːkjətər/  
    **English definition:** a person taking part in a conversation or dialogue  
    **中文释义：** 对话者；交谈对象  
    **Example:** A skilled interlocutor asks follow-up questions that help clarify the issue.  
    **中文：** 善于交谈的人会提出后续问题，帮助澄清问题。

24. **largesse** /lɑːrˈʒes/  
    **English definition:** generous giving of money, gifts, or assistance  
    **中文释义：** 慷慨赠与；施予  
    **Example:** The museum expanded its collection through the largesse of several local families.  
    **中文：** 博物馆通过几户当地人家的慷慨捐赠扩充了馆藏。

25. **penitent** /ˈpenɪtənt/  
    **English definition:** feeling or expressing sincere regret for having done something wrong  
    **中文释义：** 悔过的；表示悔意的  
    **Example:** The penitent employee acknowledged the mistake and offered to correct the record.  
    **中文：** 心怀悔意的员工承认了错误，并主动提出更正记录。
