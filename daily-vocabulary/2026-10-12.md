# Daily Vocabulary — 2026-10-12

以下 25 个词汇偏生僻但具有实用性，整体定位在英语内容覆盖率约 75%—90% 的范围。每个词包含音标、英文释义、中文释义、英文例句和中文翻译。

1. **authorize** /ˈɔːθəraɪz/  
   **English definition:** to give official permission for an action or give someone the power to act  
   **中文释义：** 授权；批准  
   **Example:** Only the director can authorize an exception to the policy.  
   **中文：** 只有主管有权批准对该政策的例外处理。

2. **cite** /saɪt/  
   **English definition:** to mention something as evidence or support, or to name a source  
   **中文释义：** 引用；举出（证据或来源）  
   **Example:** The report cites three independent studies to support its conclusion.  
   **中文：** 这份报告引用了三项独立研究来支持其结论。

3. **confer** /kənˈfɜːr/  
   **English definition:** to discuss something with others in order to make a decision  
   **中文释义：** 商议；协商  
   **Example:** I’ll confer with the project team before confirming the new schedule.  
   **中文：** 在确认新的日程前，我会先与项目团队商议。

4. **designate** /ˈdezɪɡneɪt/  
   **English definition:** to officially choose or identify someone or something for a particular purpose  
   **中文释义：** 指定；任命；标明  
   **Example:** The north entrance is designated for deliveries.  
   **中文：** 北侧入口指定为货物交付通道。

5. **detract** /dɪˈtrækt/  
   **English definition:** to make something seem less valuable, impressive, or important  
   **中文释义：** 减损；降低……的价值或重要性  
   **Example:** Minor formatting issues shouldn’t detract from the report’s findings.  
   **中文：** 细小的格式问题不应影响这份报告结论的重要性。

6. **distill** /dɪˈstɪl/  
   **English definition:** to extract the most important parts or meaning from a larger amount of information  
   **中文释义：** 提炼；浓缩（要点或精华）  
   **Example:** The summary distills a 40-page report into five key findings.  
   **中文：** 这份摘要将一份40页的报告提炼为五项关键发现。

7. **drastic** /ˈdræstɪk/  
   **English definition:** extreme and likely to have a major effect  
   **中文释义：** 大幅度的；激烈的；极端的  
   **Example:** The drop in demand forced the company to make drastic cuts to production.  
   **中文：** 需求下滑迫使公司大幅削减产量。

8. **elevate** /ˈeləveɪt/  
   **English definition:** to raise something to a higher level, position, or standard  
   **中文释义：** 提高；提升；使上升  
   **Example:** The redesign elevated the customer experience without adding extra steps.  
   **中文：** 这次重新设计提升了客户体验，同时没有增加额外步骤。

9. **enforce** /ɪnˈfɔːrs/  
   **English definition:** to make sure that a law, rule, or agreement is obeyed  
   **中文释义：** 执行；强制实施；确保遵守  
   **Example:** The platform enforces a limit on the size of uploaded files.  
   **中文：** 该平台对上传文件的大小实施限制。

10. **formulate** /ˈfɔːrmjəleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to develop or express a plan, idea, or method carefully  
   **中文释义：** 制定；构想；系统地表达  
   **Example:** We formulated a backup plan before launching the service.  
   **中文：** 在推出这项服务前，我们制定了备用方案。

11. **grapple** /ˈɡræpəl/  
   **English definition:** to struggle with a difficult problem or try hard to understand it  
   **中文释义：** 努力应对；设法解决；认真思索  
   **Example:** The team is grappling with how to protect user data without slowing the service.  
   **中文：** 团队正在设法解决如何保护用户数据、同时又不拖慢服务的问题。

12. **grievance** /ˈɡriːvəns/  
   **English definition:** a formal complaint about unfair treatment or an unsatisfactory situation  
   **中文释义：** 不满；申诉；正式投诉  
   **Example:** Employees can submit a grievance through the internal review process.  
   **中文：** 员工可以通过内部审查流程提出申诉。

13. **harness** /ˈhɑːrnɪs/  
   **English definition:** to control and use a resource, ability, or source of energy effectively  
   **中文释义：** 利用；控制并发挥（资源、能力或能量）  
   **Example:** The project harnesses local knowledge to improve flood planning.  
   **中文：** 这个项目利用当地经验来完善防洪规划。

14. **heighten** /ˈhaɪtən/  
   **English definition:** to increase the intensity, level, or awareness of something  
   **中文释义：** 加剧；提高；增强  
   **Example:** The delay heightened concerns about possible supply shortages.  
   **中文：** 延误加剧了人们对可能出现供应短缺的担忧。

15. **inadequate** /ɪnˈædɪkwət/  
   **English definition:** not good enough or not large enough for a particular need  
   **中文释义：** 不足的；不充分的；不合格的  
   **Example:** The existing backup system is inadequate for the new workload.  
   **中文：** 现有的备份系统不足以应对新的工作负载。

16. **incorporate** /ɪnˈkɔːrpəreɪt/  
   **English definition:** to include something as part of a larger whole  
   **中文释义：** 纳入；包含；使并入整体  
   **Example:** We incorporated the reviewers’ suggestions into the revised draft.  
   **中文：** 我们把审阅者的建议纳入了修订稿。

17. **intact** /ɪnˈtækt/  
   **English definition:** complete and not damaged, weakened, or altered  
   **中文释义：** 完好无损的；完整的；未受影响的  
   **Example:** The package arrived intact despite the rough weather.  
   **中文：** 尽管天气恶劣，包裹送达时仍完好无损。

18. **perceive** /pərˈsiːv/  
   **English definition:** to notice something or understand it in a particular way  
   **中文释义：** 察觉；感知；理解为  
   **Example:** Customers may perceive the policy as unfair unless we explain its purpose.  
   **中文：** 如果不解释政策的目的，客户可能会认为它不公平。

19. **presume** /prɪˈzuːm/  
   **English definition:** to accept something as true without definite proof, often because it seems likely  
   **中文释义：** 推定；假定；擅自认为  
   **Example:** We shouldn’t presume that every user has a fast internet connection.  
   **中文：** 我们不应想当然地认为每位用户都有高速网络连接。

20. **prohibit** /prəˈhɪbɪt/  
   **English definition:** to officially forbid an action or activity  
   **中文释义：** 禁止；不准  
   **Example:** The building prohibits bicycles in the main lobby.  
   **中文：** 这栋楼禁止自行车进入主大厅。

21. **qualify** /ˈkwɑːləfaɪ/  
   **English definition:** to meet the conditions needed for a particular role, benefit, or opportunity  
   **中文释义：** 符合资格；具备条件  
   **Example:** Applicants must complete the course to qualify for the grant.  
   **中文：** 申请者必须完成这门课程才有资格获得补助。

22. **recruit** /rɪˈkruːt/  
   **English definition:** to find and persuade people to join an organization, team, or activity  
   **中文释义：** 招募；招聘；吸收新成员  
   **Example:** She is recruiting volunteers for the weekend survey.  
   **中文：** 她正在为周末的调查招募志愿者。

23. **regulate** /ˈreɡjəleɪt/  
   **English definition:** to control an activity or process by using rules or standards  
   **中文释义：** 管制；规范；调节  
   **Example:** New standards regulate how manufacturers report emissions.  
   **中文：** 新标准规范了制造商报告排放量的方式。

24. **render** /ˈrendər/  
   **English definition:** to cause someone or something to be in a particular condition  
   **中文释义：** 使成为；导致处于某种状态  
   **Example:** The outage rendered the payment system temporarily unusable.  
   **中文：** 这次停电导致支付系统暂时无法使用。

25. **revenue** /ˈrevənuː/  
   **English definition:** income generated by a business or government, especially from sales or services  
   **中文释义：** 收入；收益；（企业或政府的）营收  
   **Example:** The new subscription plan increased monthly revenue.  
   **中文：** 新的订阅方案提高了月度营收。
